International Drive, for Date Night

Two date nights share this district. One glitters along the corridor, all polished rooms and famous names. The other takes the short ride west to Sand Lake, where Restaurant Row keeps some of Orlando’s most serious dining rooms. Both are on this page.

Every pick below carries the guide’s date tag in the International Drive district, which reaches from ICON Park to the Dellagio fountain cluster on Sand Lake. Each one opens its full card in the finder.

The Short Version

Pick the register, then the room

The corridor end of the district does date night as a night out: a Nikkei counter that opened in 2025 as the corridor’s first serious entry in its style, a Mediterranean supper club inside ICON Park, a lounge at Pointe Orlando carrying two very famous names. The lighting is designed, the music is part of the plate, and nobody is pretending dinner is the whole evening.

The Sand Lake end does date night as dinner itself. Norman’s, Eddie V’s, and Ocean Prime anchor Restaurant Row, Vines by H keeps the wine and jazz room tradition alive, and the Dellagio cluster strings its rooms around a fountain. Same district in the guide, two different evenings.

The curveball: The Escape Game sits on this list on purpose. A locked room is the date that tells you the most about a person, and it carries an evening before dinner better than another drink does.

Before You Go

Practical notes

Getting there and back

  • The I-RIDE Trolley covers the corridor itself, which serves the ICON Park and Pointe Orlando end of the night well.
  • Restaurant Row is a rideshare ride, not a trolley hop. The return is calmer when it is arranged before the room empties at the end of the night.
  • Several rooms on this list run at the full steakhouse register. This is the corridor where a reservation does the work that walking in cannot, above all on convention and holiday weeks.
