Blue Coast Burrito

Mexican · $

2311 N Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando, FL 32804

Blue Coast Burrito on North Orange Blossom Trail keeps fast casual honest with fresh, build your own burritos and bowls. Guests point to the pineapple salsa, Baja style fish, and queso as the things to get, with Baja fruit tea on the side. The pet friendly patio makes it an easy pick for a laid back family lunch.

Cups and Pups

Coffee & cafes · $

2413 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804

Cups and Pups on Edgewater Drive in College Park is the cute, cheerful coffee stop guests describe as a neighborhood ritual. The lavender cold brew arrives balanced without extra sweetener, joined by the Honey Nut Cheerio Latte, the Brown Sugar Pop Tart Latte, and nitro cold brew. Pastries and cinnamon rolls fill the case, pup cups keep dogs in on the fun, and the dog friendly patio makes it easy to linger with your pup beside you.

Everoak Farm

Markets & shops · $

Everoak Farm, 2335 Carrington Dr, Orlando, FL 32807

Everoak Farm on Carrington Drive is a family run, regenerative farm growing Certified Naturally Grown produce with no sprays in living soil. Weekly CSA boxes, pasture raised chicken, farm eggs, and small batch honey connect the farm stand to restaurant menus and home kitchens across Orlando.

Great Southern Box Co. Food Hall & Bar

Markets & shops · $$

The Great Southern Box Company, 2105 N Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando, FL 32804

The Great Southern Box food hall on North Orange Blossom Trail turns a historic citrus crate warehouse in the Packing District into a vibrant, group friendly hall of counters. Guests move between Antica wood fired pizza, birria tacos, Braza Brazilian steak plates, Kayos jerk chicken and oxtail, beef shawarma, poke bowls, and A Lo Cubano pressed sandwiches. The room runs lively and modern, with a beautiful atmosphere, a dog friendly patio, and plenty of variety for groups who cannot agree on one menu.

Directions → · @gsbc.foodhall

LaSpada's Original Cheesesteaks and Hoagies

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

1010 Lee Rd, Orlando, FL 32810

LaSpada's Original Cheesesteaks and Hoagies on Lee Road brings Delco standards to College Park with an old school, no frills, family run counter. The Original stacks steak with sweet onions and white American cheese with the cheese distributed through every bite, while the mushroom cheesesteak and the Famous Italian hoagie pile on what guests call an insane amount of meat. Soft bread, fresh veggies, and hot, crispy fries complete a sandwich guests describe as a beautiful meatrosity.

Ollie's Public House

Bars & lounges · $$

Ollie's Public House, 3400 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804-3602, United States

Ollie's Public House on Edgewater Drive has the easy rhythm of a neighborhood pub, with a lively, dog friendly patio, rotating craft taps, and regulars who treat work nights as a social hour. The Legend sandwich and the Wall Street Reuben anchor a deep bar food menu that also hides Finnhenry's Buffalo Chicken Wrap and a chocolate espresso martini. Service can slow when the room fills, but the unpretentious buzz keeps people coming back.

Shakers American Cafe

American · $$

Shakers American Cafe, 1308 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804-6351, United States

Shakers is a cozy College Park cottage where the walls hold a whimsical forest of donated salt and pepper shakers, and the breakfast crowd treats it like a neighborhood clubhouse. The cinnamon roll pancakes are the dish regulars say is hard to pass up, with the potato pancake and smoked salmon eggs benedict running close behind. A tight dining room means waits at peak hours, so off peak visits keep things easy.

The Current Seafood Counter

Seafood · $$

The Current Seafood Counter, 2425 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804-5303, United States

The Current Seafood Counter brings a New England fish shack spirit to College Park with counter service ease and a clean, casual room. The lobster rolls come New England and Connecticut style, the blackened mahi sandwich gets weekly repeat devotion, and Old Bay fries with malt vinegar round out the cold water comfort. Guests call it some of the best seafood in Orlando, and the short, direct menu makes it easy to see why.

Turci Pasta

Italian · $$

Turci Pasta, 2120 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804

Turci Pasta on Edgewater Drive in College Park is a cozy, romantic hole in the wall where guests watch fresh pasta being made in house. The pear ravioli stands out, alongside black truffle ravioli, purple sweet potato gnocchi, and lasagna, with tableside cannoli to finish. The room feels restful and intimate, and the limoncello dessert lands light, airy, and lemony, the kind of ending guests describe as perfection.

Walter's Tavern

Bars & lounges · $$

2105 N Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando, FL 32804

Walter's Tavern inside the Great Southern Box Company food hall in the Packing District is a spacious, lively game day room built for lingering. Guests talk about good drinks and zero pressure, with craft cocktails, mocktails, Vodkade drinks, and a house Orange Lager on hand. You can order food hall favorites like the birria crunchwrap straight to your table and settle in with your crew.

Zeytin Turkish Cuisine

Mediterranean · $$

Zeytin Turkish Cuisine, 4439 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804-1229

Zeytin Turkish Cuisine on Edgewater Drive in College Park is a cozy, family run, lantern lit room that guests call a hidden gem. The meal starts with fluffy lavash and a tray of dips, then moves to Iskender kebab, lamb doner pide, red lentil soup, and sauteed eggplant, with knafeh and baklava to finish. The small, unpretentious dining room fills quickly, and the hospitality makes first timers feel like regulars.
