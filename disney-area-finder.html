Disney Area

Park icons like EPCOT and Chef Mickey's share the list with Lake Buena Vista resort tables and the Flamingo Crossings shops at the western entrance. Fogo de Chão, Vineland Pointe Local Pick

Fine dining & steak · $$$

11543 Regency Village Dr, Orlando, FL 32821

Fogo de Chão doesn't do dinner, it stages it. The gaucho chefs arrive at your table with fire-roasted cuts, Picanha with its ember-kissed crust, Fraldinha dripping juice, Filet Mignon that yields without a fight, carving right there in front of you, one slice at a time. The Market Table is a spread of seasonal salads, exotic fruits, and cheese with honey that regulars plan around. Founded in Southern Brazil and built on the centuries-old art of churrasco, this is the kind of meal that makes an ordinary evening feel like an occasion. The Vineland Pointe patio is your calmest seat.

Evermore Orlando Resort Local Pick

Hotels · $$$

1590 Evermore Way, Orlando, FL 32836

There is a beach in Orlando, and it has eight acres of crystal-clear water surrounded by twenty acres of white sand. Evermore Bay is the centerpiece of this reimagined Grand Cypress resort, where you can rent houses and flats big enough for the whole family tree, or check into the AAA Five Diamond Conrad Orlando. Golfers get two Jack Nicklaus courses, sunset cruises glide out on classic Chris-Craft boats, and thirteen dining venues keep everyone fed. Landlocked Florida, meeting the Caribbean, right outside Disney's gates.

Amare

Mediterranean · $$$

Amare, 1255 Epcot Resorts Blvd, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The Disney bubble dissolves at Amare into something calmer, older and built around the Mediterranean. Chef Scott West treats the region as a journey rather than a theme, moving from grilled coastal fish to slow, earthy braises. Each plate has a pour to match from a wine list built entirely around the region, and curated flights shortcut a tasting of Greece, Italy and Spain in one sitting. Ask about them. The rare resort restaurant here earns the trip on its own merits.

Amazonica

Sweets · $$

8200 Vineland Ave, Orlando, FL 32821

A smart mid shopping stop sits in front of Columbia, next to Lululemon, at Vineland Premium Outlets. Amazonica's kiosk serves the same Colombian menu as the Florida Mall location: obleas, acai bowls, empanadas, cafe drinks, and fruit smoothies, all made fresh. A cholado or acai bowl gives you fruit, sweetness, and enough lift to keep browsing without dragging by the later stores. The format stays quick, bright, and built for a pause between shops. Choose it when outlet shopping needs a fresh break rather than another heavy food court plate.

Big Thunder Mountain Railroad

Attractions · $$$$

Magic Kingdom Park, 1180 Seven Seas Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The wildest ride in the wilderness is running again. Big Thunder Mountain reopened in May 2026 after a full refurbishment, and the project replaced the track, added a new Rainbow Caverns scene, and refreshed the animatronics throughout the mining town. It remains the perfect first big coaster for kids graduating from the Barnstormer, all speed and scenery without a punishing drop. The refurbished version rewards a re ride even if you have done it a hundred times, so budget time for a second lap.

Capa

Fine dining & steak · $$$$

10100 Dream Tree Blvd, Orlando, FL 32836

Seventeen floors up inside the Four Seasons, Capa makes a case that the great steakhouse tradition belongs to Spain too. Chefs Fabrizio Schenardi and Chris Edwards cook over a wood-burning grill in an open kitchen, sending out Wagyu from Florida's own Clear Creek Farm alongside tapas that show off: fried cauliflower with capers and a fried egg, pork cheeks with Marcona almonds. Start with cocktails on the rooftop terrace, where the view stretches over golf greens and wetlands to the nightly Disney fireworks. Dark wood, black and crimson, candlelight, the whole romantic apparatus, earned.

Chef Mickey's

American · $$$

Chef Mickey's, 4600 N World Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830-8413

Some Disney memories are manufactured, but the joy in this room is real. Chef Mickey's sits on the Contemporary Resort's grand concourse, monorail gliding right through the building, and every meal is a celebration: Mickey, Minnie, Goofy, Pluto and Donald working the tables while the whole room twirls napkins overhead to music. The buffet does the heavy lifting, breakfast spreads and dinner carving stations built for refills and second plates. For a kid meeting Mickey Mouse up close, there is no substitute. Book early, the world knows about this one.

Disney Starlight: Dream the Night Away

Attractions · $$$$

Magic Kingdom Park, 1180 Seven Seas Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Magic Kingdom has a new way to end the night. Disney Starlight, which debuted in July 2025, is described as the park's first new daily nighttime parade since 2016, and it was built for the drone and projection era. The floats blend classic characters with newer favorites, the soundtrack is engineered to stick in your head for days, and the route down Main Street USA delivers the moment the castle lights up behind the final float. Stake out a spot early, because everyone else has the same idea.

Disney Villains: Unfairly Ever After

Attractions · $$$$

Disney's Hollywood Studios, 351 S Studio Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The villains finally get their own stage, and the show commits to the bit. Unfairly Ever After, which debuted in May 2025 at Hollywood Studios, leans into the villains' grievances with Broadway scale staging, and the audience is encouraged to pick sides. It is a strong new stage show at the Studios, and the indoor theater makes it a strategic midday break from the Florida sun. Bring the family, pick a villain to root for, and enjoy a production that knows exactly whose side the crowd secretly takes.

EPCOT

Parks & outdoors · $$$$

200 Epcot Center Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Walt's grandest what-if still stands at Disney. EPCOT opened in 1982 as the Experimental Prototype Community of Tomorrow, his World's Fair dream of a city of the future, and four neighborhoods carry that ambition now: celebration, discovery, nature and the World Showcase, where eleven countries ring a lagoon. Come during a festival, Flower and Garden in spring or Food and Wine in fall, when the park becomes one long global tasting menu, and stay for the nighttime spectacular over the water. It is Disney's most curiosity-driven park.

Flatlands Bar & Grill

American · $$

124 Ruby Red Place, Winter Garden, FL 34787

Brand new at Flamingo Crossings, and already worth leaving the Disney bubble for. From restaurateur Nick Valenti with executive chef Dominique Filoni, formerly of Disney's Cítricos and Topolino's Terrace, Flatlands is built around Central Florida's 1920s citrus boom: the building looks like a vintage packing facility, and the cooking follows the Italian-immigrant principles behind it, great ingredients, simple preparations, zero compromise. The Maine lobster rolls are the breakout star, the cookie skillet finishes strong, and the Orange Blossom Collins drinks like Florida itself. Disney-adjacent dining, all grown up.

Four Flamingos

Fine dining & steak · $$$

Four Flamingos, 1 Grand Cypress Blvd, Orlando, FL 32836

Chef Richard Blais brought his first Florida kitchen to the Hyatt Regency Grand Cypress, playful from the start. Four Flamingos pairs an open kitchen, a raw bar, and a live lobster tank with a dining room the record calls a tropical oasis. The menu chases Florida's bounty: Tuna Tostada crowned with a passion fruit yolk, bone-in swordfish prime rib, and coconut and mango running through dishes. GEO-82

Bars & lounges · $$$

EPCOT, 200 Epcot Center Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Cocktails inside Spaceship Earth, with no kids anywhere in the room. GEO-82 opened in June 2025 as EPCOT's first adults only lounge, serving drinks inside the park's icon in a mid century space age setting. The program leans classic, with a few geodesic themed signatures, and the 21 and over policy keeps the room calm even on busy festival days. Reservations open ahead of time and go fast. Treat it as the EPCOT date night move when you want atmosphere as much as a drink.

Kung Fu Kitchen

Chinese · $$

Kungfu Kitchen, 8466 Palm Pkwy, Orlando, FL 32836-6431

Soup dumpling obsession starts with noodles and bao made the hard way. Kung Fu Kitchen, a Flushing, New York export, built its Orlando following on hand pulled ramen and knife cut noodles that land springy and fresh in soup or stir fried. Xiao long bao arrive delicate and soup filled with pork, chicken, or crab, and pan fried Shanghai pork buns quiet the table. Chocolate xiao long bao tastes like cake batter in dumpling form. Choose the red walled, booth lined room when a noodle run deserves planning across town.

Markets & shops · $$

15657 S Apopka Vineland Rd, Orlando, FL 32821

The low-stress outlet trip. Lake Buena Vista Factory Stores keeps its center compact, roughly fifty stores in the Nike, Gap, and Tommy Hilfiger tier, with easy surface parking and a Disney-adjacent address on SR 535. Le Macaron Celebration

Bakeries · $$

671 Front St #110, Celebration, FL 34747

A small piece of Paris sits on the lakeside promenade in Celebration. Le Macaron lines its case with French macarons in more than twenty flavors, including salted caramel, pistachio, rose, Colombian coffee, and birthday cake, each crisp shelled and chewy at the center. Beyond macarons, you find buttery croissants, eclairs, beignets, house made gelato, and a proper espresso menu. Magic Kingdom Park

Parks & outdoors · $$$$

Disney's Magic Kingdom, 1180 Seven Seas Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

You know this park before you ever set foot in it, and it still gets you. Cinderella Castle stands at the hub of six lands, with Space Mountain's rattling darkness, the Haunted Mansion's stretching rooms and pirates singing their song: rides that double as shared memories the country grew up with. Locals come back for the fireworks, the Festival of Fantasy parade and watching a child's first visit through their own eyes. Plan for a full day, walk Main Street slowly, and give in to the place completely.

Disney's Hollywood Studios, 351 S Studio Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

A droid DJ, a tingling cocktail, and a room built for Star Wars fans. Oga's Cantina sits inside Galaxy's Edge at Hollywood Studios, where R-3X spins behind the bar and drinks arrive in otherworldly presentations. Portillo's

Burgers & sandwiches · $

7715 Palm Pkwy, Orlando, FL 32836

Homesick Midwesterners get a direct answer in Orlando, red chrome and all. Portillo's has served Chicago street food since 1963, and the Orlando rooms go full retro diner as slow roasted Italian beef perfumes the entrance. The Italian beef sandwich drips with jus and takes sweet or hot peppers the way Chicagoans argue about. A Chicago style hot dog gets dragged through the garden correctly, and the chocolate cake shake sounds wrong until it tastes right. Order at the counter and watch families, students, and transplants reach for the same fries.

Ravello

Italian · $$$

10100 Dream Tree Blvd, Orlando, FL 32836

Some Italian dinners earn a plan made weeks ahead, birthday included. Ravello sits inside the Four Seasons Resort Orlando, where executive chef Fabrizio Schenardi, a Torino native, runs a kitchen that makes its pastas, breads, sauces, and desserts entirely in house. The veal ravioli is the signature, soft and rich the way holiday meals are remembered, and the wood-fired pies come layered with prosciutto di Parma and fresh burrata. It even holds a rare Italian taste certification few American restaurants ever earn. Bring someone you want to impress. The room will help.

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring the Muppets

Attractions · $$$$

Disney's Hollywood Studios, 351 S Studio Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

A familiar launch now comes with a Muppets storyline wrapped around it. In May 2026, the Rock 'n' Roller Coaster received its biggest change since opening, with the Electric Mayhem band taking over the story and the super stretch limo becoming a dash across Hollywood. The coaster itself stayed the same, launching from 0 to 57 in under three seconds, with inversions in the dark. Simply Capri

Italian · $$$

114 Ruby Red Place, Winter Garden, FL 34787

Nick Valenti is the man behind Via Napoli at EPCOT and Space 220, so when he built a trattoria at Flamingo Crossings, Orlando took notice. Simply Capri whisks you to the Isle of Capri on sheer atmosphere: sunny Amalfi yellows, Italian servers, and a pizza oven shipped all the way from Naples turning out blistered Neapolitan pies. The house-made pastas sing with Southern Italian soul, try the Scialatielli dei Faraglioni with shrimp, clams, mussels, and calamari, and the Delizia al Limone is the limoncello-spiked finish you'll dream about. The concept is simple, the cooking is not.

EPCOT, 200 Epcot Center Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

A hang glider seat, wind in your face, and the United States rolling out below you. Soarin' Across America debuted in May 2026, swapping the ride's usual world tour for a flight tied to the country's 250th anniversary. Stadium Club

Bars & lounges · $$

8101 World Center Dr, Orlando, FL 32821

Game day gets a two level temple at Caribe Royale, built for the moment the whole room erupts. Walls of screens carry every matchup at once, and VIP booths come with sports simulators of their own. Sushi Izuki

Japanese & sushi · $$$

8685 Fenton St #150, Orlando, FL 32836

Eight seats, one chef, eighteen courses: Sushi Izuki is Chef Leo's declaration on omakase. The menu is never printed; it's spoken, decided that morning from whatever arrived fresh, and it never repeats. Whole fish dry-aged in house, cut to order, served with deliberate, restless creativity drawn from Japan and the West. Add a private tatami room for a second, even more intimate way in. Bars & lounges · $$$$

Magic Kingdom Park, 1180 Seven Seas Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Pirates finally got a tavern of their own inside Magic Kingdom. The Beak and Barrel opened in August 2025 in Adventureland as the standalone Pirates of the Caribbean tavern fans had waited decades to see. The Little Mermaid: A Musical Adventure

Attractions · $$$$

Disney's Hollywood Studios, 351 S Studio Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The Little Mermaid: A Musical Adventure opened in May 2025 at Hollywood Studios, replacing the Voyage of the Little Mermaid show that ran for decades. Victoria & Albert's

American · $$$$

Victoria & Albert's, 4401 Floridian Way, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Inside Disney's Grand Floridian, behind a formal dress code and a hushed dining room, sits a restaurant the record calls the most decorated in the Southeast. Victoria and Albert's has held AAA's Five Diamond award since 2000 and earned a Michelin star, the first for any Disney owned restaurant. Course after course of seasonal American cooking arrives with ceremony: personalized menus, tableside flourishes, and a coffee service people talk about for years. Book months ahead, dress the part, and give the evening the reverence it deserves.

Vivoli Il Gelato

Sweets · $$

Vivoli Il Gelato, 1638 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830, United States

Florence pauses in the middle of Disney Springs, dense and unhurried. Vivoli Il Gelato is the Orlando outpost of a famous Italian gelateria, serving scoops made the old way with cannoli and espresso on the menu for good measure. Zootopia: Better Zoogether!

Attractions · $$$$

Disney's Animal Kingdom, 2901 Osceola Pkwy, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The Tree of Life hides a theater, and its newest show trades bugs for Zootopia. Zootopia: Better Zoogether opened in November 2025, replacing the long running It's Tough to Be a Bug with a 4D show.
