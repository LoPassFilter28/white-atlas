Oviedo

Black Hammock Restaurant Local Pick

Restaurants · $$

2316 Black Hammock Fish Camp Rd, Oviedo, FL 32765

An Old Florida fish camp on Lake Jesup, where the menu leans all the way into the setting: Florida style gator bites, fried gator tail, catfish nuggets and a gator burger. Airboats run off the dock, a lakeside bar pours, and guests call it a real Florida experience. Regulars say the kitchen can be uneven these days, so come for the lake, the gators and the theater of the place first.

Alma Argentina

Latin & Caribbean · $$

3607 Aloma Ave Unit 107, Oviedo, FL 32765

A family-run Argentine steakhouse on Aloma Avenue where the empanadas are fried to order and regulars call it their favorite Argentine restaurant. The parrillada mixed grill, sizzling provoleta and milanesa napolitana come out fresh and homemade, with flan or chocotorta to finish. Guests note the prices run a touch high, though most say the quality earns it.

Beirut Grill and Deli

Mediterranean · $$

Beirut Grill and Deli, 3100 Alafaya Trl, Oviedo, FL 32765-9466, United States

A family-owned halal Lebanese kitchen on Alafaya Trail where everything tastes homemade and the room feels like family. Guests rave over the beef shawarma and the creamy hummus, which one regular calls the best in Central Florida. The chicken shawarma sandwich, falafel and fried kibbeh arrive generous and fresh, and regulars tell newcomers to save room for kenefeh.

Hinode Sushi

Japanese & sushi · $$

Hinode Sushi, 1016 Lockwood Blvd, Oviedo, FL 32765-6027, United States

Hinode is the Oviedo sushi room locals call their own, a warm family-run counter on Lockwood Blvd where Chef George Tan plates sushi with real artistry. The Trio Tower stacks tuna, salmon, yellowtail, mango and avocado; the Black Widow rolls out under a spiderweb of sauce; even the avocado fries arrive looking sculpted. Guests call it the most delicious sushi they have ever had, and regulars who come for the spicy salmon lunch special keep calling it their favorite in the area. Everything is made to order, so a busy room runs at its own unhurried pace.

Lemongrass Thai Kitchen

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Lemongrass Thai Kitchen, 1016 Lockwood Blvd, Oviedo, FL 32765-6027

A Thai kitchen in an unassuming Lockwood Boulevard plaza, calm and contemporary inside. The Panang duck leads the menu and guests still talk about it months later, joined by whole snapper dinners, lobster phad thai and tamarind shrimp with housemade sauces throughout. Durian ice cream waits for the adventurous.

Overlook Park

Parks & outdoors · $

1988 Spring Ave, Oviedo, FL

A small county park on the south shore of Lake Jesup, blissfully quiet under mature oaks. The boardwalk stretches over the water where osprey circle overhead and alligators drift past, with a fishing pier and shaded picnic pavilion nearby. Parking is tight and there are no restrooms, so plan accordingly.

PokeWorks (Oviedo)

Restaurants · $

45 W Mitchell Hammock Rd, Oviedo, FL 32765

A build-your-own poke counter in Oviedo running sushi-grade ahi, salmon, albacore, shrimp and scallops over rice or greens. Regulars favor the Hawaiian Ahi bowl and the poke burrito, with garlic spam musubi as the cult side order. The room is clean, fast and friendly, and guests call it their favorite healthy stop in town.

Ramen Takagi

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Ramen Takagi, 3635 Aloma Ave, Oviedo, FL 32765-6395

A small, warm, family-run ramen shop on Aloma Avenue where everything is made from scratch, with bar seats facing the kitchen. The black garlic tonkotsu leads the menu, the pork melting in the bowl, and the pan-fried gyoza carry a ginger note that reminds guests of Japan. Regulars call it the best ramen in the Orlando area.

Saigon Flavors

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

3573 Aloma Ave, Oviedo, FL 32765

Oviedo's prettiest Vietnamese room, family-owned, with colorful walls, lit cubbies of bric-a-brac and paintings of old Saigon scenes. The pho is the anchor, with rich broth and generous beef that one guest calls the best pho here, and the Curry Chicken Noodle Soup gets called one of the best things a visitor has ever tasted. The golden pancake arrives crisp and bright, and Charlie's Special lemon chicken is the house signature.

Sushi Pop

Japanese & sushi · $$

Sushi Pop, 310 W Mitchell Hammock Rd, Oviedo, FL 32765-4921

A bold, loud sushi room with anime pop-culture decor, run like an artist's studio. Seasonal crudo, nikkei maki and buffalo rock shrimp show the range, and the nigiri earns melt-in-your-mouth praise from regulars. Rolls run pricey and the room packs out, so book ahead.

Tikka Shack Indian Grub

Indian · $$

Tikka Shack Indian Grub, 1500 Alafaya Trl, Oviedo, FL 32765-4517, United States

A fast-casual Indian kitchen on Alafaya Trail that keeps flavors bold and the mood fun and casual. Regulars say Tikka Shack never misses, singling out the masala fries and the butter masala, ordered extra hot when they want heat. The tandoori chicken pizza lands on a naan crust, and the garlic naan gets described by guests as fluffy, soft and chewy.
