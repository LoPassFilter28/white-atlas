Fixtion Restaurant & Bar Local Pick

American · $$

15 N Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32801

Fixtion shape-shifts through the day on Orange Avenue: comfort food and huge comfy booths early, club energy after dark. The menu leans playful, with a Wall Street turkey club, chimichurri skirt steak, truffle fries, and an espresso martini regulars swear by. Game days run on the giant screen, and later the room turns to DJs and bottle service without losing its chill. Service can crawl when it fills, so settle in and order with patience.

Wall Street Plaza Local Pick

Nightlife & music · $$

25 Wall St, Orlando, FL 32801

One downtown block, eight venues, and on a big night the whole street belongs to the party. Wall Street Plaza is a bar-crawl sprawl with the Cantina pouring its house margarita, country spinning at Rodeo, and quieter corners like the Hen House between the stages. Guests come for the outdoor block-party energy, cold beer, and drinks priced right, and a group with mixed tastes never has to leave the block. Event nights can carry a cover, so check before you commit the crew.

Directions → · @wallstreetorlando

Dr. Phillips Center

Attractions · $$$

445 S Magnolia Ave, Orlando, FL 32801

Dr. Phillips Center anchors downtown Orlando's cultural life on Magnolia Avenue, a beautiful glass building guests praise first for its sound. Steinmetz Hall delivers acoustics guests call fantastic, the Walt Disney Theater hosts Broadway tours, and Judson's Live keeps things intimate with cabaret, while film-in-concert nights put an orchestra under the movie. The one headache is getting in and out: the center has no garage of its own and valet backs up on show nights, so plan the parking before the performance.
