The Robinson Cocktail Room Local Pick

Bars & lounges · $$

63 E Pine St, Orlando, FL 32801

Espresso by day gives way to disco, salsa, and a crowded dance floor. Wall Street Plaza Local Pick

Nightlife & music · $$

25 Wall St, Orlando, FL 32801

Eight venues share one legendary downtown block, and on a Saturday night the whole street belongs to the party. Wall Street Plaza has been the beating heart of downtown Orlando since 1995: the Cantina pouring the famous house margarita, Rodeo spinning country, intimate corners like the Hen House, and concert stages that pull real crowds. The Tavern Bar & Tacos

Mexican · $

The Tavern Bar & Tacos, 75 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32801

Not a dive bar, a vibe bar, says the sign, and The Tavern backs it up with birria tacos, frozen margaritas and darts flying until last call. The kitchen works the Mexican fusion lane hard: chase-a-dillas loaded and griddled, street corn, the Tavern Smash burger and wings for the table, plus mocktails for anyone pacing themselves. Taco Tuesday is a standing plan, the jukebox takes requests, and the whole room has the loose energy of a neighborhood hangout that happens to sit on Colonial Drive. Come hungry, order the birria, and stay for another round.

Uncle Tony's Donut Shoppe

Markets & shops · $$

Uncle Tony's Donut Shoppe, 703 N Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32801

First, the truth: there are no donuts. Uncle Tony's is one of the great crate digging rooms in Florida, a vinyl shop stacked with rare jazz, funk, soul, hip hop, Latin, Brazilian and African records, plus eight tracks, cassettes, memorabilia and DJ gear for the working crowd. A hidden back room rewards the patient, staff picks point you toward records you did not know you needed, and listening posts turn a quick stop into an afternoon. Collectors fly in for this shop; locals just call it a community staple. Bring a list, leave with a stack.
