Where to start in Day Trips

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

1921 Mount Dora

Restaurants · $$$

1921 Mount Dora, 142 E 4th Ave, Mt Dora, FL 32757-5584

Dinner here feels like being welcomed into an elegant friend's home, in a house that explains why. 1921 Mount Dora pairs a historic 1921 home with modern Florida cuisine built on local farms. Order the Spanish octopus with hazelnut romesco, the wood-oven roasted oysters with BLT butter, and the Wagyu burger with smoked Duke's mayonnaise. The cooking treats Florida ingredients as a story worth telling well. Choose it for a Mount Dora evening that feels personal rather than formal, and book it for guests you want to impress quietly.

BakeChop

Restaurants · $$

110 Artisan Aly, DeLand, FL 32720

A scratch kitchen on Artisan Alley is where DeLand gives farm-to-table real conviction. BakeChop serves the Left Hander burger and a Peach Jam Burger with goat cheese and jalapeños that guests call otherworldly. The Fish Bowl appears as a day's special, collard greens draw regular demand, and a quinoa salad steals attention it had no obvious claim to. The family motto centers food, family, friends, service, and good times, and plates reflect that list. Choose it when you want a DeLand meal built from scratch.

Blue Spring Manatee Kayak Tours

Attractions · $$

Blue Spring State Park, 2100 W French Ave, Orange City, FL 32763

In winter, hundreds of manatees pile into the 72-degree spring run at Blue Spring State Park, and the guided kayak tours from Orange City put you right alongside them. The water is so clear you can watch the manatees roll and graze beneath the boat, and the guides know exactly where the herds gather on any given morning. Go November through March for the manatee season, book the earliest tour of the day, and remember the rule: look, do not touch.

Cress Restaurant

Fine dining & steak · $$

Cress Restaurant, 103 W Indiana Ave, DeLand, FL 32720-4209, United States

Worth the drive to historic downtown DeLand: Cress is a farm-to-table restaurant built on the radical idea that a chef should know the farmer, the dairyman, and the fisherman by name. The globally inspired menu follows Florida's seasons, so the kitchen works with whatever the land is offering right now, and the staff talk about the food like they grew up with it. Zagat named it a top Orlando restaurant, and it earned it. Refined without stiffness, serious without a lecture. DeLand's quiet fine-dining crown jewel.

Donuts To Go

Sweets · $

502 N Spring Garden Ave, DeLand, FL 32720

An early-morning sweet spot leads with a square donut built for sharing. Donuts To Go serves a generously sized Square Glazed Donut alongside Maple Bacon, French Toast, Almond Joy, and a flaky Cronut. Chocolate cake donuts draw fans who call them heavenly, and caramel work gets its own praise. Breakfast widens the visit: burritos, biscuits and gravy, and New York-style bagels with cream cheese. Fresh, homemade, and kid-friendly, the shop earns local loyalty. Come hungry enough to take donuts and a real breakfast seriously together.

The Twisted Chopstick

Japanese & sushi · $$

412 S Woodland Blvd, DeLand, FL 32720

Woodland Boulevard in DeLand has a sushi stop built around family ownership and a playful Godzilla spirit. Set in a historic building, the room keeps things approachable while the kitchen sends out Fire Tonkatsu Ramen with real heat. The Black Widow and Samurai rolls lead the sushi side, and poke bowls plus sushi burritos widen the order for mixed tastes. You get creativity without formality, which makes it an easy recommendation for a relaxed DeLand dinner with friends.

Fun-Ko's Poke Bowls and Asian Snacks

Japanese & sushi · $$

Fun-Ko's Poke Bowls and Asian Snacks, 334 N Donnelly St, Mt Dora, FL 32757-5525, United States

In a region of drive-thrus, Fun-Ko's is a small joy: a tucked-away poke counter inside Mount Dora Marketplace where the fish is fresh, the bowls are generous, and the owner treats first-timers like regulars. Build your own from tuna, salmon, shrimp or tofu, or go signature with the Classic Hawaiian or the spicy Funko Fire. Adventurous? Try the jellyfish salad or grab onigiri for the road. Quiet courtyard seating, zero fuss, and the kind of wholesome freshness that makes you a monthly visitor without noticing.

Salt & Ember

Fine dining & steak · $$$

322 N Alexander St, Mount Dora, FL 32757

Mount Dora's dining scene keeps punching above its weight, and Salt & Ember is the newest reason why. Chef-owner Fred Bisaillon spent three decades cooking through Southern California, Bermuda, the Bahamas, and Nantucket before bringing his wood-fired touch to Alexander Street, and the result is a room that pairs bold flavor with a relaxed, elevated ease. Start with the raw bar, chilled oysters and caviar, then lean into prime steaks or the lobster carbonara that locals already talk about. The tropical patio makes the evening last. Dress up if you feel like it. Nobody minds either way.

The Goblin Market Restaurant

American · $$

The Goblin Market Restaurant, 330 Dora Drawdy Way, Mt Dora, FL 32757-5593, United States

Charming locals and tourists since 1996, the Goblin Market is Mount Dora's storybook gastropub, and the name fits. Inside, book-lined rooms glow like a library that learned to cook; outside, a secret-garden courtyard beneath a big camphor tree turns spectacular after dark. The Irish Whiskey Onion Soup is non-negotiable, locals will tell you, followed by the crab and lobster bisque and the Irish Cheddar Burger. It is the kind of place where dinner stretches long because nobody wants to leave.

Winter Haven

LEGO Galaxy + Galacticoaster

Attractions · $$$

LEGOLAND Florida Resort, 1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

Space, built from bricks, lands in Winter Haven. LEGO Galaxy, which opened in February 2026 at LEGOLAND Florida, is the park's new space themed land anchored by the Galacticoaster, its first indoor launch coaster. The 36 inch height requirement makes it a genuine first coaster milestone for young kids, and the indoor queue is merciful in the summer heat. For families with small children, it gives the Winter Haven day trip one more reason to happen, especially for kids ready for a first launch.

Legoland Florida

Attractions · $$$

1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

Winter Haven's LEGOLAND is the theme park for kids who build. Opened in 2011 on the historic Cypress Gardens grounds, the resort packs over 50 rides, shows, and attractions aimed squarely at ages two to twelve: The Dragon coaster in LEGO Kingdoms, Ninjago rides, the LEGO Movie World, and Miniland USA, where millions of bricks recreate American landmarks. Add the water park and SEA LIFE aquarium for a full multi-day trip. Shorter lines, gentler thrills, and imagination everywhere. The perfect first big theme park.

Peppa Pig Theme Park Florida

Attractions · $$$

1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

Small riders grip the bar on Daddy Pig's Roller Coaster, splash through muddy puddles, then run straight back for another short line. Peppa Pig Theme Park in Winter Haven, next to LEGOLAND, is built for ages 2 to 6 with gentle rides, live shows, and Certified Autism Center support. This is a first-park day done right: go early, move at toddler pace, and leave before the meltdown hour. Tourist Trail: Worth it for preschoolers: skip it if your youngest is past Peppa.

SEA LIFE Florida

Attractions · $$$

LEGOLAND Florida Resort, 1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

An aquarium built like a theme park gives tired legs a cooler plan. SEA LIFE Florida opened in June 2025 inside LEGOLAND Florida Resort, and admission is now included with park admission. More than 3,000 creatures across 150 species fill the tanks, with a 180 degree ocean tunnel and a Theme Park Under the Sea concept aimed at kids. Use it as the midday air conditioned break between outdoor rides, when young visitors need shade, water views, and a slower pace.

Clermont

Over The Top Pita

Mediterranean · $

Over the Top Pita, 240 Citrus Tower Blvd, Clermont, FL 34711-2704

Fast food does not have to taste fast at this family owned pita shop. Warm pita holds gyro sliced right off the spit, crisp vegetables and bold sauces stacked until the wrap barely closes. Every order is made to order, with the classic Gyro Pita as the standard move and loaded pitas for the truly hungry. The room runs on no corporate kitchen warmth from people proud of what they serve. Choose it when a quick lunch should still feel made for you.

Revolution Adventures

Attractions · $$$

4000 FL-33, Clermont, FL 34714

Queues are nowhere in sight when mud is flying off an ATV tire. Revolution Adventures covers 230 acres of purpose-built terrain in Clermont, about 30 minutes west of the parks. Guided ATV tours and Mucky Duck amphibious vehicle tours run through mud pits, wooded trails, and open fields, and you get dirty whichever vehicle you pick. Go in the morning before the Florida heat settles in, bring a change of clothes, and choose this when your group needs a break from theme park lines.

The Crooked Spoon

Restaurants · $$

200 Citrus Tower Blvd, Clermont, FL 34711

A beloved Orlando food truck grew up without losing its hunger. Now a Clermont gastropub, The Crooked Spoon serves Chef Saelg's bold menu, from the 420 Burger with candied bacon and pineapple relish to lobster grilled cheese and six-cheese lobster mac. Polynesian pig wings widen the range, and blue cheese chips are legendary among regulars. A full bar, outdoor seating, and a room that feels like the neighborhood's living room complete it. Pick it for foodie ambition carried by small-town heart, in Clermont rather than downtown.

Clearwater Beach Day Trip

Parks & outdoors · $$

1 Causeway Blvd, Clearwater Beach, FL 33767

Clearwater Beach is the Gulf-coast answer to Orlando's theme-park days: powder-white sand, calm shallow water and sunsets that draw a nightly crowd to Pier 60. The drive runs about two hours each way, which makes it a legitimate day trip if you start early. Pack the cooler, claim sand early near the pier for the facilities, and stay for the sunset celebration if you can handle the drive home after dark. It is the beach day Orlando locals pick when the Gulf, not the Atlantic, calls.

Crooked Can Minneola

Breweries · $$

1600 Crooked Can Loop, Minneola, FL 34715

Winter Garden's beloved brewery went big in Minneola, and the scale changes the outing. The City Center complex packs a full production brewery and canning line, a sprawling taproom, and a food hall of 11 local vendors chosen to spotlight Lake County flavor. Out back, a beer garden, a live music stage, and a splash pad give groups room to spread out. Core brews and seasonal one offs pour at the new Crooked Can headquarters. Choose it when you want a destination brewery built as Minneola's gathering place, not just a taproom stop.

Dinosaur World

Attractions · $$

5145 Harvey Tew Rd, Plant City, FL 33565

Out in Plant City, Dinosaur World is a roadside classic in the Florida tradition. Over 200 life-size dinosaurs spread across 20 shaded acres, arranged in species groups so real some visitors swear they see movement in the trees. Kids dig for fossils, pan for gems, crack geodes, and excavate a life-size Stegosaurus at the Boneyard. A giant T-Rex greets you at the gate, and yes, the whole place is dog-friendly. Unpretentious, educational, and a slow wander through prehistory. A genuinely good half-day for dino-obsessed kids.

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex

Attractions · $$

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex, Space Commerce Way, Merritt Island, FL 32953

There is nothing on this earth quite like standing beneath a real spaceship. At the Visitor Complex you walk the ground where America went to the Moon, come face to face with the Space Shuttle Atlantis, and ride the bus tour out to the Apollo/Saturn V Center, where a Saturn V hangs overhead like the future it still promises. Meet a veteran astronaut, let the Rocket Garden remind you how small your worries are, and watch your kids decide, right there, to become astronauts.

Merritt Island Bioluminescence Kayak Tours

Attractions · $$

951 Kiwanis Island Park Rd, Merritt Island, FL 32952

On summer nights, the lagoons around Merritt Island glow. Dinoflagellates in the water light up electric blue with every paddle stroke, and mullet darting under the kayak leave comet trails behind them. Local outfitters run guided clear-kayak tours after dark from roughly June through October, timed around the new moon for maximum glow. It is one of the most surreal things you can do within an hour of Orlando, and it books out fast in peak season.

St. Augustine Day Trip

Museums & landmarks · $$

11 S Castillo Dr, St. Augustine, FL 32084

About two hours northeast, St. Augustine wears its history well. The city is the oldest continuously inhabited European settlement in the continental United States, and the Castillo de San Marcos still guards the bay. St. George Street strings together colonial architecture with ice cream shops and pubs, and the beaches sit a short drive over the Bridge of Lions. Go on a weekday to dodge the weekend crowds, and do the fort first before the afternoon heat builds. It is the day trip for travelers who want real history with their beach time.

Wild Florida

Attractions · $$

3301 Lake Cypress Rd, Kenansville, FL 34739

Forty five minutes from the theme parks, Central Florida turns genuinely wild. Wild Florida sits at the Headwaters of the Everglades, where airboats skim 4,200 acres of protected marsh while the captain points out gators on logs and hawks overhead. Back on land, the 15 acre gator park holds more than 200 native and exotic animals with feeding demonstrations, and the drive thru safari lets you feed zebras and bison from your car. Pick it for the side of Florida the coasters never show.
