Where to start in Day Trips

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

1921 Mount Dora

Restaurants · $$$

1921 Mount Dora, 142 E 4th Ave, Mt Dora, FL 32757-5584

1921 Mount Dora sets modern Florida cooking inside a historic 1921 house, and guests describe the meal as personal rather than formal, elegant and unhurried. The kitchen leans on local farms for Spanish octopus with hazelnut romesco, wood-oven roasted oysters and a Wagyu burger, and regulars warn you to go hungry and never skip the appetizers. It is the Mount Dora splurge guests call one of their best meals ever, finished with the 1921 cheesecake made with goat cheese.

Attractions · $$

Blue Spring State Park, 2100 W French Ave, Orange City, FL 32763

At Blue Spring State Park, guided kayaks slip into crystal-clear, 72-degree spring water where manatees roll and graze beneath the boat. Guests call drifting alongside them, noses, backs and tails breaking the surface, one of the highlights of a Florida trip, like stepping into another world. Fun-Ko's Poke Bowls and Asian Snacks

Japanese & sushi · $$

Fun-Ko's Poke Bowls and Asian Snacks, 334 N Donnelly St, Mt Dora, FL 32757-5525, United States

Tucked inside Mount Dora Marketplace on Donnelly Street, Fun-Ko's is a cheerful, low-key poke counter that regulars call genuinely their go to. Build your own bowl from fresh ingredients with mix and match sauces, or go signature with the Funko Salmon, the Classic Hawaiian or the spicy Funko Fire, with onigiri and a vegan bowl in the mix. Portions are big and beautiful, prices feel right, and devotees swear it rivals the best poke outside Hawaii.

Salt & Ember

Fine dining & steak · $$$

322 N Alexander St, Mount Dora, FL 32757

Salt & Ember brings a wood-fired steakhouse to Alexander Street in Mount Dora, relaxed and warm with an elevated polish. The starters run from caviar croquettes and charred octopus with chorizo to tuna tataki and bread service with herb butter, with steak frites and the much talked about lobster carbonara behind them. Guests call it a must try in the area, and the Key lime cheesecake makes a bright finish.

Directions → · @saltandemberrestaurant

The Goblin Market Restaurant

American · $$

The Goblin Market Restaurant, 330 Dora Drawdy Way, Mt Dora, FL 32757-5593, United States

The Goblin Market is Mount Dora's storybook gastropub, a cozy, book-lined hideaway on Dora Drawdy Way where guests love being surrounded by books. Regulars insist you have to have the Irish Whiskey Onion Soup, followed by the crab and lobster bisque, the Irish Cheddar Burger or a strawberry salmon salad praised as fresh and generous. The secret-garden courtyard under the camphor tree turns the room romantic, and while prices run high, nobody seems in a hurry to leave.

Winter Haven

LEGO Galaxy + Galacticoaster

Attractions · $$$

LEGOLAND Florida Resort, 1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

LEGO Galaxy at LEGOLAND Florida sends young riders into a brick-built space adventure anchored by Galacticoaster, an indoor launch coaster gentle enough to serve as a first. In the queue, kids design their own spaceship before boarding, with the Biff Dipper animatronic setting the mission. The ride has already earned the nickname Cosmic Rewind for kids: silky-smooth, immersive and tuned for family thrills.

Directions → · @legolandflorida

Legoland Florida

Attractions · $$$

1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

On the historic Cypress Gardens grounds in Winter Haven, LEGOLAND Florida is the hands-on theme park for kids who build, relaxed and uncrowded next to the mega parks. The Dragon coaster, LEGO NINJAGO The Ride and Miniland USA anchor the lands, and kids can even build LEGO creations while waiting in line. Waits stay short and the creativity is everywhere, though some rides and restaurants sit closed on any given day, and guests call Granny's Apple Fries a must.

Directions → · @legolandflorida

Peppa Pig Theme Park Florida

Attractions · $$$

1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

Next to LEGOLAND in Winter Haven, Peppa Pig Theme Park Florida is a toddler-scaled first theme park: gentle, colorful and relaxed, and guests say it never feels as overwhelming as the larger parks. Little riders loop Daddy Pig's Roller Coaster again and again, splash through the Muddy Puddles Splash Pad and float up on Peppa's Balloon Ride, with interactive play areas sized just right for small children. It is a small park by design, which families call exactly the point for first theme park memories.

Directions → · @peppapigthemeparkflorida

SEA LIFE Florida

Attractions · $$$

LEGOLAND Florida Resort, 1 Legoland Way, Winter Haven, FL 33884

Inside LEGOLAND Florida, SEA LIFE Florida builds its tanks like theme park sets, a Theme Park Under the Sea where the habitats riff on rides and coasters. The 180-degree ocean tunnel, stingray bay, the Freshwater Swamp and Key West Harbor keep the pace gentle and kid-sized. With admission folded into the park ticket, it works as the air-conditioned reset between outdoor rides rather than a standalone trip.

Directions → · @legolandflorida

Clermont

Over The Top Pita

Mediterranean · $

Over the Top Pita, 240 Citrus Tower Blvd, Clermont, FL 34711-2704

Over The Top Pita is a family-run, no-frills counter on Citrus Tower Boulevard in Clermont where every wrap is made to order. The Gyro Pita comes sliced right off the spit, stacked with crisp vegetables and bold sauces until it barely closes, and guests call the gyros among the best in Florida. Great people, great food and great prices is the regulars' refrain, with lamb, Philly cheesesteak and fajita pitas rounding out the board.

Revolution Adventures

Attractions · $$$

4000 FL-33, Clermont, FL 34714

Revolution Adventures in Clermont trades theme park lines for mud: guided ATV trail rides, side-by-side buggies and the Mucky Duck, an amphibious vehicle that splashes from trail into water. The pace reads more scenic drive than adrenaline chase, with a thorough safety briefing that leaves beginners feeling secure in helmets and goggles. Families fill out the day with the Archery Arrow Blitz and bass fishing on the private lake, and everyone expects to get dirty.

Directions → · @revolutionadventures

The Crooked Spoon

Restaurants · $$

200 Citrus Tower Blvd, Clermont, FL 34711

The Crooked Spoon is a low key Clermont gastropub on Citrus Tower Boulevard, chef-driven but laid-back, the kind of neighborhood room where guests say they never leave disappointed. The 420 Burger, lobster grilled cheese and six-cheese lobster mac carry the indulgent side, while Soco wings in garlic cheese whiskey sauce get called a must-try. It stays fairly quiet, with servers glad to steer you toward dishes you rarely find anywhere else.

Cress Restaurant

Fine dining & steak · $$

Cress Restaurant, 103 W Indiana Ave, DeLand, FL 32720-4209, United States

Cress is a warm, intimate dining room in historic downtown DeLand, farm-to-table in spirit, with a globally inspired menu that follows Florida's seasons. Guests rave about the garlic escargots, the crispy duck breast and a Moroccan lamb shank that falls off the bone, finishing with white chocolate crème brûlée. Portions run small for the price, but the service is on point and the room feels made for celebrations.

The Twisted Chopstick

Japanese & sushi · $$

412 S Woodland Blvd, DeLand, FL 32720

The Twisted Chopstick is DeLand's cozy, Godzilla-themed sushi stop on Woodland Boulevard, family-run and as relaxed as a second home. The Fire Tonkatsu ramen brings real heat, the Black Widow and Samurai rolls lead the sushi case, and starters like Dragon Balls and gyoza are built for sharing. Regulars call it the best sushi in town, and poke bowls and sushi burritos widen the order for mixed tastes.

Clearwater Beach Day Trip

Parks & outdoors · $$

1 Causeway Blvd, Clearwater Beach, FL 33767

Clearwater Beach is the Gulf coast reset: sugar-white sand, fine and soft underfoot, calm shallow water and a festive promenade along Beach Walk. Pier 60 anchors the scene with street performers, musicians and local artisans selling candles, bracelets and art. Guests call it heaven on earth, and the sunset celebration at the pier is the day's exclamation point.

Crooked Can Minneola

Breweries · $$

1600 Crooked Can Loop, Minneola, FL 34715

Crooked Can's Minneola outpost is a destination-sized take on the Winter Garden brewery: a sprawling production brewery and taproom fronting a food hall at the City Center complex. Space Cowboi IPA, Florida Sunshine and rotating beer flights pour inside, while food hall counters like This Little Piggy BBQ, Sir Benji's Donuts and The Local Butcher keep groups fed. Out back, a beer garden and live music stage give everyone room to spread out.

Directions → · @crookedcan

Dinosaur World

Attractions · $$

5145 Harvey Tew Rd, Plant City, FL 33565

Dinosaur World is an old-Florida roadside classic in Plant City, where crossing a bridge into thick, shaded trees feels like walking into Jurassic Park. Life-size dinosaurs stand in species groupings along the paths, and kids dig for fossils at the Fossil Dig, pan at the Dino Gem Mine and excavate a life-size Stegosaurus at the Boneyard. Extras like the train and gem mine cost on top of admission, but the pace stays unhurried, and dogs are welcome along for the wander.

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex

Attractions · $$

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex, Space Commerce Way, Merritt Island, FL 32953

Standing beneath the Space Shuttle Atlantis on Merritt Island, guests say you can't help but be impressed: the Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex turns real spaceflight hardware into an awe-inspiring day out. The bus tour to the Apollo/Saturn V Center, the Rocket Garden and astronaut encounters keep the history close enough to touch, and the sheer scale of the Saturn V stops people mid-sentence. Tickets, food and parking run steep, but visitors keep calling it educational at its best and worth the time and cost.

Directions → · @kennedyspacecenter

Merritt Island Bioluminescence Kayak Tours

Attractions · $$

951 Kiwanis Island Park Rd, Merritt Island, FL 32952

On dark lagoon water around Merritt Island, the bay itself lights up: dinoflagellates flare electric blue with every paddle stroke through a clear kayak. Mullet darting underneath leave comet trails, and guests say the glow is hard to get on camera but amazing in person, strongest around the new moon. Pack serious bug spray and double-check your outfitter's check-in, since several companies launch from the same park and your clothes and shoes will get wet.

St. Augustine Day Trip

Museums & landmarks · $$

11 S Castillo Dr, St. Augustine, FL 32084

St. Augustine wears its centuries openly, and the Castillo de San Marcos is its anchor: a coquina fort, built from broken shells and mortar, that guarded Matanzas Bay through Spanish, English and American rule. Guests say you feel the history in every step, and the views from the top over the harbor and old town repay the climb. St. George Street strings colonial architecture together with ice cream shops and pubs, and parking near the fort runs scarce, so the city garage earns its fee.

Wild Florida

Attractions · $$

3301 Lake Cypress Rd, Kenansville, FL 34739

Wild Florida sits at the Headwaters of the Everglades, where airboats skim protected marsh and captains know exactly where the gators bask. Guests come back raving about alligators seen up close, babies included, and the gator feeding demonstrations back on land. A drive-thru safari, a capybara encounter and a chance to hold a baby alligator round out a hands-on day that feels like the middle of nowhere.
