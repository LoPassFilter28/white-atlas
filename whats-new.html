What's new in the guide

Every verification pass, correction, addition, and removal, on the record. Places are checked against primary sources; when a listing changes, it is noted here. Spotted something off? mail@407.fyi.

October 18, 2026

Orlando's electronic music, in one section

The guide now has a home for the city's dance music: the Orlando sound section maps the rooms by sound, from house at Aero and Celine to the underground crews at Iron Cow and the touring bills at The Vanguard, with the crews who throw the nights, the shape of the festival year, and the 1990s breaks history behind it all.

October 18, 2026

An events calendar for Orlando

The guide now keeps a calendar: the Orlando events calendar rounds up what is on around the city, from the weekly markets and food truck nights to concerts, home games, festivals and the holiday nights at the parks. This edition covers the rest of October and November 2026, checked against venue, league and festival sources, and the calendar is rebuilt at the start of every month.

October 17, 2026

Four listings are on hold

Zama in Mills 50, the Orlando Magic Pregame Warmups & Dining Experience, Bora Bora Smoothie Cafe near UCF, and the Amazonica location at Vineland Premium Outlets are out of the guide for now. The research could not verify Zama's announced opening, the Magic experience's current sale, a Bora Bora listing at the UCF address, or a location-specific listing for Amazonica at the outlets. They will return only if those facts become verifiable.

October 17, 2026

A cleanup: products and unverifiable listings are out

Sixteen listings left the guide today. Ticket and pass products (Universal Orlando Resort tickets, the Go City and Orlando Essentials passes, the Universal VIP tour, and the resort pool and water park day passes) are products, not places. The three Caribbean Moonshine listings at the Vineland outlets were one business sold as three tour products. Melt N Dip, closed with its building evacuated, is out until a reopening is real. The Robinson Cocktail Room and Empanada & Co. carried closure signals we could not clear. Paradiso Grande Resort had no verifiable guest footprint. Seby's Pizza's address and map point disagreed with each other, and the Majesty Building is a fenced tower nobody can visit. Every removal follows the same rule as always: if we cannot verify it, it does not stay listed.

October 17, 2026

One removal: the airport

Orlando International Airport is out of the guide. Its food scene is genuinely good, and those restaurants earn their own listings when they stand on their own; an airport itself is not a place this guide sends you to discover.

October 17, 2026

Similar feel, on every venue page

Every venue page now ends its story with six more places matched by vibe rather than by neighborhood: the spots across Greater Orlando closest in spirit to the one you are reading, whether that is a quiet coffee bar across town or a loud group dinner spot two districts over. It runs on the same matching behind the finder's By vibe search.

October 17, 2026

Big Fin Seafood Kitchen has closed

The Dellagio seafood house on Via Dellagio Way has closed permanently, sold and then shut in late August with little notice to diners. It has been removed from the guide, along with its page and every list it appeared on.

October 17, 2026

The Long View: an Orlando heritage page

The guide now has a heritage page, The Long View: the longest-running tables, stages, markets, and landmarks in Greater Orlando, told in eras from a dining room family that dates to 1905 down to the rooms a newer Orlando already treats as permanent. Every entry links to its full page in the guide.

October 17, 2026

Venue pages carry the word from guests

The Local Picks have a new section, What People Are Saying: real words from public reviews, each one credited to its reviewer with a link to the source, the venue's Google rating stated plainly, and an honest take from the guide on the complaints that come up again and again. It starts with the Local Picks and grows from there.

October 15, 2026

West Orlando page retired

Same day, a correction: the West Orlando page has been retired and its Colonial Drive listings removed from the guide. Mikado and Teak in MetroWest and Brazi Burger in Orlo Vista remain, filed under More Orlando. The Day Trips page and the district moves from the sort below stand.

October 15, 2026

More Orlando, sorted

The catch-all page has been taken apart. The far drives (Mount Dora, DeLand, Winter Haven, the Space Coast, St. Augustine) now live on a Day Trips page, the Colonial Drive corridor has a West Orlando page, and fourteen places that were filed under More Orlando while sitting inside other districts (Dr. Phillips corridor spots on International Drive, Regency Village in the Disney Area, two Narcoossee Road spots in Lake Nona) have been moved to the districts they actually sit in. What remains in More Orlando is the honest remainder: the suburban towns and the in-town pockets without a district name yet.

October 15, 2026

Lake Nona, filled in

Thirteen Lake Nona listings join the guide: Park Pizza & Brewing, Chroma, Bosphorous, Nona Blue, HAVEN Kitchen and the Living Room Bar at the Wave Hotel, Don Julio, Maki Hibachi, Hot Taco, Tomasino's, Pho Haven, Pisco Express and BaBbi BaBbi. The district page now covers Town Center, the Wave Hotel, the Narcoossee corridor and Eagle Creek properly. Drive Shack was checked and is not listed: it closed earlier this year.

October 15, 2026

Every place gets its own page, plus the I-Drive playbook

Every place in the guide now has its own page: the full write-up, address, price, directions, and the closest verified places nearby, all reachable from any district list. Also new: the I-Drive playbook, a visitor survival guide for International Drive covering ticket booths, timeshare pitches, rental toll devices, hotel fee stacks, line-skip pricing and parking, with verified picks for dinner, late food and a calm breakfast.

October 15, 2026

Truth pass: guide tightened to 644 verified places

Eight announced listings that have not opened yet, including the Museum of Ice Cream and Eola Food Hall, are held out of the guide until they open. Four local additions join: Hollerbach's Willow Tree Cafe in Sanford, Columbia Restaurant and Celebration Town Tavern in Celebration, and Canvas Restaurant & Market in Lake Nona.

October 15, 2026

Trust and navigation pass

One disclosure wording now runs across the whole guide, a new privacy page explains what 407 FYI does and does not collect, and every district finder page is linked from the home page and the full guide, so all 647 places are reachable from any door.

October 15, 2026

Convention guide launched, guide grows to 647 places

A dedicated convention guide is live, built for visitors heading to the Orange County Convention Center: the three concourses and their street addresses, the four hotels connected by pedestrian bridge, parking and transport patterns, attendee logistics, and a what-to-avoid list for show days.

  • 34 listings added, taking the guide from 613 to 647 places.
  • Convention corridor: Hyatt Regency Orlando, Rosen Centre, Rosen Plaza, and Hilton Orlando, plus Pointe Orlando dining, Madame Tussauds, and Paradiso Grande Resort.
  • Dr. Phillips and Sand Lake Road: Norman's Orlando, Big Fin Seafood Kitchen (closed August 2026), Dragonfly, Fleming's, Eddie V's, Ocean Prime, Ruth's Chris, Vines by H, The Whiskey, and more.
  • Outlets: Disney Character Warehouse, both Ford's Garage locations, Orlando Outlet Marketplace, and Lake Buena Vista Factory Stores.
