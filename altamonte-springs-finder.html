Altamonte Springs

Altamonte Springs runs on the Cranes Roost end of I-4 suburbia, but its independent tables hide in the retail seams: a French bakery doing the classics properly, a Cantonese dining room locals swear by, and a burger counter with a following. The mall gravity is real; the good stuff sits just outside it.

Top Picks

Where to start in Altamonte Springs

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

Burger Vault

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

Burger Vault, 520 W State Road 436, Altamonte Springs, FL 32714-4000, United States

A small mom-and-pop burger counter on State Road 436 in Altamonte Springs, building halal burgers guests call insanely juicy and wonderfully messy in the best way. The Vault Burger is the regulars' order, the Volcano Burger brings the heat, and the seasoned fries want a side of vault sauce for dipping. A pistachio shake cools things down, and the whole place runs clean, friendly, and unhurried.

Directions → · @burgervaultorlando

J'Adore the French Bakery

Bakeries · $$

J'Adore the French Bakery, 910 Sand Lake Rd, Altamonte Springs, FL 32714-7053, United States

An unassuming little French bakery in Altamonte Springs that regulars describe as a sleeper. The croissants are crisp and crunchy outside, layered and puffy inside, and nothing in the case runs too sweet: baguettes, a braided bacon twist, and canele, all subtle and impeccable. The room is small, and the pastry makes the argument by itself.

Directions → · @jadore.thefrenchbakery

Yummy House

Chinese · $$

478 E Altamonte Dr #102, Altamonte Springs, FL 32701

An old-school Cantonese dining room in Altamonte Springs that transplanted New Englanders call the real thing. The Eight Treasures clay pot is the star guests point newcomers to, with salt and pepper calamari, house fried rice with real shrimp, Hong Kong roast duck and dim sum around it. Portions run huge, dishes land hot and fresh, and the room stays no-frills and family style.
