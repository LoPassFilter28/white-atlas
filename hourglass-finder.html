Charlie's Bakery

Bakeries · $$

Charlie's Bakery, 3213 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806, United States

Half a century of mornings has made Charlie's an institution on Curry Ford. The bakery has fed Orlando since 1971, filling its case with cakes, eclairs, cupcakes, and the Parker House rolls regulars call famous. The surprise is super premium ice cream churned in house, dense and rich, with rotating flavors such as the Sunshine State lemon strawberry swirl. That flavor won the Orlando Sentinel's 150th Scoop contest, according to the record. Treat this as the neighborhood stop that covers dessert twice.

El Coqui Ninja Restaurant

Latin & Caribbean · $$

El Coqui Ninja Restaurant, 3097 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806-3301, United States

Mofongo meets lo mein on Curry Ford Road, and the mashup is the whole point. El Coqui Ninja fuses Puerto Rican soul with Asian comfort in dishes like cuerno beef eggrolls and crab rangoon, set beside pepper steak and roast pork mofongo with shrimp. A sake margarita or fresh passion fruit juice helps you decide slowly. The room is quirky and cozy, with flowered couches and projector boxes at the tables. Come curious and let the island ninja combination work.

Foxtail Coffee

Coffee & cafes · $

2401 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806

A hometown coffee company can grow across a city and still pour like a neighbor. Foxtail started as one small Orlando shop and became a local institution while keeping an unhurried, neighborly warmth at each location. Espresso stays serious, cold brew flights turn tasting into a ritual, and seasonal drinks lean playful without feeling gimmicky. You can bring the dog for a pup cup, camp with a laptop, and watch the city run on caffeine. Choose Foxtail when you want Orlando coffee done right, without rushing the people drinking it.

Grocery Los Hermanos

Mexican · $

4130 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806

Buy your chiles, watch the butcher case, then sit down for tacos that shame dedicated shops. Grocery Los Hermanos on Curry Ford is a family owned market and taqueria in East Orlando. Carne asada and al pastor tacos arrive on fresh handmade tortillas, verde enchiladas carry a kick locals return for, and aguas frescas stay ice cold. The dining corner is bright and art filled. Grocery first and restaurant by destiny, it is exactly the kind of place Orlando runs on daily.

Hourglass Brewing

Breweries · $$

Hourglass Brewing, 2500 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806-2506, United States

A back patio in 2012 is where Hourglass started, two friends brewing, and that spirit never left. The Curry Ford District taproom pours in a former church with more than 40 rotating house beers, all natural, with names regulars describe as gloriously unhinged. Video game touches, an NES corner, and trivia nights make the room feel nerdy in the best way. Order a flight, settle at the long bar, and let the room's good humor work on you. Choose Hourglass when independent craft beer and playfulness matter more than polish.

Pizza Bruno

Pizza · $$

Pizza Bruno, 3990 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806-2702

Order the garlic knots first; the table will be glad you did. Pizza Bruno is owner Bruno Zacchini's love letter to the Jersey shore summers that taught him dough, turned into a wood-fired Neapolitan room on Curry Ford Road. The crust is naturally leavened, blistered and chewy, and the place hums with neighborhood conviction. Pies are playful but never gimmicky, built on tight technique, and a second location in College Park covers the craving west of the tracks. It is the Curry Ford pizza night regulars defend.

Shaka Donuts

Sweets · $$

2641 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806

Made-to-order donuts change the equation in the Hourglass District, and the difference is not subtle. Shaka serves fluffy, oversized rings: maple bacon for sweet-savory believers, banana pudding for dreamers, and Boston cream for traditionalists. A breakfast sandwich served inside a glazed donut counts as the power move. Coffee is good, the shop is sunny, and locals call these some of the best donuts in town. Believe the local verdict when you want a warm ring rather than a case donut, and order the sandwich if hungry.

Smokemade Meats + Eats

BBQ · $$

1400 S Crystal Lake Dr, Orlando, FL 32806

Concrete floors, a menu on butcher paper, and brisket doing the talking. Pitmaster Tyler Brunache learned Central Texas barbecue running pop-ups in DC that sold out in minutes, then came home to Orlando and opened Smokemade on Curry Ford West. Salt and pepper pork ribs carry a proper smoky edge, jalapeno cheddar sausage holds its own beside Texas benchmarks, and house baked bread comes alongside everything. Come hungry, greet the pitmaster if you get the chance, and expect to plan a return visit.

Theo's Kitchen

Mediterranean · $$

Theo's Kitchen, 2952 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32806, United States

A Curry Ford kitchen pulls off a double act few menus could carry. Family owned for more than 25 years, Theo's Kitchen stacks the gyro platter with tzatziki that tastes homemade, then serves fried chicken people call the best in Orlando. The bird is crispy and golden, the kind that reminds you what fried chicken should be. Greeks eat here, Southerners eat here and the line makes sense after one visit. Everybody leaves planning a return, usually for the opposite half of the menu.

Twistee Treat

Sweets · $

Twistee Treat, 4500 Curry Ford Rd, Orlando, FL 32812

A 25-foot ice cream cone rising off International Drive is impossible to miss. Twistee Treat, a Florida landmark since 1983, serves real fresh soft serve the old-school way inside that giant cone. Creamy twists come in cups, cones, shakes, malts, and floats, plus the signature Brownie Boat and banana boat. A waffle taco and cookiewich keep kids happy after park days. Prices stay cheerful and the format stays simple. Choose it for a full stop of nostalgia, especially when soft serve should taste uncomplicated.
