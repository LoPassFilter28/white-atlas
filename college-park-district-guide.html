College Park

Edgewater Drive does it all: hand-rolled pasta, birria with a cult following, and the pub where the whole neighborhood lands.

Scroll the story

College Park never needed a theme to feel like a destination. It has Edgewater Drive, a string of lakes, and neighbors who know exactly where their table is. This is Orlando at walking pace: bungalows and brick storefronts, dinner that turns into dessert two doors down, and mornings that start with pancakes and strong coffee. Small, proud, and deliciously self contained, it rewards anyone who slows down.

The Icons

The Edgewater Drive hall of fame.

Restaurant·$$

QuesaLoco

QuesaLoco started as a food truck with lines that wrapped around the block on Friday nights, and now the brick-and-mortar on Fairbanks Ave carries that same joyful chaos. This is birria country: the quesabirria tacos fold beef and cheese into a crisp tortilla built for dunking in rich consommé, and the birria ramen takes that obsession somewhere brand new. Add loco elote on a stick, birria pizza for the undecided, and a menu that reads like a greatest-hits list of Mexican street food. Go expecting noise, flavor, and no regrets.

Italian·$$

Turci Pasta

Watching fresh pasta get rolled through the pasta window at Turci Pasta is half the magic of dinner here. College Park's little Italian jewel makes everything by hand, every day: purple sweet potato gnocchi, black truffle ravioli, pear ravioli poached in wine, lasagna that tastes like someone's grandmother still runs the kitchen. Cozy, romantic, the kind of room where dates and anniversaries feel inevitable. Order the cannoli at the end and thank me later.

Pub·$$

Ollie's Public House

Ask College Park where the neighborhood goes when it wants everything at once, and they will point you to Ollie's. This Edgewater Drive public house pairs a rotating craft beer list with more than thirty TVs, live music, and a big covered patio where dogs get water and cold cuts by name. The Legend sandwich and the Wall Street Reuben are the plates regulars swear by, the bar food runs deep and satisfying, and a rotating Good Bite menu item gives back to a local cause. It is the room where weeknights are never boring.

The Main Street

Edgewater Drive is the whole show: walk it, eat it, repeat.

Italian·$$

Adriatico Trattoria Italiana

Ask College Park for its quiet showstopper and regulars lower their voices for Adriatico. A husband and wife team runs this small Edgewater Drive trattoria like a dinner party in Abruzzo: he cooks, she runs the room, and everything arrives made from scratch. The spaghetti with clams tastes like the coast, the grouper earns its regulars, and the torta della nonna closes the night exactly right. Book ahead. The room is tiny on purpose.

Ice Cream Shop·$

The Soda Fountain

Every main street needs a sugar rush with a backstory, and College Park has The Soda Fountain. Step up to the long white counter on Edgewater Drive for locally made ice cream piled into sundaes with names like the Zoo and the Apocalypto, plus floats and shakes that taste like childhood ordered correctly. Kids press against the case, regulars know their scoop, and nobody leaves in a bad mood. It is impossible. Science has checked.

Morning on the Drive

American Cafe·$

Shakers American Cafe

Shakers is the breakfast room College Park treats like a birthright. The walls wear a famous collection of salt and pepper shakers, the coffee lands fast and hot, and the cinnamon roll pancakes have ended more arguments than any mediator in Orlando. Order the bacon avocado omelette if you want the table to go quiet, or the maple glazed pork belly benedict if you want it to applaud. Arrive hungry. Leave planning tomorrow.

Coffee Shop·$

Foxtail Coffee

Foxtail Coffee is where Edgewater Drive starts its day on purpose. The College Park shop pours locally roasted coffee in a warm room of wood and metal, with a covered patio made for slow starts and laptop marathons alike. Order a flat white, add a scoop from the Kelly's Homemade Ice Cream counter sharing the space, and watch the neighborhood file in like it is roll call. Morning people are made here, not born.

The Itinerary

Make a day of it

Morning

Shakers for cinnamon roll pancakes, then Foxtail for the coffee that makes it official.

Afternoon

Walk Edgewater Drive end to end, boutiques to bookshops, and finish with a sundae at The Soda Fountain.

Evening

Birria at QuesaLoco or pasta at Turci. Either way, end the night on the patio at Ollie's.
