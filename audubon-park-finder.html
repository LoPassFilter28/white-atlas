Coro

American · $$$

Coro, 3022 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-2204

The name means chorus, and the menu sings in one voice. Chef Tim Lovero's Audubon Park restaurant earned a Michelin Bib Gourmand for small plates that change with the seasons and the chef's mood: warm potato naan with whipped chicken skin butter and marmalade, lamb shoulder so tender it surrenders, and a dessert riffing on fries and a milkshake with panna cotta, crispy sunchoke and cocoa nibs. The open kitchen puts the cooks within arm's reach, and the wine list leans natural. Choose it for intimate, evolving cooking at its best.

Domu

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

3201 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Since November 2016, Domu has been the ramen room Orlando talks about. The East End Market original earned a Michelin Bib Gourmand and a Netflix spotlight, and the bowls live up to it. Start with the kimchi butter wings, then the Richie Rich ramen, and give yourself over to the broth. The room is compact, the line is real, and first come, first served keeps the energy honest. It is worth the wait for ramen drinkers who plan around a bowl, especially on a first visit to Audubon Park.

East End Market

Markets & shops · $

3201 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Forget the food court format you know, because Audubon Park built a maker hall instead. East End Market combines a market hall, incubator kitchens and an event space under one roof. Line up for the well known Gideon's Bakehouse cookies, tear into a loaf from Olde Hearth Bread, sip Lineage Coffee and wander past La Femme du Fromage and Domu. Every corner belongs to a local maker with something to prove. Come hungry and leave carrying somebody's craft in a bag.

Foreigner Restaurant

Fine dining & steak · $$$$

Foreigner Restaurant, 2816 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-2226

Chef Bruno Fonseca's Brazilian roots run through this Corrine Drive counter like a current. Foreigner is a chef's-counter tasting room serving a menu confiance, a monthly-changing parade of globally inspired courses, where poached oysters meet caviar and brown butter powder, mackerel gets the moqueca treatment, and picanha lands with bordelaise and morels. It began as intimate popups, and the intimacy survived the move to a permanent room: Chef Bruno introduces each course personally, the wine pairings are chosen with real care, and you leave feeling like a houseguest who was fussed over beautifully.

Hinckley's Fancy Meats

Markets & shops · $$

3201 Corrine Dr #104, Orlando, FL 32803

A butcher shop inside East End Market treats meat as a craft with receipts. Chef Matt Hinckley cures, smokes and ages at this Audubon Park counter, where the charcuterie earned a nod from the New York Times and the bacon was called the best in Florida. Sandwiches put the work between bread: rare Florida porchetta with salsa piccante and broccoli rabe, proper pastrami on rye and a banh mi with nerve. Sourcing stays transparent and nose to tail. Carnivores, consider this your Audubon Park cathedral.

Kelly's Homemade Ice Cream

Sweets · $

3114 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL

Orlando-made and proud of it, Kelly's churns small-batch ice cream across a string of scoop shops. Monthly flavors read like a dessert diary: bear claw, fluffernutter, hummingbird cake, and toasted s'mores. Clear labeling covers allergies, non-dairy flavors rotate in, and Dog Pops serve four-legged regulars. The Fern Creek shop runs patio karaoke nights, which tells you plenty about the vibe. Warm and playful, the shops make leaving with one scoop difficult. Choose Kelly's when local ice cream should feel personal, changing, and neighborhood-rooted.

Lineage Coffee Roasting

Coffee & cafes · $$

Lineage Coffee Roasting, 3201 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-2224, United States

A retrofitted grill and an ice cream motor make an unlikely start for a roastery that now teaches the craft. In 2012, husband and wife Jarrett and Justine started roasting in Orlando, and by their own admission the early coffee was terrible. Lineage now roasts small, hand picked lots, cups and dials in every coffee, and teaches you through the Lineage Academy while you drink. Three locations stretch from East End Market to Mills 50 across the city. Choose Lineage when you want a cup that tastes like people who never stopped learning.

Sushi Lola's

Japanese & sushi · $$

Sushi Lola's, 2902 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803, United States

Sushi Lola's runs on a delicious split personality. Owner Aaron Pak has kept two kitchens humming on Corrine Drive in Audubon Park for over a decade: one for sushi, one for Korean, so your table can hold a Playboy roll and a sizzling dolsot bibimbap at once. The Lava Dips are a cult appetizer, the Volcano roll lives up to its name, and dessert arrives as a warm browned butter cookie, on the house. It is the rare fusion spot where both halves of the hyphen earn their keep.

Winter Park Biscuit Company

Breakfast & brunch · $$

Winter Park Biscuit Company, 3201 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-2224, United States

Everything vegan and nobody believing it is the dare running this East End Market counter. Winter Park Biscuit Company serves oat buttermilk biscuits that come out flaky and golden and disappear in minutes. The crispy chick sandwich has converted more skeptics than a sermon, and biscuits and gravy land rich and peppery. Hibiscus lemonade cuts through the comfort with bright acidity at the table. Choose it when you want Southern comfort food that never announces its virtue, because the biscuit, gravy, and sandwich make the argument on flavor alone.
