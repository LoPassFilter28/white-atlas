Buster's Sanford

Bars & lounges · $$

300 S Sanford Ave, Sanford, FL 32771

Sanford keeps a little corner of Belgium on its avenue. Buster's Bistro is a Belgian beer hall at heart, with rows of Trappist ales and lambics poured in proper glassware by people who know the difference. Food follows the same logic: a croque monsieur with smoked ham and gouda, curried chicken on warm baguette, and frites cut thick and fried to a crunch. Live music spills onto Sanford Avenue on weekends. Settle in, order the frites, and let Belgium handle the rest of your evening.

Dali's Lao Thai Cafe

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Dali's Lao Thai Cafe, 1181 Rinehart Rd, Sanford, FL 32771-7390, United States

Mom's recipes run Dali's, and you can taste the family in every bite. This small Sanford spot serves Lao and Thai cooking built on fresh ingredients and generous portions, from homemade sausage with sticky rice and fiery jaew bong pepper paste to the red curry chicken noodle soup regulars order on repeat. The pad thai and papaya salad get raved about, the curry beef empanadas sell themselves, and takeout orders arrive with sweet handwritten notes. Come for flavor, not frills, and you will not miss the frills at all.

Donutste Family Bakery

Sweets · $$

605 Crescent Executive Ct Ste 120, Lake Mary, FL 32746

Plant-based donuts carry the argument here, no butter, milk, or eggs required. Chefs Shalom and Victoria run this family-owned Lake Mary shop, and the donuts show the care: bouncy, not-too-sweet yeast rings in ube cinnamon roll, red velvet, and strawberry shortcake. FOX News called them the best around, while regulars preorder because quantities stay small and sellouts are real. The room feels bright and welcoming. Order ahead when you can, because finishing the box is usually what convinces skeptics about the vegan recipe.

Duffy's Sports Grill

American · $

Duffy's Sports Grill, 951 Market Promenade Ave, Lake Mary, FL 32746

Screens everywhere and the game always on: Duffy's knows its assignment in Lake Mary. The Florida sports bar chain built its name on taking game day seriously, and this location adds a big, busy patio that stays active through the season. The Major League Burger and jumbo wings headline a kitchen that treats game day food with real care. Henry's Depot

Markets & shops · $$

Henry's Depot, 212 W 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771-1204, United States

Sanford's old train station traded passengers for pizza, ramen and brunch. Henry's Depot stands on the site of Florida's first railroad system, built by Henry B. Plant, and now hums as an open air food hall. Vendors cover wood fired pizza, ramen, seafood, sandwiches, ice cream from the Greenery Creamery and craft coffee from Mahogany. The old Florida themed Basin anchors the hall with craft cocktails and live music, and Sunday brunch is the local ritual. Bring the whole family and stay for the entertainment.

Hollerbach's German Market

Markets & shops · $$

117 Magnolia Ave, Sanford, FL 32771

Around the corner from the well known beer hall, the Hollerbach family runs a German market built for provisions and lunch. A full service bakery turns out Black Forest cake and apple strudel, while the delikatessen counter builds sandwiches like the Reubenstein. Shelves stock imported German goods, beer and wine from over fifty breweries and wineries, Milka chocolate, Gummi bears, Haribo, Knorr and Lowensenf mustard. Pair a pretzel with a Riesling, then take home ingredients for your own German dinner and a little Gemutlichkeit.

Hollerbach's Willow Tree Cafe

Restaurants · $$

205 E 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771

Sanford's German beer hall is a family promise kept: Theo, Linda and Christina Hollerbach opened the Willow Tree Cafe on East 1st Street in 2001, and it grew into one of the largest German dining rooms in Florida. The kitchen runs on wursts, schnitzels and potato pancakes, with eisbein, an oven-roasted pork shank, as the showpiece, and apple strudel and Black Forest cake for the finish. Live entertainment, a roof terrace and a serious German beer list keep the room loud in the best way. This is Gemutlichkeit as a working business model.

Hooked Fish Camp

Seafood · $$

550 Towne Center Cir, Sanford, FL 32771

The F&D crew brought a fish camp concept to Sanford, and the room keeps that promise. Hooked Fish Camp offers rustic cabin warmth and seafood done the relaxed Florida way. Start with clam chowder, a pile of mussels, and hush puppies, then stay for crab legs and a slab of lemon pie sized for the whole table. The operation feels new, friendly, and clear about exactly what it is. Pick it when you want Sanford seafood without formality, especially with a table that shares starters and dessert.

Hot Asian Buns

Restaurants · $$

204 S Sanford Ave, Sanford, FL

A favorite food truck finally got a roof, and Sanford gained from the move. Hot Asian Buns makes every bao from scratch, steamed until pillowy and filled across borders. Korean fried chicken brings gochujang and sesame slaw, beef bulgogi brings kimchi, and lacquered pork belly comes with hoisin and peanut powder. Loaded kimchi fries make a strong addition for the table. These are buns you may think about for days afterward. Visit when you want handheld food with careful dough, bold fillings, and a permanent address.

Lighthouse Seafood

Seafood · $$

Lighthouse Seafood, 101 N Country Club Rd, Lake Mary, FL 32746, United States

Two experiences share one Lake Mary building: a seafood market and the cafe that cooks its catch. Lighthouse stacks the market with the day's seafood, then serves steamed crab legs, grilled swordfish, stone crab, and royal reds in the cafe. Regulars rave about clam chowder and argue for the key lime pie, with a whole pie to go as the pro move. Staff stay friendly and the food stays honest. Choose it when deciding among fresh options is the hardest, happiest part of dinner.

Parva

Latin & Caribbean · $$

Parva, 220 W Lake Mary Blvd, Sanford, FL 32773-5925, United States

Colombia has a word for this kind of bakery, and the Sanford shop lives up to it. Parva bakes pan de bono, bunuelos, and pan con queso fresh through the day, cheesy and golden enough that eating just one is difficult. Cafe con leche comes the proper Colombian way, and the kitchen continues with calentados, churrasco, and cachapa pabellon at lunch. It smells like nostalgia and tastes like family to regulars. Go when a bakery visit should turn into a full, comforting meal.

Quigley Coffee

Coffee & cafes · $$

Quigley Coffee, 1730 Rinehart Rd, Sanford, FL 32771-6590, United States

Quigley started as a mobile coffee cart and grew into a Sanford coffee shop with a loyal local following, and the personality came with it. The room is playful and bright, full of plants, with a skate shop tucked in the back. The drink menu rewards curiosity: seasonal creations like the Smokey Bokey, the Florida Boi, and the Bear Hug, an all-time favorite. Grab the Jammy Sammy with its habanero bacon jam if you want breakfast done right. Service is warm, wifi is free, and the vibe says stay awhile. Support local, sip something fun.

Sullivans Public House

Bars & lounges · $$

Sullivans Public House, 116 E 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771-1325, United States

Sanford gets its slice of Dublin on 1st Street, with hand carved woodwork and dim light setting the tone. A Guinness pour that people cross town for anchors the bar, and live Irish and Scottish music fills the room on weekends. The kitchen backs up the pints with shepherd's pie, fish and chips, a meatloaf sandwich on soda bread, and scotch eggs. A whiskey list worth lingering over rounds out the pub classics. Come for music, pints, or both, and you get a proper pub night without leaving Sanford.

The Current Seafood Counter-Sanford

Seafood · $$

116 E 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771

A little piece of the Northeast landed on 1st Street in Sanford, tasting like home for cold-water coasts. The Current runs a counter-service kitchen of responsibly sourced seafood. Blackened mahi sandwiches bring real char, New England lobster rolls prompted one transplant to call them the closest thing to home, and clam chowder is made the honest way. The mood stays relaxed, neighborhood, and unfussy. Order at the counter when you want to taste the Atlantic in Central Florida, especially through mahi, chowder, and lobster eaten without ceremony.
