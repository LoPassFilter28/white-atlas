Sanford

Sanford Avenue and the brick streets around it hold a working downtown, not a theme set. Seafood leads this list, with markets and bars behind it, up in Seminole County on Lake Monroe's south shore. It is the metro's north end day trip that locals actually take.

Where to start in Sanford

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

Buster's Bistro

Bars & lounges · $$

300 S Sanford Ave, Sanford, FL 32771

Buster's Bistro on Sanford Avenue pours Belgian Trappist ales and lambics in proper glassware, with a cozy, local feel that regulars compare to a living room. Dogs are welcome, and the room stays comfy and lively without losing its neighborhood ease. Order the French dip, truffle frites, and poutine, or settle into shepherd's pie, croque monsieur, and short rib carbonnade.

Dali's Lao Thai Cafe

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Dali's Lao Thai Cafe, 1181 Rinehart Rd, Sanford, FL 32771-7390, United States

Family run Dali's Lao Thai Cafe on Rinehart Road in Sanford cooks Lao and Thai food with care and fresh ingredients. Portions run generous, and regulars come back for pad thai, papaya salad, and pad see ew with a clean, balanced heat. Try the Lao sausage with sticky rice and jaew bong, the red curry chicken noodle soup, or curry beef empanadas, and save room for mango sticky rice and purple lemonade.

Donutste Family Bakery

Sweets · $$

605 Crescent Executive Ct Ste 120, Lake Mary, FL 32746

Donutste Family Bakery in Lake Mary makes fully plant based donuts with no butter, milk, or eggs, in a bright pink room guests find cheerful and welcoming. The donuts are large and rich, in rotating seasonal flavors like ube cinnamon roll, red velvet, strawberry shortcake, and the tiramisu doughssant. Batches stay small and popular flavors sell out, so regulars preorder and get in line early.

Donuts To Go

Sweets · $

1414 W 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771

An early-morning sweet spot leads with a square donut built for sharing. Donuts To Go serves a generously sized Square Glazed Donut alongside Maple Bacon, French Toast, Almond Joy, and a flaky Cronut. Chocolate cake donuts draw fans who call them heavenly, and caramel work gets its own praise. Breakfast widens the visit: burritos, biscuits and gravy, and New York-style bagels with cream cheese. Fresh, homemade, and kid-friendly, the shop earns local loyalty. Come hungry enough to take donuts and a real breakfast seriously together.

Duffy's Sports Grill

American · $

Duffy's Sports Grill, 951 Market Promenade Ave, Lake Mary, FL 32746

Duffy's Sports Grill in Lake Mary puts TVs everywhere, so every seat feels close to the game. A busy patio and a family friendly, game day room give it the feel of a neighborhood sports bar. Henry's Depot

Markets & shops · $$

Henry's Depot, 212 W 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771-1204, United States

Henry's Depot turns Sanford's historic Plant depot on West 1st Street into an open air food hall with something for everyone. Order Yugiri Ramen Project's Spicy Tonkotsu, Chin's Korean corn dogs, or a Sweet and Salty chicken sandwich, then finish with Greenery Creamery ice cream. Groups spread out, compare plates, and settle into the relaxed, family friendly space while The Basin pours craft cocktails and Mahogany serves coffee.

Directions → · @henrysdepot

Hollerbach's German Market

Markets & shops · $$

117 Magnolia Ave, Sanford, FL 32771

Hollerbach's German Market on Magnolia Avenue in Sanford stocks imported German pantry goods, Milka chocolate, and Haribo gummies alongside a full deli and bakery. Guests call it an underrated gem with a festive, laid back feel, and the Reubenstein with red cabbage earns repeat orders. Add a warm pretzel with spicy mustard, apple strudel or Black Forest cake, and a Speculoos Cookie Butter Coffee that guests say tastes like heaven and fall in a cup.

Directions → · @hollerbachs_german_market

Hollerbach's Willow Tree Cafe

Restaurants · $$

205 E 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771

Hollerbach's Willow Tree Cafe has anchored East 1st Street in Sanford since 2001, a festive, communal German dining room with live entertainment and a roof terrace. The kitchen leans on schnitzel, wurst, and potato pancakes, with pretzel and beer cheese, eisbein pork shank, apple strudel, and Black Forest cake rounding out the table. Guests call it worth the drive, and the extensive gluten free menu gets specific praise from diners who need it.

Directions → · @hollerbachs_german_restaurant

Hooked Fish Camp

Seafood · $$$$

550 Towne Center Cir, Sanford, FL 32771

Hooked Fish Camp brings relaxed, coastal fish camp cooking to Towne Center Circle in Sanford. Guests describe everything as fresh and prepared perfectly, from sweet heat shrimp and Old Bay peel and eat shrimp to deviled eggs and shrimp linguine. Finish with blackened Faroe salmon, crab legs, or a slice of lemon pie in a friendly room that keeps the mood easy.

Directions → · @hookedfishcamp

Hot Asian Buns

Restaurants · $$

204 S Sanford Ave, Sanford, FL

Hot Asian Buns grew from a Sanford food truck into a brick and mortar shop on South Sanford Avenue, where every bao is handmade from scratch. The steamed buns come out pillowy and tender, filled with Korean fried chicken, beef bulgogi, or lacquered pork belly. Guests call them melt in your mouth good, and the loaded kimchi fries and double fried chicken wings round out the table.

Lighthouse Seafood

Seafood · $$

Lighthouse Seafood, 101 N Country Club Rd, Lake Mary, FL 32746, United States

Half market, half cafe on Country Club Road in Lake Mary, Lighthouse lets you buy fish to take home or sit down while the kitchen cooks it fresh. Guests come back for crab cakes with a crisp crust and flaky center, royal red shrimp, clam chowder, and Mom Evelyn's key lime pie to finish. The room stays casual and quaint, family run and honest in the way regulars describe as real seafood cooked for us.

Parva

Latin & Caribbean · $$

Parva, 220 W Lake Mary Blvd, Sanford, FL 32773-5925, United States

Parva is a warm, homey Colombian bakery and cafe on West Lake Mary Boulevard in Sanford, where the pastry case leads the visit. Pan de bono, buñuelos, pan con queso, and pastel de yuca come out golden and generously sized, with a house cilantro sauce regulars rave about. Stay for bandeja paisa, calentado, churrasco, or cachapa, and finish with toasted coconut flan or tres leches.

Quigley Coffee

Coffee & cafes · $$

Quigley Coffee, 1730 Rinehart Rd, Sanford, FL 32771-6590, United States

A playful, bright Sanford coffee shop on Rinehart Road that started as a cart, Quigley roasts its own beans and shares the space with a skate shop in the back. Guests praise cups that are consistently amazing and single out the matcha, alongside named lattes like the Bear Hug, the Florida Boi candied orange latte, and the Smokey Bokey. The Jammy Sammy with habanero bacon jam is the food order regulars point to, sweet heat and all.

The Current Seafood Counter-Sanford

Seafood · $$

116 E 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771

The Current Seafood Counter started inside Henry's Depot and now serves a casual slice of New England on East 1st Street in Sanford. Guests praise consistently high quality food and friendly service, with blackened mahi and salmon sandwiches leading the counter. Try both lobster roll styles, New England and Connecticut, plus clam chowder, a grouper sandwich, and the fabulous hush puppies guests name again and again.

The Sullivan Public House

Bars & lounges · $$

The Sullivan Public House, 116 E 1st St, Sanford, FL 32771-1325, United States

The Sullivan Public House fills the historic Imperial building on East 1st Street in Sanford with hand carved wood, a copper ceiling, and dim, welcoming light. Guests come for what they call the best Guinness pour in all of Sanford, cold pints, and a deep Irish whiskey selection. Order shepherd's pie, fish and chips, scotch eggs, or the meatloaf sandwich on house made soda bread, with potato and garlic soup to start.
