Kelly Park / Rock Springs Run

Parks & outdoors · $

400 E Kelly Park Rd, Apopka, FL 32712

A short drive out, Florida hands you its finest natural water park at Kelly Park. Rock Springs pours out of the earth in a clear channel that looks lit from within, and the tubing run downstream is among the laziest half hours you can spend in Central Florida. Dr. Howard Kelly, a Johns Hopkins founder, gave this land to the people of Apopka in 1927, and it still feels like a gift. Paddle the Emerald Cut for jungle-walled seclusion, watch for turtles and otters, then hike. On a hot day, little else competes.
