Sourdough Bread House, 5761 US-17-92, Casselberry, FL 32707-3819, United States

Apopka

Kelly Park / Rock Springs Run

Parks & outdoors · $

400 E Kelly Park Rd, Apopka, FL 32712

The spring run is the draw at Kelly Park: clear, cold water pouring out of Rock Springs into a lazy channel made for tubing, with the Emerald Cut paddle offering a quieter, jungle-walled stretch. Guests call it a chill, shaded piece of old Florida, well kept and easy to love, with turtles and otters along the banks. The catch is popularity: on warm weekends the park fills to capacity early, and regulars line up well ahead of the gate.

Soulicious Vegan Kitchen, 15 E 5th St, Apopka, FL 32703-5244, United States

Wekiwa Springs State Park

Parks & outdoors · $

1800 Wekiwa Cir, Apopka, FL 32712

Wekiwa Springs is the old-Florida counterpoint to the theme parks: an emerald spring in Apopka feeding the Wekiva River, with clear, cold water guests call magical to swim over. Canoes and kayaks drift past turtles and the occasional manatee, and trails run through dense tropical hammock. On popular days the park reaches capacity and turns cars away, so the day-pass reservation is worth sorting out before the drive.
