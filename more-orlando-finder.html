Orlando

Agave Azul Cocina Mexicana

Mexican · $$

Agave Azul Cocina Mexicana, 5475 Gateway Village Cir, Orlando, FL 32812-4002, United States

A loud, festive, high-energy Mexican dining room at Gateway Village near the airport, immaculate and packed with a lot going on. Birria tacos, the Oaxaca Lava molcajete, smoky churrasco, and queso fundido with chorizo carry the kitchen, and the Night in Tulum margarita carries the bar. Portions run big, the kind of plates guests end up taking home.

Aloha Hawaiian Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

Aloha Hawaiian Kitchen, 6125 S Semoran Blvd, Orlando, FL 32822-1500

A laid-back island counter near the airport serving the plate lunch guests call the best Hawaiian food on the mainland. The Aloha Special stacks crispy pork belly, ono shrimp, and mochiko chicken over rice, with huli huli chicken, loco moco, spam musubi, and a mac salad guests single out. Save room for Alohasadas, filled doughnuts in ube, haupia, and pandan.

Directions → · @alohahawaiiankitchen

Amar Orlando

Mediterranean · $$

8001 S Orange Blossom Trl, Ste #1510, Orlando, FL 32809

Amar Orlando serves Lebanese cooking in a calm, intimate room just outside the Florida Mall food court, a step up in polish from its surroundings. Guests rave about the hummus, calling it the best they have had, and the chicken shawarma and mixed grill platter carry that same care. Portions run modest, so order a spread and let the Taste of Lebanon platter do the touring.

Amazonica

Coffee & cafes · $$

8001 S Orange Blossom Trl Kiosk TT124, Orlando, FL 32809

A bright Colombian kiosk inside The Florida Mall turning out obleas layered with arequipe, acai bowls with Nutella and fresh fruit, and a proper cortadito. Guests praise the friendly counter staff and call the arepas exquisite, with cholados and Dubai chocolate for the sweet finish. Bar One Orlando

Bars & lounges · $

Bar One Orlando, 1516 McCoy Rd, Orlando, FL 32809

Bar One looks tiny from the street, but inside it is a cozy, very clean neighborhood bar on McCoy Road with a pool table and dart boards. The patio is the charmer, with picnic tables, cornhole, giant Jenga and a warm fire pit under the string lights. Order the wings (guests swear you will order another round), the Venezuelan empanadas and tequenos, and expect bartenders who remember you and your drink.

Beef Bae

BBQ · $$

10249 S John Young Pkwy Ste 109, Orlando, FL 32837

Beef Bae, the South Orlando spot formerly known as Charcoal Zyka, runs American barbecue through a Zabiha halal kitchen, and regulars say the pairing works. Guests praise the beef short ribs for their tenderness, the ribs fall off the bone, and the Portuguese chicken has been the hallmark since the start. An Inferno Burger brings the heat, and a mango lassi cools it back down.

Bombay Street Kitchen

Indian · $$

6215 S Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando

Bombay Street Kitchen trades the standard curry-house script for the street carts of Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai and Bangalore, a bright, buzzy room on Orange Blossom Trail with a Michelin Bib Gourmand to its name. Guests work through pani puri, crispy kurkuri bhindi bhel and a gunpowder ghee dosa with perfectly spiced potatoes, then finish with Bombay falooda or rasmalai. The cooking is bold and fresh enough that guests say it takes them back to India, and that one visit turns into a craving for the next.

Directions → · @bombaystreetkitchenorlando

Bonefish Grill

Seafood · $

Bonefish Grill, 5463 Gateway Village Cir, Orlando, FL 32812

This Bonefish Grill sits in Lee Vista off Gateway Village Circle, polished and reliable in the way the chain built its name on: wood-grilled seafood and a room that works for date night. Bang Bang Shrimp starts nearly every table, the Chilean sea bass and parmesan-crusted rainbow trout carry the mains, and a dirty martini from the bar finishes the setup. Guests reach for words like superb and never disappoints, and birthday tables land here often.

Brazi Burger

5829 Old Winter Garden Rd, Orlando, FL 32835

Brazilian street food from the Brazi Burger food truck on Old Winter Garden Road: pastel de feira fried crisp and golden, pressed hot dogs, and the X-Tudo, a burger stacked with egg, ham, bacon, corn and shoestring potatoes. Guests say the flavors taste like the ones back in Brazil, in combinations they would never have put together. Acai bowls cool things down, and the whole setup stays casual, generous and unpretentious.

Caravan Uzbek & Turkish Cuisine

El Cilantrillo

Latin & Caribbean · $$

El Cilantrillo Restaurant, 1301 Florida Mall Ave, Orlando, FL 32809-7731

El Cilantrillo is South Orlando's big Puerto Rican table, a lively, salsa-filled room near the Florida Mall where the portions come generous and the cooking tastes made with love. Guests come for mofongo relleno, a massive chuleta kan kan, arroz con gandules and tostones rellenos, with coconut mojitos that regulars rave about. Diners born on the island call it true Puerto Rican sazón, the kind of food that takes you straight back. When the room fills, the kitchen takes its time, so order a mojito and settle in.

8421 S Orange Blossom Trl Ste 156, Orlando, FL 32809

El Paraíso Del Marisco cooks the Ecuadorian and Peruvian seafood that Orlando's Latin families describe as tasting like home. Happy Snapper Seafood Restaurant

A tiny, family-run seafood joint on South Orange Avenue that guests keep calling a hole-in-the-wall gem. 4645 S Semoran Blvd, Orlando, FL 32822

Enzo's On the Lake

Enzo's on the Lake, 1130 S US Highway 17 92, Longwood, FL 32750-5718

On the shore of Lake Kathryn in Longwood, Enzo's has been the special-occasion Italian since 1980, an old-world room guests compare to eating in an Italian villa, with the deck sitting right at the water for sunset. Anniversaries land here by habit, and guests say it never disappoints. On busy nights the dining room can get loud, with tables close together.
