Top Picks

Where to start in Other Orlando Areas

Start with these three, then work down the full list below, grouped by town and neighborhood.

Agave Azul Cocina Mexicana

Mexican · $$

Agave Azul Cocina Mexicana, 5475 Gateway Village Cir, Orlando, FL 32812-4002, United States

A loud, festive, high-energy Mexican dining room at Gateway Village near the airport, immaculate and packed with a lot going on. Birria tacos, the Oaxaca Lava molcajete, smoky churrasco, and queso fundido with chorizo carry the kitchen, and the Night in Tulum margarita carries the bar. Portions run big, the kind of plates guests end up taking home.

Directions → · @agaveazulcocinamex

Aloha Hawaiian Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

Aloha Hawaiian Kitchen, 6125 S Semoran Blvd, Orlando, FL 32822-1500

A laid-back island counter near the airport serving the plate lunch guests call the best Hawaiian food on the mainland. The Aloha Special stacks crispy pork belly, ono shrimp, and mochiko chicken over rice, with huli huli chicken, loco moco, spam musubi, and a mac salad guests single out. Save room for Alohasadas, filled doughnuts in ube, haupia, and pandan.

Directions → · @alohahawaiiankitchen

Bonefish Grill

Seafood · $

Bonefish Grill, 5463 Gateway Village Cir, Orlando, FL 32812

This Bonefish Grill sits in Lee Vista off Gateway Village Circle, polished and reliable in the way the chain built its name on: wood-grilled seafood and a room that works for date night. Bang Bang Shrimp starts nearly every table, the Chilean sea bass and parmesan-crusted rainbow trout carry the mains, and a dirty martini from the bar finishes the setup. Guests reach for words like superb and never disappoints, and birthday tables land here often.

Brazi Burger

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

5829 Old Winter Garden Rd, Orlando, FL 32835

Brazilian street food from the Brazi Burger food truck on Old Winter Garden Road: pastel de feira fried crisp and golden, pressed hot dogs, and the X-Tudo, a burger stacked with egg, ham, bacon, corn and shoestring potatoes. Guests say the flavors taste like the ones back in Brazil, in combinations they would never have put together. Acai bowls cool things down, and the whole setup stays casual, generous and unpretentious.

Directions → · @braziburger_orlando

High Tide Harry's

Seafood · $$

4645 S Semoran Blvd, Orlando, FL 32822

High Tide Harry's is the airport corridor's classic local seafood joint, no-frills and nautical, family owned and easygoing about everything except the shellfish. The Captain's Seafood Combo anchors the table, the Blue Stew has its own devoted following, and crab legs and charbroiled oysters keep the crackers busy. It is generous, fun, and proudly old-school, the kind of place locals bring out-of-towners to make a point.

Directions → · @hightideharrys

Matt's Latin BBQ

BBQ · $$

Matt's Latin BBQ, 6607 S Semoran Blvd, Orlando, FL 32822

Puerto Rican comfort cooking on South Semoran, cafeteria-style and unpretentious, with decor guests say makes you feel like you are on the island. The mofongo con carne frita is the plate people rave about, next to pernil with rice and beans, juicy rotisserie chicken, morcilla, and a tripleta sandwich. Portions are shareable, the kind of spread guests describe as enough food for days.

Directions → · @mattslatinbbq

Mikado

Japanese & sushi · $$

Mikado, 6417 Raleigh St, Orlando, FL 32835-5739, United States

An unassuming Raleigh Street room in Metrowest that guests call a little gem, and the sashimi is why. Cuts arrive large, tender, and ultra fresh, from toro and yellowtail to Scottish salmon and unagi, with the omakase, the Mikado Roll, and buttery uni toast as the standing orders. Cozy and old-school, it is the quiet room locals name when only real sushi will do.

Directions → · @mikadosushimetrowest

Pine & Oak Tavern

American · $$

8600 El Prado Ave, Orlando, FL 32825

A clubby tavern inside the revived Rio Pinar golf course, with fairways beyond the patio and a relaxing, neighborhood feel. Guests come for the prime rib and the coffee-rubbed NY strip, plus the Pine & Oak burger and bartenders who remember your drink. Live music fills the room on some nights; when events pack it in, it gets loud and service stretches, so a quiet patio table is the sleeper pick.

Directions →

Sunshine Soulfood Cafe

Restaurants · $$

Sunshine Soulfood Cafe, 2528 W Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32804-8009, United States

Soul food with a Caribbean lean on West Colonial Drive, cooked like somebody's home kitchen: oxtails that fall off the bone, creamy mac and cheese, fried chicken, and soft cornbread on the side. Guests use words like homey and no-fuss, and more than one says the plate reminded them of home. Service can be uneven, but the portions are generous and the pineapple lemonade is worth adding.

Directions → · @sunshinesfood

Teak Neighborhood Grill

Restaurants · $$

Teak Neighborhood Grill, 6400 Time Square Ave, Orlando, FL 32835-6453

Teak is MetroWest's tucked-away neighborhood grill, chill and laid-back, with a burger menu deep enough to keep regulars rotating through it. The Glazed Donut Burger and Kobe Burger get the headlines, but insiders point to the loaded pierogies and the soft pretzel with beer cheese, which one guest called perfection. Dogs are welcome on the patio, the staff reads like family to the regulars, and when the room fills the kitchen can take its time, so settle in.

Directions → · @teakneighborhoodgrill

Zorba's Kitchen

Mediterranean · $$

Zorba's Kitchen, 7325 Lake Underhill Rd, Orlando, FL 32822-6061, United States

A small, family-run Greek kitchen on Lake Underhill Road where, as one guest put it, the chef cooks like someone's Greek grandma. Everything is made fresh from scratch: lamb gyros on soft pita, the skepasti stacked with lamb, peppers, fries and feta, moussaka, spanakopita, and feta fries on the side. The dining room is intimate and welcoming, the kind of place that makes regulars feel at home.

Directions → · @zorbas_orlando

Apopka

Soulicious Vegan Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

Soulicious Vegan Kitchen, 15 E 5th St, Apopka, FL 32703-5244, United States

Inside Apopka's Halls On 5th food hall, Soulicious does vegan soul food with no interest in compromise: fried oyster mushroom chick'n seasoned to perfection, chick'n sliders, and mac and cheese that holds its own. Guests talk about mushrooms that tear apart in your mouth and an Oreo cheesecake one diner called the best they had ever had. Portions run modest for the price, but the seasoning is the point, and skeptics at the table tend to convert.
