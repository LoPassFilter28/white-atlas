Antonio's

Italian · $$$

611 S Orlando Ave, Maitland, FL 32751

Upstairs at Antonio's is the special-occasion dining room Maitland locals name first: white tablecloths, a view over Lake Lily, and wood-fire cooking throughout. The gorgonzola-walnut pasta is the dish people cross town for, while lobster ravioli and oak-grilled salmon round out a menu backed by a respected Central Florida wine list. The room dresses smart casual and leans romantic, with the lake doing quiet work beyond the windows. You choose it for anniversaries and milestone dinners, and it is worth the trip north of Winter Park.

Antonio's Market & Cafe

Italian · $$

611 S Orlando Ave, Maitland, FL 32751

Downstairs at Antonio's is the casual half of the operation: a cafe and boutique Italian market under the same roof as the upstairs dining room. Grab the award-winning pizza, a chicken parm sandwich with pasta salad, or rotisserie chicken for an easy Maitland lunch or dinner. Then browse imported cold cuts, alpine cheeses, and wines you can cork at your table, turning a meal into a small shopping trip. Same building as Antonio's restaurant, far more relaxed in pace and dress. You come hungry and leave with provisions.

Bagel King Bakery

Bakeries · $$

Bagel King Bakery, 668 Kennedy Blvd W, Orlando, FL 32810-6255, United States

Your favorite breakfast sandwich may have started as a bagel from this Orlando plant. Bagel King bakes more than twenty types of bagels every day, plus homemade danish, muffins, croissants, and specialty breads that supply delis and cafes across Central Florida. The wholesale operation keeps family roots, and the chew and shine on the bagels suggest people who care about the details. Remember the name when a cafe bagel impresses you, because there is a good chance it began here.

Boku Sushi

Japanese & sushi · $$

Boku Sushi, 140 Independence Ln, Maitland, FL 32751-5603, United States

Tucked into the corner of its Maitland complex behind the Starbucks, Boku is the kind of neighborhood sushi spot that makes regulars out of first-timers. The room glows with warm wood, tile and stone, and the rolls have cult followings: the Drama Queen, the Foxy Lady, the Out of Control, and a jalapeño hamachi one reviewer swore was the best version he had ever tasted. Sit out on the pet-friendly patio when the evening cools, and ask about the Sea Bass.

Francesco's Ristorante

Italian · $$

Francesco's Ristorante, 400 S Orlando Ave, Maitland, FL 32751

Palermo-born chef Francesco Aiello trained at Sicily's prestigious Istituto Alberghiero, opened his Maitland namesake in 2012, and just swept five awards, including Best Italian in Florida and Best Local Chef. The proof is in the open flame: wood-burning ovens blister hand-tossed Margheritas, pastas are rolled by hand, mozzarella is made in-house, and the kitchen turns out gnocchi Siciliana, spaghetti alle vongole, and a tableside Pinwheel Reggiano Flambéed Pappardella that is pure theater. Chef Francesco plates in the open kitchen and works the room like family. This is Maitland's pride, and it eats like it.

Jazz Tastings

Nightlife & music · $$

164 Lake Ave, Maitland, FL 32751

Maitland's grown-folks jazz club pairs live music five nights a week with food that punches above typical club fare. Norwegian salmon, shrimp and grits and a bread pudding people write home about arrive while the house band runs through soul, jazz and funk. The room is small, reservations are strongly recommended, and the supper-club format means dinner and the show share the same table. For a date night in Maitland that feels settled and musical rather than loud, Jazz Tastings is the reliable pick.

Lim Ros Thai Restaurant

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Lim Ros Thai Restaurant, 933 Orlando Ave S, Maitland, FL 32751

Lim Ros means "to taste" in Thai, and this modern Maitland dining room makes sure you do plenty of it. Crying tiger steak earns its name, kanom jeen namya pu brings a house curry brimming with real crabmeat, and tom kha gai arrives as a hot pot. The panang curry draws regular praise, and a full gluten-free and vegan menu widens the table. Butterfly pea tea cocktails and upscale but unpretentious service complete the picture. Pick it for a Maitland dinner where dietary needs and big flavor both matter.

Lukes Kitchen and Bar

American · $$$

Luke's Kitchen and Bar, 640 Orlando Ave S, Maitland, FL 32751-5612

Maitland gets its everyday celebration here. From the team behind Winter Park's Luma on Park and Prato, Luke's is chef Brandon McGlamery's love letter to classic American cooking with a playful brain. The blackened fish collar and Cheshire pork chop over goat cheese boursin grits are the signatures, PB&J spare ribs and a towering shellfish starter keep the adventurous happy, and locals insist the patio is the finest in Maitland. Craft cocktails use house made ingredients. It earns its regulars, and it will likely earn you.

Maitland Chocolate Factory

Sweets · $$

445 S Orlando Ave, Maitland, FL 32751

A working chocolate shop beats a gift store that happens to sell candy. Maitland Chocolate Factory makes handmade truffles, dipped strawberries, and fudge in the back each morning, then fills the front case with them. Roughly a thousand themed molds let the shop turn chocolate into almost anything for a birthday, wedding, or ordinary Tuesday. The Dubai chocolate bar is the current craze, but classics keep Maitland returning. Come when you want chocolate made on site, shaped imaginatively, and grounded in everyday local habit.

Maitland Social

Markets & shops · $$

Maitland Social, 360 E Horatio Ave, Maitland, FL 32751-4517, United States

Part food hall, part neighborhood living room, Maitland Social gives Maitland a place to gather. Pizza Bruno's wood fired pies from Bruno Zacchini lead the vendor lineup, joined by Wave Sushi's Asian fusion creations like the sushi crunch wrap supreme, Foxtail Coffee and a Thrive cocktail lounge. A fitness center sits tucked in for balance, while indoor outdoor spaces fill with families, friends and regulars. Come for one stall and stay, because the whole corridor keeps pulling you toward another stop.

Permanent Vacation

Bars & lounges · $$$

Permanent Vacation, 106 Lake Ave, Maitland, FL 32751-6424

An Employees Only door inside a Maitland sports bar opens onto a different state of mind. Permanent Vacation is a tiki speakeasy hidden inside the Copper Rocket, committed to skulls and bamboo, fish floats in netting overhead, and fog rolling through the dark. Your bartender, billed as a travel agent, hands you a rum cocktail and a tab in a passport. The Pearl Necklace is the drink people talk about most. Reserve ahead, because regulars treat a return visit like checking in on old friends.
