Rusteak Local Pick

American · $$

101 S Eola Dr, Suite 103, Orlando, FL 32801

On the Lake Eola corner in Thornton Park, RusTeak cooks like a neighborhood spot with something to prove: elevated but relaxed, polished but never stiff. The New American menu runs from honey-sage pecan chicken and a pepper-crusted NY strip to steak frites and the crab cake benedict regulars order on repeat. The patio hums, the smoked watermelon margarita has its fans, and the espresso martini gets called the best some guests have had. When the room fills it gets loud and service stretches, so the early seating is the relaxed version.

Cavo's Bar & Kitchen

Bars & lounges · $$

Cavo's Bar & Kitchen, 900 E Washington St, Orlando, FL 32801-2906, United States

Cavo's Bar and Kitchen on East Washington is Eagles country, the Thornton Park sports bar where Philly fans gather and the cheesesteak earns loud praise. Regulars call the Philly one of the best they have ever had, stacked with shaved steak and molten cheese, while roast pork sandwiches, arancini, and French onion soup give the menu range beyond game day. Lively without tipping into chaos, it is a relaxed room for a long afternoon of food and drinks.

Directions → · @cavos_bar

F&D Cantina

Mexican · $$

617 E Central Blvd, Orlando, FL 32801

F&D Cantina on East Central is Thornton Park's festive cantina, a dog friendly patio room where regulars dream about the birria tacos and the watermelon margarita. The shrimp ceviche and bacon wrapped jalapenos join a menu that mixes light, crispy bites with bigger comfort plates. On busy nights service can run slow, the honest price of a room this popular.

Jinya Ramen Bar

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Jinya Ramen Bar, 8 N Summerlin Ave, Orlando, FL 32801-2930, United States

Jinya Ramen Bar on North Summerlin keeps the lights dim and the bowls deep, with broth simmered the slow way and a sleek room that fills up fast. The Tonkotsu Black, loaded with garlic chips and garlic oil, is the purist's pick, while the spicy creamy vegan ramen converts skeptics and the JINYA Bun brings slow braised pork chashu in a steamed bun. Guests rave about the broth like it is addictive, so come hungry and plan to stay a while.

Orlando Farmers Market

Markets & shops · $

20 N Eola Dr, Orlando, FL 32801

Every Sunday, the Orlando Farmers Market turns the Lake Eola lakefront into a ritual, a shaded, dog friendly stretch of vendors with live music and a wine and beer garden. Produce stalls, artisan goods, paella, acai bowls, and drinkable coconuts share space in what guests call a foodie's dream of a half day. Parking near the lake can be a hunt on market days, so plan for that and linger.

Directions → · @orlandofarmersmarket

Osteria Ester

Italian · $$

629 E Central Blvd, Orlando, FL

Osteria Ester brings the Good Salt group's touch to Italian American cooking in the old Soco space on East Central, a warm room that reads as a neighborhood red sauce joint. Guests say the house focaccia is out of this world and the lasagna comes in generous portions built for sharing, with a tableside flambeed cheese that diners keep raving about. Cozy and unpretentious, it works for date nights and family tables alike.

Directions → · @osteria_ester

Outpost Neighborhood Tavern

American · $$

227 N Eola Dr, Orlando, FL 32801

A welcoming room two blocks off Lake Eola that guests describe as a cross between a sports bar and a boutique restaurant, with a lively patio and cornhole and trivia leagues through the week. The Southerner burger and cubano burger anchor the Southern comfort menu, joined by krispy corn ribs and a frozen sangria worth ordering. Staff treat regulars like family.

Directions → · @outpostorl

Red Panda Noodle

Noodles & Southeast Asian · $$

Red Panda Noodle, 274 N Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32801, United States

A chef-driven noodle spot at downtown's A La Cart food hall on N Orange Ave, where hand-pulled biang biang noodles arrive thick with a firm bite. The spicy chicken katsu and bourbon-spiked chicken noodles hit hard, and guests warn that when the menu says spicy, it really means it. The Goomba goes the vegan route with mushrooms, mustard greens and black vinegar.

Directions → · @redpandanoodle

The Escobar Kitchen

Japanese & sushi · $$

The Escobar Kitchen, 420 E Church St, Orlando, FL 32801-2775

The Escobar Kitchen is a family run sushi counter inside the Wine and Corks storefront on East Church Street, building Latin flavored rolls that regulars say they dream about. The Escobar Roll stacks shrimp tempura, churrasco, and sweet plantains, and the Paisa Roll earns the same devotion from repeat visitors. Small, chill, and full of bold flavor, it is a neighborhood staple for anyone who wants sushi with a twist.

The Stubborn Mule

American · $$

100 S Eola Dr #103, Orlando, FL 32801

The Stubborn Mule sits steps from the swans of Lake Eola with Moscow mules as its calling card and a cozy, lively room that regulars call simply a vibe. The Stubborn Mule Burger and the Belgian waffle burger carry the menu, and one regular calls the smash burger the best they have had in a long while, with sticky pork belly and parmesan rosemary fries earning their own loyalists. Service can be hit or miss on crowded nights, but the food and the lakefront stroll after keep people coming back.

Directions → · @thestubbornmuleorlando

Wine & Corks

Bars & lounges · $$

420 E Church St Ste 108, Orlando, FL 32801

Wine and Corks is a locally owned bottle shop on East Church Street that also runs a real sushi kitchen inside the same four walls, an unusual downtown pairing that works. The staff loves talking bottles, from crisp whites to full bodied reds, and the self serve tasting taps turn browsing into an experience. Add a salmon tempura roll or a prosecco popsicle and it becomes an easy, unhurried evening.
