Kissimmee

192, the old Irlo Bronson strip, carries most of this list. Pizza, diners and roadside attractions run alongside a few genuine destination tables like Salt and Cellar. It is the theme parks' older and cheaper front yard, and it rewards drivers willing to slow down.

Where to start in Kissimmee

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

1881 Restaurant & Bar

Mediterranean · $$

1881 Restaurant, 308 Broadway, Kissimmee, FL 34741-5718

Inside downtown Kissimmee's historic Makinson Hardware building, 1881 Restaurant & Bar pairs old-Florida bones with a lively bar and grill energy, and guests say the walls themselves tell the story of the place. The menu runs broader than the building suggests: a Rodeo Burger, mango habanero wings and loaded mac and cheese with pulled pork beside Uzbek touches like fried chuchvara and a mixed grill. Settle onto the patio with a drink, catch the game, and expect a room that leans as much bar as restaurant.

Directions → · @1881.kissimmee

Boggy Creek Airboat Adventures

Attractions · $$

2001 E Southport Rd, Kissimmee, FL 34746

Boggy Creek Airboat Adventures runs airboats out onto East Lake Tohopekaliga from Southport Road, skimming open water then slowing into the marsh grass where captains spot gators, turtles and wading birds. Guests call it a highlight of their Florida vacation, and the night tours, with spotlights sweeping for glowing eyes, are the ones people talk about for years. Back on shore there is gem mining, a butterfly garden and a Native American village to wander.

Brazi Burger

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

5405 W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee, FL 34746

Brazi Burger's Kissimmee spot is a food truck at Food Trucks Heaven on 192, casual and unpretentious, serving Brazilian-style burgers guests say taste like the ones back in Brazil. The X-Tudo and X-Brazi lead the menu, with a picanha sandwich, pastel and a catupiry hot dog for variety, plus an acai bowl and Guarana Antarctica to drink. Portions are generous and everything is made to order.

Celebration Town Tavern

American · $$

721 Front St, Celebration, FL 34747

Celebration Town Tavern brings a cozy, quaint slice of New England to Front Street, right across from the lake. Guests swear by the clam chowder, calling it some of the best they have ever had, alongside a Maine lobster roll, fried clams, steamers and fish and chips. Finish with Boston cream pie that regulars call wicked good, and the whole room feels like a little trip to Boston.

Columbia Restaurant

Latin & Caribbean · $$

649 Front St, Celebration, FL 34747

The Columbia in Celebration brings a piece of old Florida to Front Street, an old-world, lakeside room of hand-painted tiles and courtyard style, sister to the Ybor City original founded in 1905. The Original 1905 Salad is prepared tableside and guests call it to die for, the sangria is mixed at the table too, and the Paella a la Valenciana anchors the menu alongside empanadas and roast pork. Finish with the white chocolate bread pudding in a room that stays festive and lively.

Green Meadows Petting Farm

Attractions · $$

1881 Jack Calhoun Dr, Kissimmee, FL 34741

Green Meadows Petting Farm spreads out under the live oaks at Lancaster Ranch Park in Kissimmee, all open air and hands-on. Kids feed goats, alpacas, donkeys and pigs, climb into the corn pit, and ride out on a tractor-drawn wagon through Old Florida pasture. Guests call it the most screen free and special time, the kind of peaceful place where little ones just run and burn off energy.

Kamayan Grill

Restaurants · $$

Kamayan Grill, 7868 W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee, FL 34747-1738, United States

Kamayan Grill is a cozy, family-run Filipino kitchen on 192 in Kissimmee where guests say the food tastes just like home. Plates are built for sharing: the Ultimate Bilao, crispy pata, sizzling sisig, kare-kare and lumpia shanghai, with halo-halo to finish. Waits can run long when the room is busy, but regulars call the spread a real celebration on the table.

King O Falafel

Mediterranean · $$

King O Falafel, 5045 W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee, FL 34746-5345, United States

A family-owned Kissimmee storefront on 192 since 2009, King O Falafel is unassuming outside and all flavor inside, a hidden gem regulars have returned to for years. The combination platter and chicken kabob lead, with falafel sandwiches, red lentil soup and a fresh Lebanese salad, plus pistachio baklava or kinafeh to finish. Guests call it the best Mediterranean and Lebanese food in town and say it has never disappointed them.

Lazy Dog

American · $$

8019 W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee, FL 34747

Lazy Dog brings a cozy, lodge-style cabin feel to 192 in Kissimmee, relaxed and family-friendly, with a dog menu on the patio so four-legged guests get a plate too. The comfort food runs from firecracker shrimp and crispy deviled eggs to the bison burger and campfire pot roast, with mountain berry cheesecake to finish. Service can slow down when the room fills up, but regulars call it their place to go for comfort food.

Main Street Pizza

Pizza · $$

16 Broadway, Kissimmee, FL 34741

Main Street Pizza is downtown Kissimmee's old-school New York style joint, family oriented and welcoming, with slices guests swear are bigger than their face. The Monster and the Colorado pile on the toppings, the Buffalo Chicken rides a white base, and one guest says it tastes just like back home in Jersey. Save room for the nitrogen ice cream, and do not be surprised if it becomes your go-to pizza spot in Kissimmee.

Medieval Times Dinner & Tournament

Attractions · $$$

4510 W Vine St, Kissimmee, FL 34746

Medieval Times fills a castle arena on Vine Street in Kissimmee with jousting knights, falconry and a cheering crowd that eats with its hands, no utensils provided. The feast centers on tomato bisque guests call to die for, roasted chicken and garlic bread, while each section roars for its knight. Drinks and souvenir photos cost extra as sellers work the aisles, but guests say the show and the meal together earn the price.

Metro Diner (Kissimmee)

Breakfast & brunch · $

2714 Osceola Pkwy, Kissimmee, FL 34741

Metro Diner's Kissimmee room is a classic Florida diner, cozy and old school, doing comfort food without irony. The fried chicken and waffles headline a menu one guest calls home to some of the best fried chicken they have ever had, with cinnamon roll pancakes, Iron City meatloaf, shrimp and grits and pumpkin bread pudding rounding out the greatest hits. Portions run ample, the welcome is genuine, and fans say the wait at busy times is worth it.

Pienezza Pizza

Pizza · $$

Pienezza Pizza, 3236 N John Young Pkwy, Kissimmee, FL 34741-7549, United States

Pienezza Pizza is a family-run, no-frills Kissimmee shop on John Young Parkway where the Grandma pie is the one regulars say brings it home. Guests call the slices as close to an authentic New York slice as you will find around here, crisp with a great undercarriage, and the Hey Brooklyn, Crazy Margherita and Sicilian Flag give the menu its Brooklyn accent. Garlic knots and cannoli round it out, though some regulars find the pies a touch greasy and the tangy sauce divides the table.

Polynesian Fire Luau

Attractions · $$$

4740 W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee, FL 34746

Polynesian Fire Luau brings a tropical dinner show to 192 in Kissimmee, festive and high-energy, with drums, hula and fast Tahitian dancing building toward the fire knife performers. A tropical buffet and a flaming Hawaiian rum punch come first, and audience participation pulls guests into the dances along the way. Guests call it one of the best evenings of their whole Orlando vacation, welcoming and immersive from start to finish.

Puro Dulzor by Karolene

Breakfast & brunch · $

1354 E Vine St, Kissimmee, FL 34744

Puro Dulzor by Karolene is a cozy, family-run hole in the wall on East Vine Street where the coffee hits first: guests call it the best in Kissimmee. Then come the Puerto Rican plates, a towering tripleta sandwich, crisp empanadillas, crema de maiz like abuela made it and a mayorca stuffed with Nutella for the sweet finish. The owner greets customers herself, and regulars say the little room simply feels like home.

Reunion Resort & Golf Club

Hotels · $$

7593 Gathering Dr, Kissimmee, FL 34747

Reunion Resort spreads across Kissimmee at full resort scale: signature golf courses drawn by Palmer, Watson and Nicklaus, a water park with a lazy river, tennis and pickleball, and the rooftop restaurant Eleven for fireworks over the parks. Guests call it a perfect place to relax with a family atmosphere, a big clean property that has everything you would need. Villa condition can vary from unit to unit, so the golf and the grounds carry the stay.

Salt & the Cellar by Akira Back

Restaurants · $$$

Salt & the Cellar by Akira Back, 3001 Sherberth Rd, Kissimmee, FL 34747-1787

Inside the Ette Hotel in Kissimmee, Salt & the Cellar by Akira Back is a dim, polished room that feels like an occasion: elegant, warm and quietly luxurious. The menu crosses steakhouse, Mediterranean and Japanese-leaning flavors, with a tuna pizza to start and a Chilean sea bass guests praise for its crispy skin and buttery center. Service draws words like fabulous, and the afternoon tea gives the room a second life beyond date night.

Pizza · $$

The Jersey Spot Pizza and Pasta, 1137 W Columbia Ave, Kissimmee, FL 34741-3202, United States

The Jersey Spot is a small, family-run Kissimmee joint on Columbia Avenue serving thin-crust, New Jersey-style pies that transplants say taste like home. Tropico Mofongo

Latin & Caribbean · $$

Tropico Mofongo, 3160 Vineland Rd, Kissimmee, FL 34746-4657, United States

Tropico Mofongo is a bright, cheerful Kissimmee room on Vineland Road built around Puerto Rican comfort: plantain, chicharron and garlic pounded into mofongo that guests say tastes like their grandmother's cooking. World Food Trucks

Markets & shops · $$

5811 W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee, FL 34746

World Food Trucks turns a Kissimmee parking lot into a passport you can eat your way through: truck after truck serving arepas, tacos and elotes, fruit shakes, gator nuggets and smash burgers.
