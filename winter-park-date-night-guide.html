Winter Park, for Date Night

The Short Version

Park Avenue is the date; the rooms are the choices

At one end of the register sits Ômo by Jônt, sixteen seats and three rooms of Michelin-starred theater. At the other sits The Wine Room, where you load a tasting card and pour for yourselves, and Peterbrooke’s chocolates, handcrafted on Park Avenue in the European tradition since 1983, as the walk-off closer.

Between those poles the avenue covers the middle registers: The Chapman’s warm, artful room, Sixty Vines and its wall of wines on tap, Vinia’s welcome like a guest at home, and a gastropub reached through a blue door in the Hidden Garden, where the glass atrium does the rest.

The move: dinner, then one more stop on the same street. Park Avenue dates work because the second act, a pour at The Imperial, a glass from the tap wall, a box of chocolates, never requires a car.

Ômo by Jônt

Fine dining & steak · $$$$

Ômo by Jônt is Winter Park's Michelin-starred splurge, a tasting menu staged as a journey through a series of intimate rooms.

The Chapman

Fine dining & steak · $$$

On Park Avenue, The Chapman is the warm, artful dining room Winter Park kept asking for, built on a scratch kitchen and Florida ingredients.

The Wine Room on Park Avenue

Bars & lounges · $$

Wine tasting without the intimidation: load a Tasting Card and pour your own tastes, from a sip to a full glass, off a wall of wines from around the world.

The Imperial on Park

Bars & lounges · $$

Park Avenue's neighborhood bar, warm and laid back, pouring boutique wines, craft beers and cocktails built on Winter Park Distillery spirits.

Sixty Vines

American · $$

A wine-country room transplanted to Winter Park, with a tap wall of wines poured by the glass, carafe or bottle and a greenhouse patio that glows after dark.

The Grove Bar + Kitchen

American · $$

The Grove wears its citrus-grove heritage warmly, a golden, textured room in Winter Park Village built for lingering.

Jala Indian Cuisine

Indian · $$

Jala brings founder Manish Nagar's Ahmedabad roots to Morse Boulevard, a serene, intimate room where the cooking stays slow-simmered and tandoor-fired.

Peterbrooke Chocolatier

Sweets · $$

Peterbrooke has handcrafted chocolate in the European tradition on Park Avenue since 1983, and the cocoa scent reaches the sidewalk.

Vinia Wine & Kitchen

Italian · $$

Dinner at Vinia feels like being welcomed into someone's home: a small, low-lit room in Hannibal Square, a boutique wine list, and plates that move between Italy and Brazil.

Garp & Fuss

American · $$

You enter through a blue door in Winter Park's Hidden Garden, and the glass atrium does the rest; guests call it a hidden gem that feels like a secret even when it is packed.
