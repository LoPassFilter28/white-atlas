4 Hens Creole Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

1412 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803

Chef Brandi's all-day Creole stall in City Food Hall cooks with real confidence. The chicken and croffles, a croissant crossed with a waffle and dripping hot honey, are the signature move. Gumbo ramen and a loaded Bourbon Street po'boy deserve your attention too, with rich, soulful seasoning throughout. The record describes the stall as Black and women owned, set in a warm room where the music never misses. Pick this Ivanhoe Village stop when you want New Orleans comfort that tastes seasoned, generous, and assured.

Barkhaven

Parks & outdoors · $$

Barkhaven, 724 Brookhaven Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-2505, United States

The one rule at BarkHaven is that your dog has as much fun as you do. Orlando's dog bar pairs a supervised off-leash park and a splash zone with on-site grooming, plus a full restaurant and bar so humans can eat properly while the pups play. The kitchen goes beyond pub fare with burgers, guava wings and a brisket bratwurst that regulars praise. Fire pits, live DJs and a whole community of regulars on four legs complete it. Bring the dog; the humans are well covered here.

Egg Dose

1412 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803

Ivanhoe Park Brewing Company

Breweries · $$

1300 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803

Local history pours into the pint here, starting with the name itself. Ivanhoe Park honors Lake Ivanhoe founder George Russell and the old Joyland amusement park that once stood nearby. The beer follows with personality, from crisp Park Hopp'r pilsner to bold Tupac ShaPorter. A reclaimed wood taproom and dog friendly beer garden hum with neighborhood energy, and rotating taps keep locals returning to see what is new. Choose this independent brewery when you want an Ivanhoe Village hangout where story, setting, and beer all pull together.

Orlando Science Center

Museums & landmarks · $$

777 E Princeton St, Orlando, FL 32803

Every Orlando kid of the last thirty years has a Science Center memory, and many involve that dome. The Orlando Science Center started in 1960 as the Central Florida Museum and grew into four floors of hands-on exhibits in Loch Haven Park: dinosaur digs, a living cypress swamp and the observatory roof. The Dome by Dr. Phillips Charities is the place's soul, a 300-seat giant dome theater that surrounds you with Everest climbs and ocean depths. Go learn something and feel like a kid again, whatever your age.

The Pinery

American · $$$

The Pinery, 295 NE Ivanhoe Blvd, Orlando, FL 32804-6434

Right on Lake Ivanhoe, The Pinery is Ivanhoe Village's love letter to its own past, named for the pineapple farms that once grew where the condos stand. Owner Carol Holladay runs a warm, lake-view dining room built on Southern hospitality and chef-driven Florida cooking: smoked peanut soup, a bread board with pimento cheese, broiled blue crab dip, and shrimp and grits that taste like the season they were made in. The patio catches the water breeze, the cocktails lean tropical, and the whole room glows. Dinner with a view, done the Ivanhoe way.
