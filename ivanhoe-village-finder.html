Where to start in Ivanhoe Village

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

4 Hens Creole Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

1412 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803

Chill and soulful inside City Food Hall, 4 Hens Creole Kitchen cooks with a NOLA spirit, music in the air, and seasoning guests call out of this world. The chicken and croffles with hot honey lead, with gumbo ramen, a Bourbon Street po'boy, and shrimp and grits in smoked gouda grits close behind. Beignets and N'awlins bread pudding finish the stall's generous, assured take on New Orleans comfort.

Directions → · @4hensorlando

Barkhaven

Parks & outdoors · $$

Barkhaven, 724 Brookhaven Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-2505, United States

Playful and dog obsessed on Brookhaven Drive, Barkhaven pairs a supervised off leash park and splash zone with a full restaurant and bar, so pups play while their people actually relax. The kitchen goes beyond the expected with guava wings, the BH brisket bratwurst, and the Big Rich burger, plus pup cups and a dog menu of their own. Guests say their dogs leave happily worn out, and the cocktails, including the Raise the Woof old fashioned and BarkHaven IPA, give the humans a real reason to stay.

Directions → · @barkhavendogbar

Brews and Bites Walking Tour

Attractions · $$

Ten10 Brewing Company, 1010 Virginia Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

The Brews and Bites Walking Tour moves through Ivanhoe Village breweries and bars with guides guests describe as knowledgeable, upbeat and full of local history. Small groups sample craft beer alongside soft pretzel with beer cheese and locally made pizza, with behind the scenes stories at each stop. It feels like a guided introduction for people who want to map the Virginia Drive beer scene with company.

Directions → · @wanderingpalmfoodtours

City Food Hall

Markets & shops · $$

City Food Hall, 1412 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803-1862

Modern and bustling on Alden Road, City Food Hall gathers chef driven stalls under one roof so a group can split across smash burgers, egg sandwiches, Creole plates, sushi, pasta, and tacos without settling. Guests love the variety and the yard outside to relax, even as the room can run warm and prices lean high for a food hall. The central bar and the Topgolf Swing Suites upstairs turn a meal into a longer hang.

Breakfast & brunch · $$

Ivanhoe Park Brewing Company

Breweries · $$

1300 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803

Relaxed and cozy with indoor and outdoor room to spread out, Ivanhoe Park Brewing Company on Alden Road is the neighborhood taproom guests call super dog friendly. Pours run from the crisp Park Hopp'r pilsner and Joyland IPA to Tupac ShaPorter and the Guavacation sour, with the Firestarter jalapeno hot honey ale for heat. Irregular Smash burgers and rotating food trucks keep the table covered while the beer garden stays unhurried.

Directions → · @ivanhoeparkbrew

Attractions · $$$

Well organized and led by a knowledgeable local guide, the Ivanhoe Village Food Tour walks a lakefront vintage district of small shops and cafes through a run of tasting stops. Orlando Science Center

Museums & landmarks · $$

777 E Princeton St, Orlando, FL 32803

The Orlando Science Center is Loch Haven Park's hands-on museum, where the interactivity is the real kind, all digging, building and touching rather than pressing buttons. KidsTown turns young visitors loose in a miniature city, DinoDigs hides fossils to dig up, and the Crosby Observatory opens the roof to the stars. The Dome wraps its giant screen around the room for a show guests call stunning and immersive. Most exhibits skew toward younger kids and garage parking adds up, but there is easily enough to fill a full day.

Directions → · @orlandosciencecenter

Santiago's Bodega

Mediterranean · $$

Santiago's Bodega, 802 Virginia Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Santiago's Bodega on Virginia Drive in Ivanhoe Village is a lively, eclectic tapas room built for sharing plates on the patio. Guests linger over miso crusted short ribs, beef carpaccio, saganaki, patatas bravas and yellowfin tuna ceviche, with red sangria poured in huge glasses. Regulars call it one of the best spots in Orlando for food and service that feels impeccable.

The Pinery

American · $$$

The Pinery, 295 NE Ivanhoe Blvd, Orlando, FL 32804-6434

Warm and lakeside on Lake Ivanhoe, The Pinery pairs a rustic elegant room with Florida cooking that guests call fabulous. Start with the broiled blue crab dip and bread board with pimento cheese, then move to crab stuffed salmon, smoked duck, or shrimp and grits. American · $$

White Wolf Cafe, 1829 N Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32804

Quaint and vintage with a kitschy, eclectic jumble of light fixtures, White Wolf Cafe in Ivanhoe Village feels whimsical from the first plate. Yakuza Sushi

Japanese & sushi · $$

1412 Alden Rd, Orlando, FL 32803

Fresh and playful with bold sauces, Yakuza Sushi at City Food Hall blends Japanese and Mexican flavors into fusion rolls guests call some of the best sushi they have had in a while. The Dragon Roll and the Volcano Roll, with its baked topping, lead, alongside the Roku Roll, Ceviche Roll, and Fuji Special. It is a fun, unpretentious counter for a group with split cravings.

Ziggie's Pizza

Pizza · $$

Ziggie's Pizza, 603 Virginia Dr, Orlando, FL 32803-1844, United States

Ziggie's Pizza is a playful little counter on Virginia Drive in Ivanhoe Village, attached to the Thirsty Topher craft beer bar and turning out very thin, personal-sized pies. The Killa Beez layers fig jam, prosciutto, pepperoni, goat cheese and house hot honey with real heat, while the Stardust piles on pistachio pesto and truffle oil, and guests call every pie a flavor adventure. The crust lands crisp with just the right cheese to sauce balance, light enough that you still feel good on your feet afterward.
