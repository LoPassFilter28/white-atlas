407 FYI Heritage

Orlando's longest-running places

This is the guide's roll of the places that have outlasted fads, storms, and the corridor's endless turnover: family dining rooms, stubborn little shops, stages, and markets that locals treat as permanent. Every entry links to its full page in the guide.

Heritage

The Deep Roots

Before Orlando had theme parks, it had tables, stages, and boat docks. These are the places that were already institutions when the interstates were still on paper.

Columbia Restaurant

The family's Ybor City original dates to 1905, and the Celebration dining room carries five generations of that kitchen with it.

Since 1905 · Latin & Caribbean · Celebration

 The Beacham & The Social

A vaudeville theater on Orange Avenue that has been giving Orlando a place to lose itself in music since 1921.

Since 1921 · Nightlife & music · Downtown

 Winter Park Scenic Boat Tour

Gliding through the Winter Park chain of lakes and its narrow canals since 1938.

Since 1938 · Attractions · Winter Park

 Gatorland

Orange Blossom Trail's alligator landmark, doing its one unmistakable thing since 1949.

Since 1949 · Attractions · South Orlando

The Postwar Table

The Orlando that locals grew up with was built in rooms like these: family-run, unhurried, and stubborn about staying exactly themselves.

Beefy King

Freeman and Margaret Smith's Bumby Avenue shop has steamed its roast beef the same way since 1968.

Since 1968 · Burgers & sandwiches · Milk District

 Charlie's Bakery

Feeding Orlando cakes, eclairs, and the Parker House rolls regulars swear by since 1971.

Since 1971 · Bakeries · Orlando

 Lam's Garden Chinese Restaurant

Cantonese cooking in the Milk District since 1975, with weekend dim sum rolling out of the kitchen.

Since 1975 · Chinese · Milk District

The Eighties Hold

Briarpatch

Park Avenue's breakfast institution since 1980, holding the corner through every reinvention of the street.

Since 1980 · Breakfast & brunch · Winter Park

 Enzo's On the Lake

Central Florida's special-occasion Italian on Lake Kathryn since 1980.

Since 1980 · Italian · Longwood

 Peterbrooke Chocolatier

Handcrafting chocolate in the European tradition on Park Avenue since 1983.

Since 1983 · Sweets · Winter Park

 Christini's Ristorante Italiano

Restaurant Row's white-tablecloth occasion since 1984.

Since 1984 · Italian · Dr Phillips

 Orlando Farmers Market

Downtown's Sunday ritual on the Lake Eola lakefront, running since 1987.

Since 1987 · Markets & shops · Lake Eola

 Camila's Brazilian Restaurant

Feeding Brazilians in Florida at serious scale since 1989.

Since 1989 · Latin & Caribbean · I-Drive
