The District

Future, With An Appetite

Most planned communities feel like a spreadsheet in a polo. Lake Nona feels like the future snuck in early. Appetite first: a medical city around lakes, then chefs, artists, wakeboarders, a container food hall, sculpture garden, a lake built for playing.

01

01 · Attraction

Play Hooky On The Lake

Nona Adventure Park turns a lake into a full-contact playground: an inflatable aqua park to bounce, climb and wipe out across, a cable park for wakeboarders, a sixty-foot tower with rock walls. Few places wear you out this happily.

Nona Adventure Park · Attraction · $$

Dinner As Edible Theater

Say Bacán and Lake Nona sits up straighter: an immersive room around an open theater kitchen, the Americas' bold flavors turned into edible theater. Order the whole Florida snapper, save room for banana tart, watch the chocolate sphere melt open.
