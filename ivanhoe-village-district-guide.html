Flashback · Orlando History

Why is Lake Ivanhoe named after a knight?

After Sir Walter Scott's 1819 novel Ivanhoe. Early landowner F.N. Abbott named the lake for the Saxon knight, and around the same time, neighbor John W. Childress named nearby Lake Adair after his wife.

So one lake is literature and the next one is a love letter.

Local note: two lakes, one medieval novel, one marriage. Orlando's original romantics.
