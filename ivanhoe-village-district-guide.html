Ivanhoe Park Brewing pours heart into every pint, its name honoring Lake Ivanhoe founder George Russell and old Joyland amusement park. Park Hopp'r pilsner to Tupac ShaPorter, reclaimed wood, dog-friendly beer garden, rotating taps, locals keep returning.

Lake First, Tapas Last

Brunch at White Wolf, a slow walk to glassy water, a clear kayak on Lake Ivanhoe, studio doors along Orange Avenue. Evening: tapas at Santiago's as sky goes lavender, then a brewery pint, the neighborhood, start to finish.

Flashback · Orlando History

Why is Lake Ivanhoe named after a knight?

After Sir Walter Scott's 1819 novel Ivanhoe. Early landowner F.N. Abbott named the lake for the Saxon knight, and around the same time, neighbor John W. Childress named nearby Lake Adair after his wife.

So one lake is literature and the next one is a love letter.

Local note: two lakes, one medieval novel, one marriage. Orlando's original romantics.
