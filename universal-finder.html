Where to start in Universal

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

Beth's Burger Bar

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

Beth's Burger Bar, 9938 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819-8702, United States

Beth Steele built the burger joint of her dreams, and the room keeps that big kid energy. At Beth's Burger Bar you build your burger your way, from patty to last pickle, inside a late night diner setting. Signature stacks go wild with a peanut butter burger, a mac and cheese burger, and a pizza burger for the brave. Board games line the shelves, bingo nights fill the calendar, and fridges stock beer for the table. Choose Beth's when playful, loud customization matters more to you than a quiet, classic burger script.

Bubba Gump Shrimp Co.

Seafood · $

6000 Universal Blvd Ste 735, Orlando, FL 32819

Shrimp arrives every way Bubba named in the movie, with trivia under your plate. Bubba Gump serves fried shrimp, coconut shrimp, peel-and-eat shrimp, and shrimp mac and cheese at Universal CityWalk. Southern comfort covers other appetites: baby back ribs, fried chicken, and homemade desserts. Staff keep the show going throughout the meal. Take the photo in the Forrest Gump running shoes out front and find the bus bench. It is touristy, knows it, and serves shrimp that is genuinely good. Give in to the theme completely.

Cold Stone Creamery

Sweets · $

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL

Dessert becomes theater on a frozen granite slab at CityWalk. Cold Stone crew members fold mix-ins into fresh-churned ice cream in front of you, sometimes singing while they work. Your Creation can press birthday cake pieces, brownie chunks, candy, and fruit into every fold, exactly to order. The shop is small and seating sits outside, so grab a cup and stroll CityWalk with it. Brownie a la Cold Stone, a hot brownie under cold scoops, suits a long park night. Come for customization performed at the counter.

El Burro Loco

Mexican · $

6524 Carrier Dr, Orlando, FL 32819

A pandemic era food truck grew into one of I-Drive's boldest Mexican kitchens without losing its energy. El Burro Loco serves food made fresh daily, built for the hungry and curious. The Classic Burro Loco wraps rice, fries and burro sauce into a fourteen inch tortilla that takes commitment, while birria tacos drip with au jus and cheese shell tacos go full indulgence. The kitchen runs late for midnight cravings. Come ready for volume, in both the room and the portions.

Fat One's Hot Dogs & Italian Ice

Burgers & sandwiches · $

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Yes, that Joey Fatone, and the hot dogs go well beyond ballpark fare. The *NSYNC member and Orlando native opened a permanent CityWalk restaurant with longtime manager Joe Mulvihill, and the menu loads nine creations. The Fat One buries a footlong under roasted pork, pepperoni, chili beans, cheddar cheese sauce, and pop fries. The Brazilian pairs grilled linguica with Calabrian honey and jalapeno veganaise, while the Dirty K-Pop Dog adds kimchi slaw and gochujang mayo. Cool down with old school Italian ice and a specialty beer for pure Orlando pop culture joy.

Directions → · @fatonedogs

Five Guys (CityWalk)

Burgers & sandwiches · $

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

A theme park burger stop gets serious when the counter is built for custom stacks. Five Guys landed on CityWalk's upper level with a breakfast sandwich built for the morning trek into the parks, before the menu settles into familiar classics. You stack burgers your way, hand cut fries arrive in an overflowing cup, and milkshakes stay thick enough to matter. After a full day on your feet, the red and white counter feels like a promise kept. Choose it when familiarity, volume, and a morning option matter more to you than novelty.

Half Barrel Beer Project

Breweries · $$

Half Barrel Beer Project, 9650 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819-8766, United States

Small room, serious beer obsession, and no reason to judge by size. Half Barrel squeezes a twelve tap room onto Universal Boulevard, with two house brews pouring beside a curated selection of Florida craft. Coolers stack deep with bottles and cans to take home, and staff talk beer the way regulars do. The room stays industrial and unpretentious, and fall off the bone wings have a following of their own. Choose Half Barrel when you want a craft beer stop that rewards browsing, tasting, and taking a few bottles with you.

Hard Rock Cafe Orlando

American · $$

6050 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

A guitar hangs over your table, a stage glows next door at Hard Rock Live, and your server points out a jacket worn on an actual tour. Hard Rock Cafe Orlando at Universal CityWalk is described as the world's largest Hard Rock Cafe, and lunch doubles as a memorabilia walk for music fans. The set meal, with gratuity included, keeps the planning simple. Plan it for a CityWalk day, ideally before or after a show next door, and go for the collection as much as the food.

Directions → · @hardrockorlando

Hard Rock Live

Nightlife & music · $$

6050 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Three thousand people, and somehow every seat feels close to the stage. Hard Rock Live delivers concerts, stand-up and big touring names with arena-grade sound and lighting in a room with the intimacy of a club. It sits beside the Hard Rock Cafe at CityWalk, so the memorabilia is everywhere: Elvis's suit, smashed guitars, a museum of loud. Check the calendar before your CityWalk night, because the lineup decides the evening. For a touring show without arena distance, this room is hard to beat.

Margaritaville (CityWalk)

Latin & Caribbean · $$

6000 Universal Studios Plaza Ste 704, Orlando, FL 32819

CityWalk does escapism in a margarita glass, with a volcano doing the centerpiece pour. Margaritaville, Jimmy Buffett's tropical escape at Universal CityWalk, pairs the open air Porch of Indecision with a giant ship's mast rising from the dining room and live bands at night. The Cheeseburger in Paradise and coconut shrimp keep the beach theme on the plate. Come for lunch, stay toward sunset and leave wearing a lei. You go for the scene first and the familiar songs of the room second.

O-Town West

Markets & shops · $$

8652 Fenton St, Orlando, FL 32836

Around Epic Universe, a new food and stay corridor is still taking shape. O-Town West is the emerging mixed use district where the area's next wave of restaurants is landing, with 2025 and 2026 openings like Kappo Tsan setting the tone. The district positions itself for Epic Universe visitors who want something beyond the parks, though it remains early. The trajectory is clear even while the grid fills in. Watch it if you like seeing a dining district before it settles.

Sal's Pizza Bar

Pizza · $$

Sal's Pizza Bar, 11020 Daryl Carter Pkwy, Orlando, FL 32836, United States

Real New York pizza in Florida is a debate that never ends, and Sal's has strong opinions on the matter. Sal came to Orlando via Italy and the Bronx, and his family-owned bar turns out proper Brooklyn-style pies: the vodka burrata, with creamy fresh burrata melting over hot crust, a beloved grandpa slice, and garlic knots with fresh garlic, parsley and pecorino. The crust is chewy with bite, the sauce is on point, and Sal himself is often behind the counter. That personal touch makes it a neighborhood institution.

Sushi Yama

Japanese & sushi · $$

Sushi Yama, 6748 Grand National Dr, Orlando, FL 32819-8526

All-you-can-eat sushi on International Drive anchors Sushi Yama, with a lineup deep enough to reward ordering in waves. Dragon rolls, rainbow rolls, and crazy yellowtail cover the rolls, while thick-cut sashimi and yellowtail jalapeno keep the raw side interesting. Hibachi steak and fried rice widen the table, and fried tempura cheesecake waits at the end for anyone who paced the early rounds well. Lines form for a reason, and the room suits groups who eat at different speeds. Bring an empty stomach and pace yourself like the pros at the next table.

The Cowfish Sushi Burger Bar

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Can't decide between a burger and sushi? The Cowfish at Universal CityWalk refuses to make you choose. This is the first and only Sushi Burger Bar, and its signature Burgushi fuses the two worlds: sushi rolls built from burger components, pickup sandwiches stuffed with sushi ingredients. The Rise and Swine burger and the fresh tuna rolls are the favorites, and the bento boxes let the indecisive sample both. The bar leans playful, with spiked hand-spun milkshakes and exotic martinis, and kids get bento boxes with candy sushi. Quirky, loud, and a whole lot of fun.

The Toothsome Chocolate Emporium & Savory Feast Kitchen

American · $$

The Toothsome Chocolate Emporium & Savory Feast Kitchen, 6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819-7611

You walk in and the factory swallows you whole. Brass, gears, towering chocolate-colored smokestacks, and one wild idea behind everything: chocolate belongs in every course. Start with the warm Chocolate Almond Bread, rich with salted caramel, then the Marshmallow Crisp milkshake, unapologetic and impossible to finish with dignity. Watch for Professor Doctor Penelope Tibeaux-Tinker Toothsome and Jacques, her robot sidekick, wandering the room like they own the place. Come hungry and share the milkshake.

Topgolf Orlando

Attractions · $$

9295 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Golf skill is optional here, and that is exactly the point. Across three floors and 102 climate controlled bays, micro chipped balls trace your shots toward glowing targets while the kitchen sends out burgers and a full bar keeps the table supplied. A rooftop terrace with fire pits draws the golden hour crowd, and games range from precision challenges to Angry Birds target play. Choose it for birthdays, conventions, and friendly rivalries when cosmic bowling energy sounds better than a quiet round.

Universal 2-Park 1-Day VIP Experience

Attractions · $$$$

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

You walk past a posted wait, follow your guide through a staff door, and sit down before most guests find the queue entrance. The Universal 2-Park 1-Day VIP Experience is a guided group day across both original parks, with rides prioritized for you. The splurge buys back hours, not just comfort, which matters most when your schedule is tight. If you have only one day for both parks and time matters more than budget, check this option first.

Universal Epic Universe

Parks & outdoors · $$$$

1001 Epic Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

The first major theme park to open in Orlando in 25 years shows its ambition in every square foot. Five worlds wait behind the portals at Epic Universe: Celestial Park's gardens, the Ministry of Magic's wizarding streets, Super Nintendo World's playable chaos, the Isle of Berk with its dragons, and Dark Universe, where the classic monsters live. The immersion works at a scale the industry rarely attempts, and a full day barely scratches the surface. Plan like it is a destination, because for Universal fans it now is one.

Directions → · @universalorlando

Universal Helios Grand Hotel

Hotels · $$$$

8505 S Kirkman Rd, Orlando, FL 32819

Dinner inside a theme park rarely feels this grown-up. The Universal Helios Grand Hotel rises right out of Epic Universe's Celestial Park, and its dining program leans full Mediterranean romance. Flora Taverna serves comfort cooking in a light, airy room with an open kitchen and al fresco tables overlooking the park's fountains, while Bar Helios, the rooftop lounge, pours craft cocktails and tapas against a sweeping panorama of the entire universe below. Even if you're not staying over, this is a destination meal in its own right.

Universal Orlando Resort Tickets

Attractions · $$$$

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

A wand chooses someone up front, and butterbeer foam lands on a kid who swore he was too old for this. Universal Orlando Resort tickets cover the original parks plus Epic Universe, the new park that opened in 2025. For a family, the real decision is park to park access or one park per day, and it shapes both price and pace. Decide before you buy, not at the gate, especially if Epic Universe is the reason you came.

Universal Stella Nova Resort

Hotels · $$

4500 Epic Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Stella Nova opened in January 2025 as one of the two new value-tier resorts serving Epic Universe, and it is the budget play for the new park. The space-themed design runs through the lobby and the 750 rooms, the pool deck is built for recovery days, and shuttle service runs to all the Universal parks. Rates stay well under the in-park Helios Grand, which makes it the practical base camp for a multi-day Epic Universe trip.

Universal Studios Florida

Parks & outdoors · $$$$

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

The original Universal park opened in 1990, and it remains the one where movie magic feels personal. Slip through the brick wall into Diagon Alley with butterbeer in hand, and the wizarding world opens around you. Revenge of the Mummy throws you backward into ancient curses, E.T. still pedals through the night sky, and Springfield's Krustyland keeps the whole family laughing. Eight lands, deep nostalgia and a lagoon-side movie-parade finale fill the day. For movie fans, this park still sets the terms in Orlando.

Directions → · @universalorlando

Universal Terra Luna Resort

Hotels · $$

5500 Epic Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Terra Luna is Stella Nova's sister resort, opened March 2025 with the same value-tier brief: clean, modern rooms themed around exploration, a big resort pool, and quick shuttles to Epic Universe and the rest of Universal Orlando. The two resorts share the same DNA, so pick whichever has the better rate on your dates. Both beat the I-Drive chain hotels on location if Epic Universe is the reason for the trip.

Universal's Islands of Adventure

Parks & outdoors · $$$$

Universal's Islands of Adventure, 6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

If the Studios is about the movies, Islands of Adventure is about the adventure itself. Eight themed islands ring a central lagoon, each a fully realized world: Hogsmeade's snow-dusted rooftops beneath Hogwarts, where Hagrid's motorbike ride sets the bar for coasters; Marvel Super Hero Island's Hulk launch; and the VelociCoaster's raptor hunt through Jurassic jungle. Then you drift into Seuss Landing's whimsy with the kids. The park rewards wanderers, because every island carries its own weather and its own story from path to path.

Voodoo Doughnut

Bakeries · $

Voodoo Doughnut, 6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Portland weirdness arrived at CityWalk in pink boxes and never toned itself down. Voodoo Doughnut sells more than fifty varieties, led by the Voodoo Doll, pierced with a pretzel stake and filled with raspberry jelly, and the Bacon Maple Bar that sounds wrong and tastes right to fans. You can watch decorators work at the counter, take the throne photo, and choose from a vegan lineup that gets its own attention. Expect a line, and treat it as part of the spectacle rather than a warning.
