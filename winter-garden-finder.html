Winter Garden

Plant Street's brick blocks are the draw in this small historic downtown west of Orlando. Burgers and sandwiches lead the list, with breakfast and sushi filling in, all within a short walk of each other. Come on a Saturday and the downtown feels like the whole town turned out.

Where to start in Winter Garden

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

Bruno's Oysters

Seafood · $$

Bruno's Oysters, 426 W Plant St, Winter Garden, FL 34787, United States

Inside Plant Street Market's Barrel Room in Winter Garden, Bruno's Oysters feels moody, rustic and intimate around a small counter. Guests linger over oysters on the half shell, Oysters Rockefeller and roasted bone marrow, with the lobster roll and crab cake sandwich earning repeat orders. Regulars call it a little diamond, even if the counter pace can stretch when the market fills.

Eggdose

Breakfast & brunch · $

12615 W Colonial Dr, Winter Garden, FL

Farmers market roots turned into a proper Winter Garden room without losing the simple idea. Eggdose keeps one philosophy: breakfast should be simple, fresh, and satisfying. Egg sandwiches are the star, made how you want them on fresh bread with quality eggs. The Maple Dose uses a French toast bun with thick cut bacon, soft scrambled eggs, cheddar, powdered sugar, and maple syrup on the side. The picanha steak sandwich brings savory muscle to the menu. Choose it when you want breakfast done plainly and well, and leave plotting your next visit.

Directions → · @eggdosefl

Harlow Grove

American · $$$

186 S Main St, Winter Garden, FL 34787

Beneath a century old oak on South Main Street in Winter Garden, Harlow Grove pairs an elegant, soothing room with an open air terrace overlooking town. Downstairs stays lively around cocktails like the Eden Spritz and HG Espresso Martini, while upstairs feels quieter for plates like goat cheese croquettes, short rib dip, heirloom burrata, signature ribs, and lobster lasagna. Guests return for the decor, the attentive welcome, and a setting they describe as a whole vibe.

Harrell's Hot Dogs and Cold Cones

Burgers & sandwiches · $

54 W Plant St, Winter Garden, FL 34787

Tucked into a narrow space on Plant Street in Winter Garden, Harrell's is a cute little hideaway for grilled all beef dogs that guests say elevate a simple hot dog and fries. Buns arrive soft, slightly sweet, and toasted with butter, loaded as a Chicago Dog, a Mac Monster, or a chili cheese dog, with hand cut fries and truffle fries on the side. Cold cones and dirty sodas finish things while the Plant Street crowd strolls past the outdoor seats.

Indian Pavilion

Indian · $$

Indian Pavilion, 13770 W Colonial Dr, Winter Garden, FL 34787-4202, United States

Indian Pavilion on West Colonial Drive in Winter Garden serves modern Indian cooking built on bold spice, careful texture, and generous portions guests say often leave leftovers. Garlic naan arrives with real quality, alongside Paneer Makhani with plenty of paneer, Old Delhi butter chicken, chicken tikka masala, and chicken biryani. Regulars point to consistency as its hallmark, the flavor and quality they want from a good Indian restaurant.

Ispirazione

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

Ispirazione, 1711 Amazing Way, Ocoee, FL 34761-3279, United States

On Amazing Way in Ocoee, Ispirazione centers on tigelle, small round Italian breads baked fresh to order with a crunch outside and a soft center. Fillings lean imported and creamy, from the Tigella Deliziosa and Bella Vita with burrata, prosciutto, and mortadella to a Modena basket, with pistachio tiramisu and cannoli to finish. Guests call it a cozy, hidden away find, the kind of place you return to once you know it is there.

Norigami

Japanese & sushi · $$

Norigami, 426 W Plant St, Winter Garden, FL 34787-3014

Inside Plant Street Market in Winter Garden, Norigami is an intimate sushi counter where each piece is prepared right before your eyes. The omakase leans on pristine fish and careful rice, with popcorn hamachi and toro carpaccio with truffle among the standouts, and sushi served on colorful washi paper that guests fold into cranes. Guests call it the best sushi they have had since a trip to Japan, a warm and precise experience built on trusting the chefs.

Play Street Museum

Museums & landmarks · $$

12107 Stoneybrook West Pkwy, STE 104, Winter Garden, FL 34787

Play Street Museum in Winter Garden is a tiny town built for young children, where play runs through a doctor's office, a fire station, pet rescue, and make believe ice cream cones. Sessions add hands on extras like a slime lab and paint your own pottery, keeping the space fresh and age right. Local first time owners run it with obvious care, a fit for birthday parties and rainy day outings in west Orange County.

Schweini's Delicatessen

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

Schweini's Delicatessen, 108 S Main St, Winter Garden, FL 34787-3556, United States

Main Street in Winter Garden gets a German deli built straight from the owner's heart. Chris Frommeyer pounds schnitzel thin, breads it, and stacks it on pretzel rolls with lingonberry compote, pickled red cabbage, and horseradish that wakes you up kindly. The Die Behrens, a pork schnitzel sandwich with real swagger, is the order. German beers pour cold, soccer plays on screens, and your dog is welcome on the patio. Choose Schweini's when you want to arrive hungry and leave grinning after a sandwich with crunch, tang, and lift.

SEO Basilico Pizzeria

Pizza · $

12107 Stoneybrook W Pkwy Suite 105, Winter Garden, FL 34787

SEO Basilico in Winter Garden's Stoneybrook area serves artigianale pizza rooted in Sao Paulo Italian tradition, with founders from Mooca shaping the menu. Pies run from Italian classics like margherita with fresh basil and fior di latte to Brazilian staples like Frango e Catupiry and Calabresa, with blistered, leopard spotted crusts from patiently fermented dough.

Southern Steer Butcher (Orlando)

Markets & shops · $

11024 W Colonial Dr, Ocoee, FL 34761

On West Colonial Drive in Ocoee, Southern Steer is a family run butcher where the staff are as much guides as sellers, walking you through the case and how to cook what you take home. Guests come for fresh meat sold by the pound, pre marinated cuts ready for the grill, dry aged steak, house burger patties, and chicken crack sausages with flavor guests call unforgettable. One regular put it as what they had been looking for since moving to Florida, a neighborhood shop built on helpful, knowledgeable service.

Volcano Hot Pot & BBQ

Chinese · $$

Volcano Hot Pot & BBQ, 3279 Daniels Rd Suite 102, Winter Garden, FL 34787, United States

Volcano Hot Pot and BBQ on Daniels Road in Winter Garden puts a bubbling hot pot and a Korean BBQ grill at the same table, so you cook each bite your own way. The all you can eat spread runs large, with seafood including crab, sliced prime brisket, meats, vegetables, and udon noodles to slide into the broth. Guests call it their favorite spot for Korean BBQ, a lively, hands on room with good vibes built for groups sharing plates.
