Inside Still Lounge by Dre and Snoop at Pointe Orlando. Photo: venue.

The Write-Up

The full story

A low-light lounge inside Live! at Pointe Orlando, built around Dr. Dre and Snoop Dogg's Still G.I.N. Leather couches, golden light, and throwback hip hop and R&B give it what guests call a grown and sexy elegance unusual for International Drive. The bar leans into the duo's gin with drinks like the Platinum Negroni, the bourbon based OG-OF, and a lavender honey Bee's Knees.
