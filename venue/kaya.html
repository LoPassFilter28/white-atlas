Kaya

Fine dining & steak · Filipino · $$$$ · Mills 50

The Write-Up

The full story

Kaya brings Filipino cooking to a cozy, funky house on Thornton Avenue, where guests say it feels like grandma's house if grandma cooked at a very high level. The menu gives classics a modern twist: oxtail kare kare, lumpiang shanghai, kinilaw and adobo risotto, with halo-halo and kalamansi pie for dessert. The team explains every dish with care, and the whole meal reads as a love letter to Filipino culture.
