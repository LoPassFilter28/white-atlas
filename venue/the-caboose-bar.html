The Write-Up

The full story

The Caboose Bar on N Orange Ave in Ivanhoe Village is a dive where guests say the bartenders make you feel like a regular. The kitchen turns out Caboose Wings guests have called the best wings in Orlando, plus fully loaded tots, mozzarella sticks and boiled peanuts. The vibe stays chill and welcoming, with regulars who treat newcomers like family.
