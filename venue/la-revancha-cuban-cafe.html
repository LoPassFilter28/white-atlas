La Revancha Cuban Cafe

Latin & Caribbean · Cuban · $$ · Orlando

The Write-Up

The full story

La Revancha Cuban Cafe presses Cubans on East Colonial Drive with a standard it has defended since 1998, salsa on the speakers and an unpretentious, relaxed room. The Classic Cuban sandwich, ropa vieja with peppers, empanadas, and tostones anchor the savory side, while a cortadito, cafe con leche, and the Cubano iced coffee keep the coffee strong. Guests call it a favorite go to, with guava pastries and quesitos for a sweet finish and portions that satisfy.
