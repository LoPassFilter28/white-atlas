The Write-Up

The full story

Zaru is a small, minimal udon room on East Colonial built around a single obsession: springy Sanuki-style noodles. Guests rave about the mentaiko udon and the shrimp tempura udon, calling it the most slurpable udon they have had, with ikura onsen tamago and hojicha panna cotta rounding out the meal. It is cozy and focused, the kind of gem people make their go-to whenever they are in Orlando.
