Moa Kai Hawaiian Diner

Restaurants · Hawaiian Diner · $$ · Orlando

The Write-Up

The full story

Moa Kai brings retro diner comfort to Hawaiian cooking on East Colonial Drive, all tropical color and aloha warmth. Plate lunches anchor the menu, loco moco, shoyu chicken, kalua pork and garlic shrimp, and the deep-fried Spam musubi is the plate guests push on first timers. Malasadas and ube haupia pie close things out with a glass of POG, and guests say every bite feels like the islands and a big warm hug.
