Eddie V's Prime Seafood

Seafood · Seafood & Steak · $$$$ · Sand Lake

The Write-Up

The full story

Eddie V's pairs prime seafood and steak with the V Lounge, where live trios keep the night moving after the plates clear. Guests single out the tuna tartare, Chilean sea bass, shellfish tower and bananas foster butter cake, all carried by polished, celebration-minded service. On busy nights the music can swallow the table talk, so ask for a quieter corner if the conversation matters.
