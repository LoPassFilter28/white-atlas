Burger Vault

Burgers & sandwiches · Burgers · $$ · Altamonte Springs

The Write-Up

The full story

A burger should never feel like an afterthought, and this halal spot on 436 builds like it agrees. Burger Vault starts with handcrafted halal beef cooked how you want it, then builds with intent. The Volcano Burger brings heat from chili volcano sauce and jalapenos, while the Mozza Melt stacks fried mozzarella with mushrooms and house sauce. Shakes, including a pistachio one, cool the table down after the heat. Served fresh and built with care, it suits you when craft, heat, and halal beef need to share one tray.
