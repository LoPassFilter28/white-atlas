Christini's Ristorante Italiano

Italian · $$ · Dr Phillips

The Write-Up

The full story

On Restaurant Row since 1984, Christini's stages dinner as an old-world occasion: tuxedoed service, candlelight, and live accordion drifting through the room. Guests call it one of the most romantic dinners in town and insist on the veal parm on the bone, with the veal chop and Chilean sea bass over shrimp risotto close behind. It is priced for milestones rather than weeknights, and regulars say it earns it.

Our honest take

The highest rated room in this wave, and priced like it: reviewers call it expensive in the same breath as superb. Standing on Restaurant Row since 1984, this is the corridor's special-occasion standard. Budget for the occasion rather than the weeknight and it delivers the old-world ceremony it promises.
