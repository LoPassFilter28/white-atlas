Pio Pio Latin Cuisine on South Semoran Boulevard. Photo: Pio Pio Latin Cuisine.

The Write-Up

The full story

On South Semoran, Pio Pio is the Colombian staple locals lean on, a warm, family-style room where the plates land generous and homestyle. The rotisserie chicken is the order regulars swear by, alongside bandeja paisa piled high, sizzling lomo saltado, and fried yucca made for dragging through the sauces. Guests talk about the service as much as the food, friendly and attentive in a way that makes the whole table feel looked after.
