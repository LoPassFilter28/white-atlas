Oza Izakaya

The dining room at Oza Izakaya, past the zen garden on Central Florida Parkway. Photo: Oza Izakaya.

The Write-Up

The full story

Oza Izakaya is a sleek, garden-like Japanese room on Central Florida Parkway, beautiful and unhurried in a way that suits a date night. Small plates are built for sharing: yuzu hamachi, buttery uni toast, wagyu gyoza with truffle, grilled octopus with yuzu foam and miso yaki cod. Regulars praise the spot-on consistency, and more than one guest ranks the food among the best Japanese they have had in any major city.
