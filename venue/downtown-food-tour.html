Downtown Food Tour

Attractions · Food Tour · $$$ · Downtown

A food tour group seated around a restaurant table in downtown Orlando. Photo: Downtown Food Tour.

The Write-Up

The full story

This walking food tour shows you the real Orlando, the downtown locals actually eat in, with a guide who folds the city's history and art into the route. Stops hit vendors you would not normally try, for tastings like Sticky Pork Bites, a Quesito, Colombian street food and a Deviled Egg Smash Sandwich, plus a secret dish. Groups stay small and personal, and guests warn that you will get full.
