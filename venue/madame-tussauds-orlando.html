The Write-Up

The full story

The wax museum inside ICON Park, where guests pose with pop stars, superheroes, sports legends, and a Justice League lineup made for group photos. The figures reward a close look, staff pass along fun facts as you wander, and the whole thing stays playful and hands-on. It is a brisk, air-conditioned walkthrough, so pair it with the wheel or the aquarium next door.

Our honest take

Wax figures live or die on the photo, and this lineup is built for them: pop stars, superheroes, a Justice League set for group shots. It is a brisk, air-conditioned hour inside ICON Park, best with kids or superfans in tow. Go in playful and it pays off; go in ironic and it cannot help you.
