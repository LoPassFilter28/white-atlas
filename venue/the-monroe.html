The Write-Up

The full story

Childhood favorites grow up in an airy midcentury room at The Monroe. The Good Salt group's fourth concept sits on the ground floor of the Julian apartments in Creative Village, built as a diverse social gathering place. You get famous fried chicken, pork belly s'mores, and jalapeno popper deviled eggs, plus a coffee bar by day that turns cocktail bar by night. Next door, the Front Porch overlooks Luminary Green Park. Choose it for a working lunch, a long happy hour, or a dinner that lingers without any effort from you.
