The Write-Up

The full story

The Guesthouse is Mills 50's plant-filled living room, greenery trailing over white walls around a square bar. Sunlight floods the big windows early; later the room goes candlelit and cozy, full of lively conversation. The bartenders build the classics with conviction, a Tom Collins one guest called the best she had ever had, plus margaritas, a passionfruit margarita, and a proper army navy. On busy nights, Fridays especially, it packs wall to wall, so weeknights are the version the regulars fell for.
