The Write-Up

The full story

Espresso by day gives way to disco, salsa, and a crowded dance floor. The Robinson Cocktail Room on Pine Street pours real espresso and iced lattes as a cafe, then turns into a joyful nighttime room where dancing carries the schedule. Drinks range from a proper espresso martini to the Dancing Queen, while chili relleno and tamales give you reason to stay past the first round. You do not need to know how to dance before you arrive. The room will make you want to learn.

What People Are Saying

The word from guests

Real words from people who have been, gathered from public reviews. The Robinson Cocktail Room holds 4.7 on Google across 221 reviews.

Our honest take

The Robinson's two halves do not always match. The cafe side can be spotty, and upstairs the bar and door staff draw the complaints: slow drinks, a bartender reviewers call rude, a heavy-handed bouncer. When the music is on and the floor is full, people forgive most of it. Know which Robinson you are walking into.
