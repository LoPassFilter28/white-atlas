Everoak Farm

Markets & shops · Farmstand · $ · College Park

The Write-Up

The full story

Three acres, ten minutes from downtown, are growing food the slow and deliberate way. Husband and wife team Mike and Nikki Garcia run Everoak as a regenerative, biointensive family farm with no sprays, bees working the rows and pasture raised chickens roaming. Certified Naturally Grown produce comes from living soil, and their Saturday market gathers fellow small businesses beside the farm stand. Their CSA feeds seventy local families, and harvests reach restaurant menus across town. Start here to taste where Orlando farm to table begins.
