Power House Cafe

The storefront and patio of Power House Cafe in Winter Park. Photo: Power House Cafe.

The Write-Up

The full story

Power House Cafe is a small, bright Mediterranean spot just off Park Avenue in Winter Park where everything tastes fresh. Guests come back for the chicken shawarma wrap and falafel on pita, the Powerhouse Salad, and smoothies like the Good Karma and the mango Margo. Service moves fast and the room stays casual and friendly, so it works as easily for a quick solo stop as for a relaxed bite with friends.
