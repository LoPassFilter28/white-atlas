The Write-Up

The full story

Sorekara hides on New Broad Street in Baldwin Park, where Chef William Shen builds a tasting menu around Japan's micro seasons and moves guests through multiple rooms as the courses unfold. Diners describe it as not just a meal but an experience, precise and beautifully executed, with uni courses, a carrot soda welcome drink, and house made nonalcoholic pours along the way. The storytelling is intimate and transporting, the kind of night guests call a core memory.
