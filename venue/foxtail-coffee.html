The Write-Up

The full story

Foxtail Coffee is the Orlando-born chain that still pours like a neighborhood shop, with a peaceful, low-key room at 2401 Curry Ford Rd in the Hourglass District. Cold brew runs serious, with the Vanilla Caramel Cold Brew and Foxtail Original pouring alongside cafe con leche and matcha tea lattes, plus a Chicken Bacon Ranch Crunch Wrap if you are hungry. Guests call it a perfect spot to sit and catch up, with a dog-friendly patio, pup cups, and laptops welcome at the tables.
