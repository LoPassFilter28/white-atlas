Khaos Sports Bar and Grill

The Write-Up

The full story

A lively dive bar sports hub on west 434 that expanded in 2026 with a new kitchen, more pool tables, and a smoke free interior. Wings are the draw, with darts, karaoke, and domestic beer buckets on game day.
