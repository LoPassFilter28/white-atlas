Stefano's Trattoria

Italian · $$ · Winter Springs

The Write-Up

The full story

A cozy, old-school Italian dining room tucked into a Tuskawilla Road shopping center, easy to miss and worth finding. Live piano drifts through the room while gnocchi della nonna in pink sauce, lobster ravioli, and veal piccata land in generous portions, with tiramisu cheesecake to finish. Longtime fans still prize it, though recent regulars say the cooking has turned uneven under new ownership.
