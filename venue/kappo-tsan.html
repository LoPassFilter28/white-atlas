Kappo Sushi

Japanese & sushi · Sushi · $$$ · Disney Area

The Write-Up

The full story

Kappo means "cut and cook," and this intimate O-Town West dining room lives up to it: chefs preparing seasonal, ingredient-driven dishes right before your eyes, knife work and fire and plating in real time. The 15-course omakase at the sushi bar runs from delicate raw preparations to warm composed dishes to precision nigiri. The six-seat counter is the full experience, but even à la carte at the bar carries the same calm focus. Now led by Michelin-starred chef Stone Lin as Kappo Sushi, it is one of Orlando's serene special-occasion rooms.
