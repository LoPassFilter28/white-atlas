Peterbrooke Chocolatier

Sweets · Chocolatier · $$ · Winter Park

The Write-Up

The full story

Peterbrooke has handcrafted chocolate in the European tradition on Park Avenue since 1983, and the cocoa scent reaches the sidewalk. The chocolate-covered popcorn is the signature, the house gelato gets called the best some guests have had in the US, and dipped strawberries, truffles, and malt shakes fill out the cases. Prices run Park Avenue, so buy the good stuff and buy less of it.

Handcrafting chocolate in the European tradition since 1983, and the discipline shows: this is Park Avenue's quality benchmark for sweets and gelato. Reviewers call it pricey even while praising it, which is the deal in one line. Buy the good stuff, buy less of it, and it is worth every cent.
