Windermere

Windermere is the lakeside village west of the parks: a brick Main Street under a heavy oak canopy, a Friday market in front of Town Hall, and a moneyed quiet that never needed to shout. The food follows the register: a Southern breakfast cafe, a hometown brewery, wood-fired pizza, a serious wine shop and Tuscan dinners out in Summerport. Paloma anchors the morning.

Bella Tuscany

Italian · $$

13424 Summerport Village Pkwy, Windermere, FL 34786

A family-owned Tuscan room in Summerport with a wine list past 700 bottles and Wine Spectator recognition to show for it. Beef carpaccio and a lobster and pumpkin risotto served in the pumpkin itself lead the menu, with chicken parm for the traditionalists. The patio keeps date night outside when the weather behaves.

Kelly's Homemade Ice Cream

Sweets · $

14387 Bridgewater Crossings Blvd, Windermere, FL 34786

The Windermere scoop shop of the Orlando-born ice cream maker that started in Audubon Park. Small-batch flavors rotate through salted caramel Oreo, PB&J potato chip and guava cheese, and flights exist for the undecided. A Summerport evening ritual in the making.

Paloma Parlor

Coffee & cafes · $

625 Main St, Windermere, FL 34786

The coffee shop Windermere regulars treat as their own now trades as Paloma Parlor at 625 Main St, a small, plant-filled spot with a contemporary, small-town charm. The lattes come strong, smooth, and never bitter, with lavender, rose, and the Lucky Latte keeping the menu playful beside house-baked croissants and macarons. Ice cream joined the lineup after the move, and guests call it worth the short trip out of Orlando.

Tim's Wine Market

Markets & shops · $$

428 Main St, Windermere, FL 34786

The Windermere storefront of Orlando's long-running independent wine shop, with shelves in Orange Avenue and Sanford siblings. Small-producer bottles are the point, and the staff teach as they pour. On Fridays the Wine Down pours free tastes for whoever wanders in.

Windermere Brewing Company

Breweries · $$

111 W 5th Ave, Windermere, FL 34786

Windermere's hometown brewery, a block off Main Street with a dog-friendly backyard and a food truck rotation. The Watermann helles and Orange Grove Outlaw IPA pour beside a peach wheat called Party Boat. Trivia and bingo nights give the week a rhythm.

Directions →

Windermere Farmers Market

Markets & shops · $

520 Main St, Windermere, FL 34786

Every Friday, Town Square Park in front of Windermere's Town Hall fills with a small hometown market: fresh produce and seafood, breads and pastries, homemade pasta, local honey, plants and hanging baskets. Live music drifts over the brick street and leashed dogs are part of the crowd; pairing it with coffee at Paloma down the block is the local routine.
