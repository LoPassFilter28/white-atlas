Bakery 1908 on South Apopka Vineland Road in Orlando is the second location of the Asian bakery whose original shop sits on East Colonial Drive. This location adds a noodle bar serving ramen and pho alongside the bakery offer.

Chef Art Smith's Homecomin'

American · $$$

1602 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Chef Art Smith's Homecomin' brings a homey Florida farmhouse feel to Disney Springs, generous and welcoming from the first plate. Art's Famous Fried Chicken leads, with Thigh High Chicken Biscuits, Church Lady Deviled Eggs, and Momma's Mac & Cheese piled in portions guests describe as huge. Save room for Hummingbird Cake or Shine Cake, and expect a wait that regulars say is worth it.

Drawn to Life: Cirque du Soleil

Attractions · $$$

1478 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Drawn to Life at Disney Springs blends Disney animation with Cirque du Soleil acrobatics in a breathtaking, nostalgic production. A larger-than-life animation desk anchors the staging, with aerial and acrobatic acts set to Disney music and familiar characters woven into the storyline. Guests call it the best of both Disney and Cirque du Soleil, and say there really isn't a bad seat in the house.

Gideon's Bakehouse (Disney Springs Flagship)

Bakeries · $$

1600 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Gideon's Bakehouse at Disney Springs is a dark, gothic and candlelit bakehouse where guests line up for cookies they describe as massive, rich and packed with flavor. The Original Chocolate Chip, Pistachio Toffee Chocolate Chip and Coffee Cake cookies anchor the case alongside cake slices and Peanut Butter Cold Brew. Guests say the cookies stay balanced rather than overly sweet, and many call the wait worth it.

House of Blues

Nightlife & music · $$$

1490 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

House of Blues at Disney Springs pairs a Southern leaning restaurant with a music hall wrapped in folk art. Guests praise generous portions, from barbecue to blackened chicken with flavor they remember, and service with a smile. Families note the kids meals feel fairly priced for the area while the room keeps a fun, memory making energy.

Jaleo

Mediterranean · $$$

Jaleo, 1482 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830-8518

Jaleo at Disney Springs is a vibrant, festive Spanish tapas room guests describe as a non stop party of flavors. Wood fired paella, croquetas de pollo, gambas al ajillo, jamon iberico and patatas bravas arrive as small plates meant for sharing, with sangria and flan to finish. Diners call the cooking the most authentic Spanish food they have had outside Spain, though the small portions mean the table orders with the price in mind.

Level 99

Attractions · $$

1486 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Level 99 at Disney Springs fills the former NBA Experience building with life sized challenge rooms for physical games, logic puzzles and head to head duels, all tracked by an RFID wristband. The hands on format feels high energy, competitive and immersive, with Detroit style pizza and drinks available when players pause. Busy periods can mean waits that cut into play time, so groups pace their rounds.

Morimoto Asia

Restaurants · $$$

Morimoto Asia, 1600 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Morimoto Asia at Disney Springs is a sleek, theatrical dining room guests call a diamond at Disney Springs. The pan Asian menu centers on Morimoto Peking duck, spare ribs, tableside buri-bop yellowtail and dim sum, with orange chicken and mango sticky rice rounding out the table. Diners praise flavorful, unique cooking and cheerful service, at premium prices that set high expectations.

Paddlefish

Seafood · $$$

1670 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Paddlefish serves seafood aboard a steamboat setting at Disney Springs, with waterfront views and a rooftop deck that keep the mood picturesque and relaxed. Regulars return for shrimp pasta, clam chowder, lobster corn dogs, and whole Maine lobster, plus focaccia that guests praise at the table. It gets loud when the boat is busy, and loyal guests still say it hits the spot on repeat visits.

Raglan Road

Bars & lounges · $$

1640 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Raglan Road at Disney Springs is a lively Irish pub where live music and Irish dancers keep the room energetic. Guests return for fish and chips, shepherd's pie, chicken pie and bread pudding, calling it always a fun time. The premium prices for pub food give some regulars pause, but the energy keeps the room full.

Six Ravens

Burgers & sandwiches · $$

1600 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Six Ravens at The Landing in Disney Springs is the savory companion to Gideon's Bakehouse, serving Coffyns, hand pies in house baked yeast rolls with storybook names and fillings. The dark, gothic and heavily themed counter also serves Tell Tale Tatties smashed potatoes and a cookie tart, in a tiny space with little seating. Early coverage calls it pricey for counter service.

STK Steakhouse

Fine dining & steak · $$$

1580 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

STK Steakhouse at Disney Springs pairs a chic steakhouse with a club-like lounge energy, and guests sum it up as vibe dining. Tomahawk steak, Wagyu Lil' BRGs, A5 wagyu, and sweet corn pudding anchor the table, with a rooftop terrace above the room. The sound runs loud and lively, part of the scene rather than a quiet backdrop.

The Boathouse & Vintage Amphicars

Seafood · $$$

1620 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The Boathouse at Disney Springs is a lively, classic waterfront room where guests sit by the water for Gibsons steaks, a raw bar with Lucky Duck oysters, filet mignon sliders, lobster bisque and a grouper sandwich. Diners call it the best restaurant on Disney property and praise the rolls and seasoned steaks, while noting the room can get loud and prices run high. Captain guided vintage Amphicar tours splash from the dock, tying the meal to the lake.

The Ganachery

Sweets · $$$

The Ganachery, 1486 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

The Ganachery is a jewel-box chocolate shop at Disney Springs, where an open show kitchen lets you watch ganache squares, sea salt caramels, and chocolate piñatas being made. Artisan s'mores are torched to order, and hot ganache and the Minnie Ganache Pop add to the indulgence. Guests call the bites little morsels of heaven, an homage to a chocolate lover's desires in their words.

The Polite Pig

BBQ · $$$

1536 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Chinese · $$

Wine Bar George

Bars & lounges · $$$

1610 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Wine Bar George feels like a calm escape from the Disney Springs crowds, cozy and welcoming with knowledgeable guidance at the table.
