Albin Polasek Museum & Sculpture Gardens

Museums & landmarks · $

633 Osceola Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

The story of this place is almost as moving as the sculpture inside it. Czech-born Albin Polasek spent 30 years leading sculpture at the Art Institute of Chicago, then retired to Winter Park at 70, where a stroke took half his body. It did not stop him: he devised a way to keep carving with an assistant holding the chisel. Half of his known works are here, set among gardens on the shore of Lake Osceola. The view justifies the trip and the art does the rest.

Charles Hosmer Morse Museum of American Art

Museums & landmarks · $

445 N Park Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

Modest from the street, breathtaking inside: the Morse holds a comprehensive collection of Louis Comfort Tiffany in Winter Park. Hugh and Jeannette McKean salvaged truckloads of art and architecture from Tiffany's fire-ravaged Long Island estate and brought it here, including the Byzantine-Romanesque chapel interior built for Chicago's 1893 World's Columbian Exposition. Lamps, leaded-glass windows and iridescent vases shift color as you move through the galleries. People make pilgrimages for this material, and standing in the rooms you understand why. It is the anchor of Winter Park's arts claim.

Winter Park History Museum

Museums & landmarks · $

200 W New England Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

The museum telling Winter Park's story lives in a former railroad freight depot, which fits, because the tracks built this town. The Winter Park History Museum packs 5,000 artifacts into a cozy, volunteer-loved space: vintage postcards, area railroads, military and school relics, and exhibits like the celebrated trains-of-Winter-Park show. It also anchors a free self-guided walking tour of twenty historic sites, so the visit extends into the streets outside. Small and passionate, it rewards anyone curious about how Winter Park became Winter Park.
