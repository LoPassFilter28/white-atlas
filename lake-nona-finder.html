Boxi Park Lake Nona Local Pick

Restaurants · $$

BaBbi BaBbi Korean Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

10799 Moss Park Rd, Suite 102, Orlando, FL 32832

K pop plays over a bright, casual room where the food arrives in sizzling stone and iron. Bibimbap bowls come ready to mix, the rice crisping at the edges under bulgogi, vegetables and egg, while kimbap rolls slice into neat seaweed wrapped rounds. Korean corn dogs stretch their cheese pull, tteokbokki chews pleasantly in its spicy sauce, and little banchan dishes crowd the table before you have settled in. Stone pot soups and stews keep the steam coming long after the opening round lands.

Bacán

Latin & Caribbean · $$$$

Bacán, 6100 Wave Hotel Dr, Orlando, FL 32827

Dinner at Bacan begins in an open theater kitchen, where the cooking is part of the night. Chef Guillaume Robin, honored as a Maitre Cuisinier de France, works with Sous Chef Ryne Wood in Lake Nona. Their menu draws on flavors from across the Americas, led by whole Florida snapper. Save room for the banana tart finished with gold leaf and the chocolate sphere that melts open at your table. Book it for an elevated night that still feels approachable and unexpected.

BENTO Asian Kitchen + Sushi

Restaurants · $$

12851 Narcoossee Rd, Suite 100, Orlando, FL 32832

In Lake Nona, BENTO runs the fast-casual formula the chain built its name on: fresh sushi rolls, wok-tossed bento boxes, and boba, served quick at a fair price. Regulars point to the Pao Pao chicken and the rotating shiso-accented specials as the orders that lift it above a routine stop. Outdoor seating gives the meal air, and easy takeout covers the nights when cooking loses the argument. The room works for families and solo lunches alike. You choose it as a neighborhood workhorse: dependable, varied, and ready when the day runs long.

Bosphorous Turkish Cuisine

Mediterranean · $$

6900 Tavistock Lakes Blvd, Suite 100, Orlando, FL 32827

Lavas bread lands on the table still puffed from the oven, ready to tear and drag through hummus, ezme and haydari. The dining room wraps you in Turkish carpets and warm wood while kebabs come off the grill smoky at the edges: lamb shank gone tender, chicken adana with a slow heat, mixed grills built for two people to share. Sigara böreği crackle, pistachio baklava flakes apart, and a small glass of Turkish tea stretches the meal out. A long, shared, unhurried dinner in the middle of Town Center.

Canvas Restaurant & Market

American · $$$

13615 Sachs Ave, Orlando, FL 32827

Laureate Park's lakeside table: Canvas opened in January 2016 in the Village Center, a Tavistock Restaurant Collection room seating 278, with water views from the dining room and patio. The New American menu moves from lobster eggs benedict and a toasted Cubano at lunch to whole roasted local snapper and cumin-rubbed steak frites at night. A Foxtail Coffee cafe inside keeps mornings covered. Come for a long lunch on the water, and stay for dinner as the light drops over the lake.

Cañonita

Mexican · $$$

6877 Tavistock Lakes Blvd, Orlando, FL 32827

A Venetian born brand brings Mexico City street food to Lake Nona in two forms. Canonita began at The Venetian in Las Vegas with canal front dining and a deep tequila list. In Lake Nona, the walk up Street Taqueria inside Boxi Park serves street tacos, nachos and chips with salsa under string lights, while the full service Canonita at Lake Nona West is run by the Tavistock Restaurant Collection. Grab tacos, a margarita from the Boxi Park bar and let the crew order differently.

Chroma Modern Bar + Kitchen

Restaurants · $$

6967 Lake Nona Blvd, Orlando, FL 32827

The room glows in saturated color, all jewel tones and low light, and the menu is built for passing plates around the table. Whipped ricotta crostini, crab cakes and charred octopus get things started, then the truffle fettuccine is tossed tableside in a Parmesan wheel until every strand shines. Brussels sprouts arrive crisped and caramelized, sliders stack up small and rich, and the bar answers with a Paloma or an espresso martini. A proper night out that stays easy, in the middle of Town Center.

Don Julio Mexican Kitchen & Tequila Bar

Mexican · $$

9776 Eagle Creek Center Blvd, Orlando, FL 32827

Birria is the headline at this Eagle Creek fixture: tacos with a rich dipping consommé, birria folded into ramen, birria showing up at brunch. The ceviche bar keeps things bright with lime and seafood, carne asada comes off the grill for fajitas and combination plates, and the tequila bar shakes passion fruit margaritas built for the whole table's attention. HAVEN Kitchen

Mediterranean · $$$

6100 Wave Hotel Dr, Orlando, FL 32827

Upstairs at the Wave Hotel, the room runs emerald and midnight blue under a trellis ceiling, with the pool glittering below the windows. Hot Taco Street Taqueria

Mexican · $

9680 Narcoossee Rd, Suite 103, Orlando, FL 32827

Neon pink, agave murals and patterned tile set the tone at this Lake Nona Village taqueria, where tortillas are made fresh daily. The crunchy birria and quesabirria arrive crisped and cheesy with brisket tucked inside, joined by al pastor, pollo pibil, carnitas and lengua or tripe for the adventurous. Lake Nona Deli and Market

Burgers & sandwiches · $

8510 Insular Ln, Orlando, FL 32827

Village Walk gets a neighborhood heartbeat from a counter that refuses to be only one thing. Lake Nona Deli and Market combines deli, pizzeria, and market, built on Boar's Head meats and cheeses, Olde Hearth bread, and local farm fresh goods. The Italian Classic sandwich stacks thick, pizzas draw a loyal crowd, and market shelves cover everyday needs. Gelato and boutique wine events add reasons to linger beyond the sandwich run. Choose it when you want a third place where lunch, groceries, and community warmth share one address.

Maki Hibachi

Japanese & sushi · $$

11954 Narcoossee Rd, Suite 1, Orlando, FL 32827

Small, sleek and serious about fish, this Lake Nona Landing dining room brings in seafood weekly from Japan and treats sushi like a craft. Tuna tataki, salmon carpaccio and hamachi kama open the meal, then hibachi plates arrive with fried rice and the white sauce regulars swear by. Specialty rolls run to lobster and scallop, and the chef's omakase is the move for a bigger night. Portions lean generous, the room stays lively, and dinner splits neatly between raw bar delicacy and hibachi comfort.

Nami

Japanese & sushi · $$$$

Nami, 6004 Artist Ave, Orlando, FL 32827

Lake Nona's Wave Hotel hides one of Orlando's transporting dining rooms. Nami, a Michelin-recommended contemporary Japanese restaurant, is built around drama done right: a backlit, undulating wooden ceiling, Japanese maple trees anchoring the room, and a sleek bar made for lingering. The menu goes bold and global, lobster donuts, BBQ eel donburi, A5 steak, refined small plates that keep surprising, and the separate Chef's Counter turns dinner into an extended chef-led tasting. Precision, artistry, and a cocktail program with tableside flair. Dress up and make it a night.

Nona Adventure Park

Attractions · $$

14086 Centerline Drive, Orlando, FL 32827

This is where Lake Nona goes to play hooky. Nona Adventure Park turns a lake into a full-contact playground: a sprawling inflatable aqua park you bounce, climb, and wipe out across, a cable park for wakeboarders of every level, and a sixty-foot climbing tower with ropes courses and rock walls for the vertically ambitious. Refuel at Splashes Bar & Grill, catch the sunset over the water, and come back for karaoke nights and live music. Few places in Orlando wear you out this happily.

9685 Lake Nona Village Pl, Orlando, FL 32827

A big center bar anchors the room, thirty six seats deep in bourbon, wine and game day chatter, while the kitchen cooks from scratch. Firecracker shrimp snap with heat, lobster mac and cheese runs rich and golden, and the tuna is cut thick for salads and searing. Steam rises off extra large bowls in this bright Eagle Creek storefront, the broth clear, fragrant and warm with star anise. Pisco Express

Latin & Caribbean · $

9717 Eagle Creek Center Blvd, Suite 100, Orlando, FL 32832

Peruvian cooking at counter speed: ceviche cut fresh and bright with lime and red onion, lomo saltado sizzling with the fries folded right in, chaufa rice carrying Peru's Chinese influence in every bite. The Living Room Bar

Bars & lounges · $$

6100 Wave Hotel Dr, Orlando, FL 32827

A pink dogwood tree blooms in the middle of the Wave Hotel lounge, and the whole room arranges itself around it: low chairs, pop art niches, a wide staircase made for lounging. Pizza · $

14009 Narcoossee Rd, Suite 130, Orlando, FL 32832

This is the neighborhood slice shop done right: New York style pies with a crisp fold, sold big and meant to be shared.
