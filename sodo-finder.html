SoDo

South of downtown, Orange Avenue does the work. Coffee and breakfast start the list, burgers and seafood follow, all along the South Orange Avenue corridor locals shorthand as SoDo, the city's Downtown South Main Street. It is a drive-to district built for locals' routines rather than visitors' itineraries.

Where to start in SoDo

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

Clean Eatz

Restaurants · $$

Clean Eatz, 2205 Orange Ave S, Orlando, FL 32806-3038

Healthy food rarely gets to keep this much flavor, but this SoDo cafe tries. Clean Eatz builds bowls to order and serves a Bang Bang Shrimp Wrap, Monterey Chicken, and buffalo chicken wraps in tomato-basil tortillas. Weekly meal plans and grab-and-go cases cover dinners beyond the visit. Every menu item lists its calories, so you can choose with the numbers in front of you. Come here when you want a health-food lunch that stays informed rather than guilty, and practical enough to repeat during a busy week.

MOOYAH Burgers, Fries & Shakes

Burgers & sandwiches · $

3155 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

The name promises three things, and SoDo's MOOYAH builds its meal around all of them. Certified Angus Beef burgers are built to order, hand cut fries arrive hot and salty, and buns bake fresh daily. Hand spun shakes use real ice cream, the Double Diablo brings heat, and the Bacon Hot Jam Burger is the house favorite. Sweet potato fries have a following of their own, and the room stays fresh, fast, and family friendly. Choose MOOYAH when the burger craving hits and a shake belongs in the same order.

Peach Valley Cafe

Breakfast & brunch · $$

Peach Valley Cafe, 2849 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806-5403

Hot apple fritters arrive first, and patience gets tested immediately. Peach Valley has served Central Florida as a neighborhood brunch answer since 2001, a local chain that still cooks like a name is on the sign. Chicken and waffles come with blueberry butter and Cajun spiked syrup, and shrimp and grits taste of the low country. A sunlit porch suits slow mornings and big crews around the table. Choose it when everyone at your table needs a plate that feels made for them, from fritters to grits and back again.

Rockpit Brewing

Breweries · $$

Rockpit Brewing, 10 W Illiana St, Orlando, FL 32806-4408, United States

A coal mining theme sounds like a costume until the taps and the room settle you in. Rockpit, Orlando's neighborhood brewery in SoDo, rotates small batch brews poured right behind the bar. SoDo Blondin goes down easy, red ales bring the malt, and sours keep adventurous drinkers happy. Live music fills the room most nights, smoked BBQ lands at your table, and warm wood keeps conversation easy. Choose Rockpit when you want a taproom that feels like a second living room, with small batch beer doing the hosting.

The Juicy Crab

Seafood · $

296 E Michigan St, Orlando, FL

A seafood boil arrives here the way New Orleans intended, with a family legend behind it. The Juicy Crab began with the Chen family, inspired by New Orleans and Grandfather Chen's travels. Crab arrives drenched, seasoned, and impossible to eat politely. Build a combo with shrimp, sausage, corn, potatoes, snow crab, or crawfish, or splurge on Dungeness and king crab. Fried shrimp basket and coco bread cover non-boil loyalists, with a full bar alongside. Grab a bib and commit fully, because polite half measures miss the point.

The Smoothie Room

Coffee & cafes · $$

The Smoothie Room, 25 W Crystal Lake St, Orlando, FL 32806-4403, United States

SoDo's answer to the sugar-bomb smoothie chain is this bright little room where nothing comes from a powder, a concentrate, or a pump. The Bahama Mama and Berry Dose are made fresh on the spot, thick with real fruit, and the PB Falcon acai bowl is the move when you want something with substance. Wheatgrass shots, ginger shots, cold-pressed juices, all of it in a space that feels calm on purpose. Locals swear this is the best smoothie in Orlando. One sip and the argument is over.

Zaza Cuban Comfort Food

Latin & Caribbean · $

1621 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

Hospital Row got a cafecito stop in mid 2026, and the morning crowd already explains it. Zaza's newest location extends the local chainlet formula with cafecito that wakes you up, medianoche sandwiches pressed to order and daily specials like ropa vieja over rice. The cooking tastes like a grandmother is still running the kitchen, even in a counter service diner format. Prices reflect the format, not white tablecloth ambitions. Hospital staff and regulars make it a SoDo staple in the making.
