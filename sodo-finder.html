SoDo

South of downtown, Orange Avenue does the work. Coffee and breakfast start the list, burgers and seafood follow, all along the South Orange Avenue corridor locals shorthand as SoDo, the city's Downtown South Main Street. It is a drive-to district built for locals' routines rather than visitors' itineraries.

Where to start in SoDo

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

4 Rivers Smokehouse

BBQ · $$

4 Rivers Smokehouse, 3200 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

The SoDo smokehouse on South Orange Avenue serves Signature Angus brisket that guests call A+, alongside burnt ends, the Messy Pig, and the Texas Destroyer. Portions run generous, with jalapeño cornbread, homestyle sides, and Crispy Cream Bread Pudding from the bakery case. The line moves fast past the trays, and the outdoor pavilion gives families room to spread out for a full feast.

Cecil's Texas Style Bar-B-Q

BBQ · $$

Cecil's Texas Style Bar-B-Q, 2800 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

Cecil's Texas Style Bar-B-Q on South Orange Avenue in SoDo has run cafeteria style since 1992, an old school roadhouse where trays move down a fast, loud and friendly line. Brisket is hand sliced to order alongside ribs, pulled pork and hot links smoked over hickory, with jalapeno mashed potatoes, collard greens and sweet potato souffle piled on as sides. Guests talk up huge portions and value for the price, and the free self serve soft serve closes the tray on a sweet note.

Clean Eatz

Restaurants · $$

Clean Eatz, 2205 Orange Ave S, Orlando, FL 32806-3038

Clean Eatz on South Orange Avenue in SoDo is a neat, welcoming health focused cafe where bowls and wraps keep their macros front and center. Guests single out the Southwest Bison Wrap for big flavor and the Bang Bang Shrimp Wrap for repeat orders, alongside the Clean Eatz Burger and Build A Bowl. Portions run lean by design, which regulars read as part of the trade for food that feels light and intentional.

Formaggio and Friends

Italian · $$

Formaggio and Friends, 4745 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

Formaggio and Friends on South Orange Avenue in SoDo is a cozy Italian grocery and wine bar where cheese cases, pantry shelves and a communal table share one small room. Guests urge each other to run for the mortadella sandwich and call the crab cakes the best in town, alongside the TomaRashi board and hand pulled bocconcini and burrata. Hosts pour tastings with knowledgeable guidance, keeping the feel closer to a neighborhood hangout than a shop.

Burgers & sandwiches · $

3155 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

Palm Beach Meats

Markets & shops · $$$

Palm Beach Meats, 3421 Orange Ave S, Orlando, FL 32806-6127

Palm Beach Meats is a butcher shop and counter on South Orange Avenue built on one obsession: Wagyu, done every way. The menu runs decadent and creative, highbrow meets lowbrow, with a Wagyu burger, an Australian Wagyu filet katsu sando, a Wagyu Philly and Wagyu tapa over garlic rice, plus a Frito Pie for good measure. The market cases stock Wagyu cuts to take home, and the beef tallow Nutella brownie earns the melt-in-your-mouth praise guests keep repeating.

Peach Valley Cafe

Breakfast & brunch · $$

Peach Valley Cafe, 2849 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806-5403

On South Orange Avenue in SoDo, Peach Valley Cafe pairs a cute, family friendly room with porch seating and plates that guests describe as generous. The Orchard Fresh Apple Fritters arrive warm and shareable, while biscuits with sausage gravy bring big chunks of sausage under giant fluffy biscuits. Shrimp and grits, chicken and waffles and the Philly Steak and Egg Croissant Benedict round out a menu rooted here since 2001.

Rockpit Brewing

Breweries · $$

Rockpit Brewing, 10 W Illiana St, Orlando, FL 32806-4408, United States

Rockpit Brewing on West Illiana Street in SoDo is treated by regulars as Orlando's neighborhood brewery, a laid back and cozy taproom where newcomers get welcomed like long lost friends. The board moves from SoDo Blondin to Mustache Ride red ale and Blackstrap con Leche milk stout, with Paul's Fault cinnamon vanilla moonshine poured for flights. Cecil's barbecue arrives at the tables, dogs settle in, and live music keeps the room feeling like a second living room.

Directions → · @rockpitbrewing

The Juicy Crab

Seafood · $

296 E Michigan St, Orlando, FL

The Juicy Crab on East Michigan Street in SoDo is built for messy, casual seafood boils shared around the table. The Build Your Own Seafood Combo piles up snow crab legs, shrimp and fixings in Old Bay sauce, with a fried shrimp combo and Dungeness crab for bigger appetites. Guests praise big portions and seasoning that lands bold, though regulars note the boil can swing between visits.

The Porch South Orange

American · $$

The Porch South Orange, 4757 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

The Porch South Orange on South Orange Avenue brings front porch energy to a welcoming neighborhood room that guests choose over touristy spots. The Southern Mama Jama burger stacks pimento cheese, house bacon jam and crispy onions, while blackened grouper tacos, shrimp and grits and Porch Tenders keep the menu Southern and hearty. A wedge salad with regular fans and a patio built for game day crowds round out the draw.

The Smoothie Room

Coffee & cafes · $$

The Smoothie Room, 25 W Crystal Lake St, Orlando, FL 32806-4403, United States

The Smoothie Room on West Crystal Lake Street in SoDo is bright, clean and calm, built around all natural blends with nothing hidden in the cup. Guests point to the PB Falcon acai bowl for its thin coconut shell over granola and toppings, and keep the Iron Hammer, Bahama Mama and Berry Dose in rotation. Prices sit on the premium side for smoothies, a trade regulars accept for knowing exactly what goes into each blend.

Vanbarry's Public House

Bars & lounges · $$

Vanbarry's Public House, 4120 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32806

Vanbarry's Public House on South Orange Avenue in SoDo starts with a jar of house pickles on the table and a covered patio facing Lake Jennie Jewel.
