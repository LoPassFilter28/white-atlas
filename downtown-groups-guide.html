The Short Version

Start at the food hall, end on Orange Avenue

The downtown group night has a natural shape. Eola Food Hall, in the bones of the old University Club, feeds the indecisive first. Broken Strings pours on West Church Street, two breweries under one roof with live music in the room’s DNA. Then the night funnels toward Orange Avenue and Central Boulevard.

That spine holds the range: The Social’s small brick room, where indie music in Orlando gets baptized; The Vanguard, carrying itself like a major concert room inside the historic Firestone Tire Building; a rooftop dance floor at Aero with the skyline as its backdrop; and Tier, downtown’s big-room statement, when the group wants scale.

Daylight counts too: Lake Eola’s loop, swan boats and the fountain under the skyline, is the district’s shared front yard and the easiest group plan downtown before the night rooms take over.
