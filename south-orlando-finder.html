South Orlando

South Orlando is the working half of the map: the Orange Blossom Trail's dining rooms, the Florida Mall's orbit, Conway's neighborhood joints and the airport corridor's kitchens. It is where the city's Latin, Uzbek, Indian and seafood cooking runs deep without a district brand to sell it. This page simply gives the cluster the name residents already use.

Where to start in South Orlando

New to the district? Start with these three, then work down the full list below.

Amar Orlando

Mediterranean · $$

8001 S Orange Blossom Trl, Ste #1510, Orlando, FL 32809

Amar Orlando serves Lebanese cooking in a calm, intimate room just outside the Florida Mall food court, a step up in polish from its surroundings. Guests rave about the hummus, calling it the best they have had, and the chicken shawarma and mixed grill platter carry that same care. Portions run modest, so order a spread and let the Taste of Lebanon platter do the touring.

Amazonica

Coffee & cafes · $$

8001 S Orange Blossom Trl Kiosk TT124, Orlando, FL 32809

A bright Colombian kiosk inside The Florida Mall turning out obleas layered with arequipe, acai bowls with Nutella and fresh fruit, and a proper cortadito. Guests praise the friendly counter staff and call the arepas exquisite, with cholados and Dubai chocolate for the sweet finish. It is a quick, tropical pause between stores that beats the standard food court routine.

Bar One Orlando

Bars & lounges · $

Bar One Orlando, 1516 McCoy Rd, Orlando, FL 32809

Bar One looks tiny from the street, but inside it is a cozy, very clean neighborhood bar on McCoy Road with a pool table and dart boards. The patio is the charmer, with picnic tables, cornhole, giant Jenga and a warm fire pit under the string lights. Order the wings (guests swear you will order another round), the Venezuelan empanadas and tequenos, and expect bartenders who remember you and your drink.

Beef Bae

BBQ · $$

10249 S John Young Pkwy Ste 109, Orlando, FL 32837

Beef Bae, the South Orlando spot formerly known as Charcoal Zyka, runs American barbecue through a Zabiha halal kitchen, and regulars say the pairing works. Guests praise the beef short ribs for their tenderness, the ribs fall off the bone, and the Portuguese chicken has been the hallmark since the start. An Inferno Burger brings the heat, and a mango lassi cools it back down.

Directions →

Bombay Street Kitchen

Indian · $$

6215 S Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando

Bombay Street Kitchen trades the standard curry-house script for the street carts of Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai and Bangalore, a bright, buzzy room on Orange Blossom Trail with a Michelin Bib Gourmand to its name. Guests work through pani puri, crispy kurkuri bhindi bhel and a gunpowder ghee dosa with perfectly spiced potatoes, then finish with Bombay falooda or rasmalai. The cooking is bold and fresh enough that guests say it takes them back to India, and that one visit turns into a craving for the next.

Directions → · @bombaystreetkitchenorlando

Caravan Uzbek & Turkish Cuisine

Mediterranean · $$

8015 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32809

Caravan is a small, cozy room on South Orange Ave where Uzbek and Turkish cooking share one table, fully halal and family run. Guests come for the mixed grill and lamb skewers, flaky samsa, fragrant To'y Osh pilaf, and fried laghman, with honey cake to finish. The welcome is the other half of the draw: people keep describing a place that made them feel like family.

Directions → · @caravanhalal

Crayola Experience

Attractions · $$

8001 S Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando, FL 32809

A hands-on color playground inside The Florida Mall, built for younger kids. Children name and wrap their own custom crayons, melt wax into shapes, try drip art, and watch their coloring come to life on screen. Guests call it creativity packed into every corner: colorful, playful, and easy to wander at your own pace indoors.

Directions → · @visitcrayolaorl

El Cilantrillo

Latin & Caribbean · $$

El Cilantrillo Restaurant, 1301 Florida Mall Ave, Orlando, FL 32809-7731

El Cilantrillo is South Orlando's big Puerto Rican table, a lively, salsa-filled room near the Florida Mall where the portions come generous and the cooking tastes made with love. Guests come for mofongo relleno, a massive chuleta kan kan, arroz con gandules and tostones rellenos, with coconut mojitos that regulars rave about. Diners born on the island call it true Puerto Rican sazón, the kind of food that takes you straight back. When the room fills, the kitchen takes its time, so order a mojito and settle in.

Directions →

El Paraíso Del Marisco

Seafood · $$

8421 S Orange Blossom Trl Ste 156, Orlando, FL 32809

El Paraíso Del Marisco cooks the Ecuadorian and Peruvian seafood that Orlando's Latin families describe as tasting like home. Encebollado arrives the way Manabí cooks it, the ceviche mixto snaps bright and fresh, and the chaulafán de mariscos is built to share, with bolón de verde for the plantain devotees. Plates are made to order, so the kitchen takes its time, in a warm, unfussy, family-run room.

Directions →

Happy Snapper Seafood Restaurant

Seafood · $$

Happy Snapper Seafood Restaurant, 6327 Orange Ave S, Orlando, FL 32809-5109, United States

A tiny, family-run seafood joint on South Orange Avenue that guests keep calling a hole-in-the-wall gem. The grouper sandwich is the order regulars defend, the deep-fried whole snapper arrives crackling, and the blue crab bisque earns its own following, all cooked, as one guest put it, perfectly, not over salted or over spiced. Seating is limited because the room is small, and that is part of the deal.

Directions → · @happysnapperseafood

Marlow's Tavern

American · $$

6889 Eagle Watch Dr, Orlando, FL 32822

A neighborhood tavern in Lee Vista that treats first timers like regulars in the making, starting with pub chips loaded with blue cheese and bacon on the house. The kitchen runs scratch American: hand-cut tavern fries, crab dip, asparagus fries, shrimp and grits, and a Black and Blue burger. Guests call it a hidden gem, warm and lively without trying too hard.

Directions →

Orlando Watersports Complex

Attractions · $$

8615 Florida Rock Rd, Orlando, FL 32824

A laid-back cable park where beginners and pros share the water, with an inflatable Aqua Park for bouncing between runs. Coaches teach first timers on the beginner cable while experienced riders lap the main cables, and Nautique boat pulls round out the day. Guests call the staff encouraging and genuinely invested in your success, the kind of place where kids forget their phones for hours.

Directions → · @orlandowatersports

Sazoncito Latin Food

Latin & Caribbean · $

4542 S Semoran Blvd, Orlando, FL 32822

A humble Conway counter serving Dominican comfort food that guests describe as tasting like home. Mofongo with chicharron, bistec encebollado, rabo guisado and sancocho dominicano come out fresh and generous, at prices guests call great value. The room runs lively, with sports-bar energy, and the music keeps it loud.

Uncle Tony's Backyard BBQ Orlando

BBQ · $$

Uncle Tony's Backyard BBQ Orlando, 6807 S Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32809-6025, United States

A hole-in-the-wall on South Orange Avenue with a small-town feel that makes you forget you are in a big city. Brisket and dino beef ribs lead, with Brisket Birria Tacos and the Mac Daddy Burger showing the kitchen's playful side, and a sweet vinegar barbecue sauce guests call worth the trip alone. The sides are no afterthought here; the mac and cheese is the one guests say stays on their mind.
