Universal

Beyond the gates: CityWalk's late nights, a brand-new universe of a theme park, and the chocolate factory that ate a restaurant.

Scroll the story

Everyone has a first Universal memory, and it usually involves screaming. This is the Orlando of big drops and bigger stories, where movie sets swallow you whole and the night does not end when the park gates close. Locals know it is really two trips in one: the parks by day, CityWalk by dark, and a chocolate factory standing guard over both. Start here, and pace yourself.

The Icons

The new guard and the old favorites.

Park·$$$$

Universal Epic Universe

The first major theme park to open in Orlando in 25 years, and it shows in every square foot. Five worlds wait behind the portals: Celestial Park's celestial gardens, the Ministry of Magic's wizarding streets, Super Nintendo World's playable chaos, the Isle of Berk soaring with dragons, and Dark Universe where the classic monsters live. This is immersion at a scale the industry has never attempted, and a full day barely scratches it. Plan like it's a destination, because now it is one.

Chocolatier·$$

The Toothsome Chocolate Emporium & Savory Feast Kitchen

You walk in and the factory swallows you whole. Brass, gears, towering chocolate-colored smokestacks, and one wild idea behind everything: chocolate belongs in every course. Start with the warm Chocolate Almond Bread, rich with salted caramel, then the Marshmallow Crisp milkshake, unapologetic and impossible to finish with dignity. Watch for Professor Doctor Penelope Tibeaux-Tinker Toothsome and Jacques, her robot sidekick, wandering the room like they own the place.

Speakeasy·$$$

600 Block

Most people walk straight past it inside Pat O'Brien's, and that is the point. 600 Block is CityWalk's password-door speakeasy, 21 and up, tucked behind the dueling-piano noise into something smokier and slower, where a jazz musician sets the tempo and the cocktails get the same care as the secrecy. Sit outside if you want the piano drifting up from downstairs and CityWalk people-watching at its finest, sit inside if you want the full hushed, dimly lit spell. It keeps vanishing and returning, so when the door is open, you go.

After the Gates Close

CityWalk after dark

The parks close, CityWalk wakes up: clubs, dueling pianos, and doughnuts at midnight.

Dueling Piano Bar·$$

Pat O'Brien's

Two pianos, two players, and a room that runs on requests scribbled on napkins. Pat O'Brien's brought its New Orleans piano bar across the state line and dropped it in the middle of CityWalk, Hurricanes and all. You will sing along to songs you forgot you knew, the players will roast the bachelorette table, and nobody leaves early. It is loud, loose, and exactly what a park day needs at the end of it.

Doughnut Shop·$

Voodoo Doughnut

The pink box is a CityWalk rite of passage. Voodoo Doughnut rolled in from Portland with a case full of glorious weirdness: the Bacon Maple Bar, the Grape Ape, a doughnut shaped like a voodoo doll with a pretzel stake through its heart. The shop glows pink at the edge of the lagoon, the line moves faster than it looks, and a warm dozen at midnight fixes almost any mood the day left behind.

Wizarding World·Universal Studios

Diagon Alley

Push through the brick wall behind the London waterfront and the city disappears. Diagon Alley is all crooked rooftops and packed shop windows, with a dragon on top of Gringotts Bank that breathes real fire over the crowd. Ride the vault carts deep under the bank, trade paper money for wizard gold at the money exchange, then just wander. Even people who never read the books end up standing there grinning like kids.

Roller Coaster·Islands of Adventure

Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure

Ask a local which ride is worth the longest line and this is the answer, every time. Hagrid's puts you on a motorbike, or in the sidecar if you lose the argument, and launches you through the Forbidden Forest past a three headed dog, a swarm of Cornish pixies, and a drop track hiding in the dark. It is half coaster, half story, and it ruins lesser rides for you. Go early. Go twice.

The Itinerary

Make a day of it

Morning

Rope drop at Islands of Adventure. Hagrid's first, while the line is still just a rumor.

Afternoon

Cross over to Epic Universe and take the portals slow. Celestial Park at golden hour is the whole point.

Evening

Back to CityWalk for the night shift: dinner inside the chocolate factory, dueling pianos after, and a pink box for the ride home.
