Orlando, Week by Week

The concerts, games, festivals and holiday nights filling Orlando's calendar, plus the markets and food truck nights that anchor every week.

This edition covers the rest of October and all of November 2026, checked against venue, league, team and festival sources. The calendar is rebuilt at the start of every month. Start times and dates can change, so confirm with the venue before you go.

The Standing Rhythm

Every week

The recurring gatherings the rest of the calendar bends around, from Monday evening in Audubon Park to the Sunday market at Lake Eola.

Audubon Park Community Market

Mondays · 5:00 PM

Stardust Video and Coffee · Audubon Park

Weekly Monday evening market in the Stardust Video and Coffee lot with local growers, prepared food and artisan vendors, rain or shine.

Tasty Takeover

Tuesdays · 6:30 PM

Tasty Takeover lot, 2424 E Robinson Street · Milk District

Weekly food truck gathering in the Milk District, the original Tasty Takeover Orlando event, with a rotating lineup of local trucks.

Winter Park Farmers Market

Saturdays · 8:00 AM

Central Park West Meadow · Winter Park

Weekly Saturday morning farmers market in the Central Park West Meadow off Morse Boulevard, run by the City of Winter Park.

Winter Garden Farmers Market

Saturdays · 8:00 AM

Downtown Pavilion, Winter Garden · Winter Garden

Weekly Saturday morning farmers market under the Downtown Pavilion on Lakeview Avenue in downtown Winter Garden.

Orlando Farmers Market

Sundays · 10:00 AM

Lake Eola Park · Downtown Orlando

Weekly Sunday market at Lake Eola Park run by Orlando Farmers Market, with produce, food, plants and local vendors along Central Boulevard and Eola Drive.

Maitland Farmers Market

Sundays · 9:00 AM

Lake Lily Park · Maitland

Weekly Sunday farmers and artisan market at Lake Lily Park run by the City of Maitland, with live music, food trucks and local products.
